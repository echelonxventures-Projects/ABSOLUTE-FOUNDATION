### 4.1 The permanence test

Every step in §5–§17 was tested against five questions. A step failing any one was reduced to HOLD rather than admitted in weakened form.

1. Does it survive its own success — is it still correct once the gap is closed?
2. Does it introduce a second answer to a question something already answers?
3. Does it require an authority that does not exist?
4. Is its completion falsifiable by an executable check?
5. Does it widen any attack surface?

**Steps reduced to HOLD by this test: performance governance (Q3), protocol representation (Q3, Q5), UI model (Q3), threat model (Q3), value model (Q3), reasoner plugin (Q3).**
