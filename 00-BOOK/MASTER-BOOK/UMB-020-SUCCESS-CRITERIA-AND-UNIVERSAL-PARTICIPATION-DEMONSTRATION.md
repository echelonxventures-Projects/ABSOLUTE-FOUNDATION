## PART II — WORKED DEMONSTRATION: A BRAND-NEW ENTITY

Consider an entity of a kind that does not yet exist today — call its class `X` (a not-yet-imagined artifact or runtime object). Trace its automatic participation end to end:

1. **Declare once (if a new family).** Append a `config.py` classification rule, chain, root, cross-program edge, and (if thematically new) a volume — append-only, exactly as the `UMB` family was declared. For an entity in an already-declared family, even this step is unnecessary (REG-AUTO-001 L4/§12).
2. **Create.** The `X` file/record appears under an in-scope path (or a connector emits its first Signal).
3. **`T` fires (unskippable).** The `PostFileCreate` hook / commit guard / CI guard run the Atomic Registration Transaction:
   - **Identity** — ledger allocates a fresh `UCOS-X-NNNNNN`, never reused, with a `first_seen` serial and a fixed page range (UMB-003).
   - **Classification & Nomenclature** — the rule assigns program/category/volume/status-domain; the native ID is preserved verbatim as a crosswalk alias (UMB-004).
   - **Registration** — all seven registers reflect `X` atomically; count parity holds (UMB-005).
   - **Traceability & Graph** — Parent/Child + Depends-On (and any typed) edges materialize; `X` is a graph node reachable from the BOOK root with a return path (UMB-006/007).
   - **Versioning & Lineage** — `version`/`content_hash` recorded; lineage chain rooted at `first_seen` (UMB-009/010).
   - **Twin & State** — Signals about `X` roll up to its five independent domain states; the twin reflects it (UMB-002).
   - **Search & Navigation** — search indices and a portal page are regenerated; `X` is findable and navigable (UMB-013).
   - **Publication** — any view of `X` is generable on demand in any format (UMB-011).
   - **Audit** — every step is an append-only, attributed entry in the audit ledger (UMB-016).
   - **Certification** — `twin --check` re-certifies the twin including `X` (UMB-017).
4. **Change Intelligence.** Any later modification to `X` is a governed `CHG` change producing knowledge, impact, regeneration requirements, and institutional memory automatically (UMB-008; UCI-001).

At no step is there manual registration, manual synchronization, hard coding, or an artificial limit. `X` participated fully **by construction**.
