*Append-only extension overlay. Specifies the complete future-state Universal AI Knowledge Layer as inference over the authoritative evidence base. It creates no AI registry, model store, or identifier namespace (UCI-001 Part XVIII.5), embeds no secret (RR-07), and is AI-provider-agnostic (AUTH-INF-001 CR-INF-003).*

---

## 1. PURPOSE

Provide predictive and assistive intelligence — impact/risk/dependency prediction, recommendations, and a knowledge assistant — **derived on demand** from the authoritative evidence base, never persisted as authoritative fact (UCI-001 IP-3).

## 2. EVIDENCE-ONLY INFERENCE

The AI layer reads **only** the four authoritative stores and their derived views: Artifact Registry, ID Ledger, Knowledge Graph, and Signal Ledger, plus institutional memory (closed-change Knowledge Models) (UCI-001 IP-5; IL-10). It reads truth; it does not invent it.

## 3. DERIVED-INTELLIGENCE LAWS

| Output | Derived from | Rule |
|--------|-------------|------|
| Impact prediction | Knowledge-Graph traversal | never persisted as fact (UCI-001 IL-11) |
| Risk prediction | prior change outcomes + Signals | IL-12 |
| Dependency prediction | `Depends-On` edges + dependency arrays | IL-13 |
| Recommendations / rankings | inference over evidence | generated on demand (IP-3) |

Every AI output SHALL **cite its provenance** (the Signals, edges, versions, decisions it used) and be **reproducible** from the evidence base (UCI-001 IP-4/IL-14). No AI output SHALL fabricate, assume, or simulate status, authority, or completion (IL-15).

## 4. PROVIDER-AGNOSTICISM & FUTURE COMPATIBILITY

**No fixed AI provider.** The inference interface is provider-neutral; a model/provider is a pluggable adapter bound at request time. A not-yet-existing AI capability is a future adapter — no core change (AUTH-INF-001 CR-INF-003; UMB-000 Part III). New AI capabilities are incorporable without foundational redesign (CR-INF-008).

## 5. INSTITUTIONAL MEMORY

Institutional memory is the append-only accumulation of change Knowledge Models and closed-change outcomes (UCI-001 IL-09), projected to this layer for predictive intelligence (IL-10). It is permanent and append-only; correcting it appends a superseding change, never edits history.

## 6. AUTHORITY-NEUTRAL AI

AI outputs are advisory knowledge only; they confer no authority, ratify nothing, and authorize no EC-series step. An AI recommendation is a derived opinion citing evidence — never a governance act (AUTH-06; UCI-001 Part XVIII).

## 7. TRACEABILITY

Every AI output is reverse-traceable to the exact evidence it cited; because it is reproducible, an auditor can regenerate it from the same stores (UMB-007/016).

## AUTHORITY BOUNDARY (MANDATORY)

UMB-014 holds no constituent, governance, ratification, or EC-series authority and cannot authorize EC-1. It is knowledge/operational-intelligence only, append-only, subordinate to the frozen corpus, STATUS-001, REG-AUTO-001, UCI-001, AUTH-INF-001, and all prior determinations. It creates no AI registry/model store/engine/identifier/lifecycle, treats `00-SOURCE/`/`99-FREEZE/` as read-only, and embeds no secret (RR-07). Any conflicting statement is void to the extent of the conflict.
