## PART III — THE TWELVE CONSTITUTIONAL RULES

### CR-INF-001 — NO TERMINAL STATES

1. No UCOS artifact, program, phase, domain, universe, capability, module, feature, workflow, interaction, state, composition, service, application, platform, data structure, registry, graph, factory, ecosystem, federation, runtime, engine, authority, or governance construct MAY be interpreted as terminal.
2. **Certification closes scope. Certification never closes evolution.** A construct that is certified, frozen, or archived is closed *for the scope it certified/froze/archived*; it remains eligible for future append-only evolution and expansion (CR-INF-008).
3. "Final", "complete", "closed", "terminal", and equivalent tokens, wherever they appear in any UCOS artifact, SHALL be read as *complete for current scope*, never as *incapable of future extension*.
4. The last member of any ordered set (the terminal of a chain, the highest-numbered artifact, the head or tail of a graph) SHALL NOT be interpreted as the maximum possible member.

### CR-INF-002 — NO ARTIFICIAL NUMERIC CEILINGS

1. Numbers indicate **sequence**. Numbers do **not** indicate **limits**.
2. Worked interpretations (binding): `APPLICATION-018` does not prohibit `APPLICATION-019+`; `BLOCK-144` does not prohibit `BLOCK-145+`; `PHASE-006` does not prohibit `PHASE-007+`; `MODULE-999` does not imply a maximum number of modules; `NODE-999` does not imply a maximum number of nodes.
3. No sequence number, count, index, cardinality, or numeric identifier anywhere in UCOS SHALL be read as an upper bound on the population it enumerates.
4. Zero-padding width (e.g. `NNNNNN` in `UCOS-<CATEGORY>-NNNNNN`) is a formatting convenience for sort-stability, **not** a ceiling; padding width MAY be extended append-only if a population approaches its current width, without renumbering prior members.

### CR-INF-003 — ZERO HARD CODING

1. No constitutional artifact, architecture, registry, program, domain, universe, capability, graph, factory, or federation MAY define a hard maximum **unless imposed by physical reality** (e.g. finite storage, finite compute, protocol field widths).
2. All limits MUST be externally configurable. All capacities MUST be architecturally expandable.
3. Where a physical-reality limit exists, it SHALL be declared as *configuration bound to the physical constraint*, never as an architectural ceiling, and SHALL be raised by reconfiguration (not by renumbering or rewrite) when the physical constraint is relieved.
4. This rule embodies **Build Once · Configure Forever · Generate Anything**: capacity, extent, and shape are configuration and generation concerns, never hard-coded architecture.

### CR-INF-004 — UNIVERSAL UNIQUE IDENTITY

1. Every governed object SHALL possess a Universal Unique Identity.
2. The rule applies to: Artifacts, Programs, Phases, Domains, Capabilities, Modules, Features, Services, Applications, Platforms, Data Objects, Registries, Graphs, Nodes, Relationships, Factories, Universes, Federations, Ecosystems, and Authorities.
3. **This identity already exists and is reused, not re-created.** The append-only Universal ID Ledger (`00-BOOK/DATA/id-ledger.json`, form `UCOS-<CATEGORY>-NNNNNN`) is the authoritative allocator. AUTH-INF-001 constitutionalizes that ledger's identities as Universal Unique Identities and introduces **no new identifier namespace** (anti-duplication law, GOV-INT-001 §0; REG-AUTO-001 P4).
4. Governed objects that do not yet carry a native identifier acquire their Universal Unique Identity upon registration through REG-AUTO-001 transaction `T`; existence without registration confers a draft, not an identity of record (REG-AUTO-001 §5).

### CR-INF-005 — IDENTITY SHALL NEVER DEPEND ON SEQUENCE

1. Sequence numbers are **aliases**. Sequence numbers are **not** identity.
2. Identity SHALL remain stable regardless of Renumbering, Reclassification, Reorganization, Expansion, Evolution, or Federation.
3. Native sequence identifiers (`RUNTIME-014`, `PLATFORM-001`, `APPLICATION-018`, …) are crosswalk aliases over the stable Universal ID; changing, extending, or reordering a native sequence SHALL NOT change the Universal Unique Identity of any object.
4. Because identity is sequence-independent, expansion of any sequence (adding `RUNTIME-015`, `APPLICATION-019`, `NODE-1000`, …) disturbs no existing identity and is therefore always non-destructive.

### CR-INF-006 — UNIVERSAL ADDRESSABILITY

1. Every governed object SHALL be **addressable**.
2. Every identity SHALL be **resolvable**.
3. Every object SHALL be **discoverable** through registry mechanisms.
4. Addressability, resolvability, and discoverability are realized by the existing Universal Artifact Registry, Universal Page/ID Ledger, and Knowledge Graph (`artifacts.json`, `id-ledger.json`, `relationships.json`) and their generated indices under `REGISTRIES/`; no new resolver, address space, or discovery store is created (anti-duplication preserved).

### CR-INF-007 — OPEN REGISTRY PRINCIPLE

1. All registries are **open sets**. Registries SHALL support unlimited future entries.
2. Registries SHALL NOT define maximum counts for Artifacts, Capabilities, Nodes, Relationships, Domains, Universes, Programs, Federations, or Factories.
3. The eleven-register set fixed by GOV-INT-001 §6.2 (seven REG-AUTO-001 registers + four UCI-001 registers) is hereby interpreted as an open, append-only set: each register admits unbounded future entries, and the *set of registers itself* is extensible append-only (GOV-INT-001 §2.5, UCI-001 Part XIX).
4. A register's current entry count is a *snapshot of membership*, never a declared capacity.

### CR-INF-008 — OPEN EVOLUTION PRINCIPLE

1. All certified constructs remain eligible for future evolution through constitutional determination.
2. No certification SHALL prohibit future expansion.
3. No freeze SHALL prohibit future expansion.
4. A freeze pins an *immutable baseline* of a defined scope (a `content_hash` at `FROZEN` status — REG-AUTO-001 §5, UCI-001 Part X.2); it guarantees the frozen scope will not be mutated in place, and it simultaneously **permits** append-only growth *around* that baseline (new members, new scope, new versions via supersession — UCI-001 Parts XI, XVI). Freeze protects; it does not forbid.

### CR-INF-009 — INFINITE EXPANSION PRINCIPLE

1. All domains, programs, universes, registries, graphs, capabilities, modules, services, applications, factories, ecosystems, and federations SHALL be architecturally expandable without predefined upper limits.
2. Expansion is a first-class, always-available operation; the architecture SHALL never require a rewrite, renumber, or migration to admit the next member.
3. Each concrete expansion is realized append-only through REG-AUTO-001 transaction `T` and governed as a change by UCI-001; AUTH-INF-001 authorizes expansion *as a principle* (it is never prohibited), while the mechanics remain owned by REG-AUTO-001 and UCI-001.

### CR-INF-010 — INFINITE SCALE PRINCIPLE

1. UCOS Ω∞ SHALL NOT define architectural ceilings for: Identity Count, Artifact Count, Capability Count, Node Count, Relationship Count, Registry Count, Program Count, Domain Count, Universe Count, Federation Count, Factory Count, or Graph Count.
2. Scale expansion SHALL occur through constitutional evolution — append-only, non-destructive, identity-stable.
3. The only admissible limits are physical-reality limits (CR-INF-003), which are configuration bounds, not architectural ceilings.

### CR-INF-011 — CERTIFICATION INTERPRETATION RULE

1. Certification confirms: **Integrity, Consistency, Completeness of current scope, and Readiness.**
2. Certification does **not** establish: Finality, Termination, Maximum Scope, or Permanent Closure.
3. Every existing and future certification determination (e.g. RUNTIME-GOV-002, the Digital-Twin Certification `twin --check`) SHALL be read under this rule: it attests the integrity/consistency/completeness-of-scope/readiness it measured, and asserts nothing about the impossibility of future scope.
4. This rule is consistent with STATUS-001: certification is DOMAIN-D and confers no cross-domain or terminal meaning (STATUS-001 §2, non-projection).
