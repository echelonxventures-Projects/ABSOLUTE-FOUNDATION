## 6. Law Evolution

### 6.1 The rule

`constitutional-authority-alignment.json` `non_goals[3]`, verbatim:

> *"Adding an article, invariant or stop condition to `engine/uckp/law.py`. Extension is by registration (`UCKP-ART-17`)."*

`how_to_extend[2]`, verbatim:

> *"`engine/uckp/law.py` is never amended to fit the data (`UCKP-ART-17`)."*

### 6.2 What this means, precisely

Law evolution in UCOS is **not** the amendment of the twenty articles. It is:

| Change wanted | Lawful mechanism | Plane |
|---|---|---|
| A new governed category of entity | `VocabularyRegistry.extend()` on `GOVERNED_CATEGORY` | DATA |
| A new non-authoritative technology category | Same, on `NON_AUTHORITATIVE_CATEGORY` — and `ART-04`'s operative clause is *"every future technology"*, so an unnamed one is already bound by category | DATA / already bound |
| A new family of constitutional objects | A provider module exposing `ucko_objects()`; `universe.py` is never edited | CODE (additive, discovered) |
| A new authority role | `authority_roles` append | DATA |
| A new instrument under the law | `subordinate_instruments` append + `constitutional_superior` block | DATA |
| An amendment to an article | **No mechanism declared.** `T1` is VACANT | **Unavailable** |

**The last row is the honest one.** There is no declared amendment procedure for the twenty articles, and the tier that would exercise one is vacant. That is not a defect of the evolution model — `ART-17` is explicit that admission is *"by registration, never by amendment"* — but it does mean the repository has no route to change what an article says, only to extend what falls under it.

> *"Categories of entity that shall exist as a UCKO (Article 2). **Open by Article 17: an unknown future category is admitted through `VocabularyRegistry.extend`, not by editing this tuple.**"*

And above `NON_AUTHORITATIVE_CATEGORIES`:

> *"Also open: 'every future technology' is the operative clause, so this tuple names the present members and **the law binds the unnamed ones by category, not by list**."*

### 6.4 Verdict

**PERMANENT**, with the boundary in §6.2 stated: extensible without limit, amendable not at all. This is the intended shape, and `ART-17` is the article that intends it.
