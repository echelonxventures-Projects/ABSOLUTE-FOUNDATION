> **Purpose.** Establish the **canonical registry of the USIS universes** — the second Wave-1 capability of the Substrate Foundation (Constitution → **Universe Catalog** → Science Catalog → Meta-Model → Theory/Ontology/Taxonomy). This catalog enumerates the constitutional universes as members of an **open, recursively extensible registry** (LAW USIS-09), each mapped to its realizing MIP universe/part, each the **single canonical owner** of one science/intelligence concern, each inheriting the cross-cutting capability contract (USIS-001 Part E) and the 7 prime properties. **This instrument establishes only the catalog.** It authors no individual universe content, no science catalog (USIS-003), no meta-model (USIS-004), and no per-universe architecture — those are separately-authorized later missions.

## PART A — Constitutional basis and scope

This catalog is the registered instantiation of the constitutional universe enumeration mandated by USIS-001 and USIS-GOV-000:

- **USIS-GOV-000 §1** establishes USIS as the substrate beneath **21 constitutional universes** (USIS-002), of which the former Universal Intelligence Platform is exactly one (the Universal Intelligence Universe; UIP ⊂ USIS).
- **USIS-001 LAW USIS-01** admits *all* sciences and *all* intelligence paradigms as first-class, observer-relative universes.
- **USIS-001 LAW USIS-02** binds every universe that realizes an existing MIP universe to **reference** it, never fork a competing catalog/ontology/taxonomy/registry.
- **USIS-001 LAW USIS-05** requires exactly one home and one owning family per universe (No-Orphan).
- **USIS-001 LAW USIS-09** makes the universe set **open and recursively extensible**: a universe may contain universes; growth is append-only and uncapped.

**Scope of this instrument (Wave 1 · Mission 2).** This catalog:
- creates the `15-UNIVERSAL-SCIENCE-INTELLIGENCE/06-UNIVERSES/` home and this single registered catalog artifact;
- enumerates the 21 constitutional universes as registry rows with canonical ownership and MIP realization references;
- founds downward-only on `USIS-001` (Depends-On; parents to the USIS program root), introducing no upstream change and no cycle;
- authors **no** individual universe architecture, discipline, domain, algorithm, model, or capability content;
- reuses, without duplication, the existing registration/validation/certification engines (`ukb`, `ukbx`, `register.sh`), the universal registries, and the governance instruments as the sole authorities.

## PART C — Universe registry model (the catalog IS the registry)

Each universe is a registry row of the canonical form:

```
{ id, home: 06-UNIVERSES/<UNIVERSE>/, realizes: <MIP anchor>, owner: USIS, concern, status, parent-universe? }
```

- **Single canonical owner (LAW USIS-05).** Each universe owns exactly one science/intelligence concern and has exactly one canonical home. No two rows own the same concern; no concern is unowned.
- **Recursion (LAW USIS-09).** `parent-universe` records containment; any universe MAY register child universes/disciplines/domains without redesign. The substrate is self-similar at every level and admits new levels by registration.
- **Openness / append-only.** `USIS-U-*` growth is append-only and uncapped (Directives D3/D8/D9). A new universe registers as a new row + (later) a homed artifact; no existing row is ever rewritten, renumbered, or removed.
- **Inheritance.** Every universe inherits the cross-cutting capability contract (USIS-001 Part E: `register · describe · compose · govern · secure · monitor · observe · meter · bill · audit · prove · comply · certify · discover · search · remember · know · reason · simulate · compile · evolve · explain`) and the 7 prime properties (REPRESENTABLE · GOVERNABLE · TRACEABLE · EXPLAINABLE · SIMULATABLE · EVOLVABLE · COMPILABLE) — inherited (⟳) unless a member explicitly deviates at its own authoring.
- **This catalog is the canonical Universe Registry.** Per USIS-005 §3, the standalone USIS Master Registry is a distinct, later artifact (USIS-021); this catalog is the single canonical enumeration of the universe set. The machine-readable registry projection is produced by the **reused** universal registration mechanism (`ukb build` → `00-BOOK/DATA` + `00-BOOK/REGISTRIES`); no parallel allocator, ontology, or competing registry is created here (LAW USIS-02).

The two are distinct areas.
- **USIS-005 §3** maps artifact sequence "**USIS-002 | UNIVERSE-CATALOG | Area 06**".

**Determination:** the canonical home for the Universe Catalog and for every per-universe sub-home is **`15-UNIVERSAL-SCIENCE-INTELLIGENCE/06-UNIVERSES/`**. The blueprint `06-DOMAINS/<UNIVERSE>/` phrasing is a superseded shorthand (it conflated the universe area with the distinct cross-universe domain area) and does not govern.
