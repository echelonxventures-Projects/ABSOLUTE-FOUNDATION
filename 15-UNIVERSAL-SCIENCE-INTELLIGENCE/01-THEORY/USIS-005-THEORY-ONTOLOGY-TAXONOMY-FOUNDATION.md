## PART C — Theory foundation (area 01-THEORY, meta-model tier 6)

A **USIS Theory** is the explanatory foundation of a science/intelligence capability — the constitutional statement of *what it is and why it holds* — from which its ontology (tier 7) and taxonomy (tier 8) are derived. The Theory framework is a contract, not a content library:

- **T-1 (single explanatory owner).** Each theory has exactly one owning capability (parent edge, USIS-004 tier 6) and one canonical home; no two theories explain the same capability (Knowledge-Once).
- **T-2 (derivation edge).** A theory's ontology (tier 7) and taxonomy (tier 8) MUST declare a `Derives-From` reference to the theory; an ontology/taxonomy without an owning theory is inadmissible (fail-closed).
- **T-3 (technology neutrality, LAW USIS-04).** A theory names no vendor/model/framework; it is a specification tier (USIS-004 Part E, tiers 6–13 are technology-free).
- **T-4 (reuse before creation, LAW USIS-02).** Before authoring a theory the owner searches the canonical knowledge universe (U24 / MIP Part 19); an existing explanatory instance is **referenced**, never forked.
- **T-5 (open, recursive, LAW USIS-09).** The set of theories is uncapped; new theories append without redesign; a theory may host sub-theories (a strict forest).

This foundation authors **no individual theory**; it defines the contract every later per-member theory satisfies.

## PART D — Ontology foundation (area 02-ONTOLOGY, meta-model tier 7)

The **substrate Ontology** is the concept/relation model of USIS: the set of **concepts** (typed nodes) and **relations** (typed, directed edges) that give the universes and sciences their shared meaning. USIS-005 defines the ontology *framework* and its closure rule.

**Ontology framework:**
- **O-1 (concept).** A concept is a typed node with exactly one canonical owner, a parent concept (or a substrate root concept), and a `Derives-From` reference to its owning theory (T-2).
- **O-2 (relation).** A relation is a typed, directed edge `(source-concept) --relation-type--> (target-concept)`; both endpoints MUST resolve to registered concepts.
- **O-3 (reuse, LAW USIS-02).** The substrate ontology **reuses** the canonical U24 Knowledge / MIP Part 19 knowledge-ontology registry; it specializes concepts for science/intelligence by **reference**, and forks no competing ontology-core.
- **O-4 (single home, No-Orphan).** Every concept has exactly one ontological home; duplicate concepts are inadmissible (LAW USIS-05; USIS-011 obl 2/3/8).

**Ontology Closure rule (USIS-011 obligation 11 — foundation level, fail-closed):**
> The substrate ontology is **closed** iff (a) every registered entity (universe, science, domain, capability, knowledge object) has **exactly one** ontological concept; (b) every concept resolves through its parent chain to a **substrate root concept** (no dangling concept); and (c) **every relation endpoint** resolves to a registered concept (no dangling edge). Any unresolved concept or relation ⇒ **ontology NOT closed** ⇒ NOT integrated (LAW USIS-00 C-00.3; NOT-DONE, TRACK-001).

This foundation discharges Ontology Closure at the **foundation level** (the rule and the root-concept anchor); per-member concept instances discharge it at their own Wave-3 realization.

## PART E — Taxonomy foundation (area 03-TAXONOMY, meta-model tier 8)

The **Taxonomy** is the parent-ordered classification of sciences, universes, domains, algorithms, and models — the constitutional answer to *where each entity sits*. USIS-005 defines the taxonomy *framework* and its closure rule.

**Taxonomy framework:**
- **X-1 (taxon).** A taxon is a classification node with exactly one **parent taxon**, terminating at one of the substrate **root taxa** (Science, Universe, Domain, Algorithm, Model — the classification roots named by USIS-002/003/004).
- **X-2 (forest, acyclic).** The taxonomy is a strict forest: no taxon is its own ancestor; no cycles (CIOA; USIS-011 obl 5).
- **X-3 (derivation edge).** Every taxon declares a `Derives-From` reference to its ontology concept (tier 7 → tier 8 parent edge, USIS-004 Part C).
- **X-4 (reuse + openness).** Cross-universe classifications **reference** the canonical home (LAW USIS-02); `*-FUTURE-*` / `*-UNKNOWN-*` taxa are permanent open receptors (routed to `USIS-U-FUT` / `USIS-U-UNK`), never closed (LAW USIS-09).

**Taxonomy Closure rule (USIS-011 obligation 12 — foundation level, fail-closed):**
> The taxonomy is **closed** iff (a) **every** classified entity resolves to exactly one taxon; (b) **every** taxon resolves through its parent chain to a **substrate root taxon** (no orphan taxon); and (c) the parent relation is **acyclic** (a forest). Any orphan taxon, unclassified entity, or cycle ⇒ **taxonomy NOT closed** ⇒ NOT integrated (LAW USIS-00 C-00.3; NOT-DONE, TRACK-001).

This foundation discharges Taxonomy Closure at the **foundation level** (the rule and the root-taxa anchor); per-member taxon instances discharge it at their own Wave-3 realization.
