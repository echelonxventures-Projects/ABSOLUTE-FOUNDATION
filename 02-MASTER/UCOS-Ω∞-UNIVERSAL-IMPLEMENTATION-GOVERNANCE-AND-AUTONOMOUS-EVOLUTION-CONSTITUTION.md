# PART I — CONSTITUTIONAL POSITION & SUBORDINATION

## 1. CONSTITUTIONAL POSITION

- **CP-01** This Constitution is a **technical-governance instrument** within the Implementation family (Program IMP, Volume VOL-004). It is the operating model beneath which every implementation is planned, admitted, built, measured, validated, certified, registered, synchronized, and evolved.
- **CP-02** It holds **AUTHORITY = NONE**. "Universal", "Constitution", and "Governance" denote **implementation-discipline supremacy** — the guarantee that no implementation escapes the model — never governance, constituent, or ratification authority.
- **CP-03** It **integrates** the IMP-000 package (Implementation Governance Baseline, Implementation Master Plan, Technology Constitution, Implementation Program Tracker) as its concrete engineering discipline, and consumes the Universal Runtime Constitution (RUNTIME-001) as the execution substrate. It **duplicates none of them**.
- **CP-04** It introduces **no new constitutional concept** that already exists in the frozen corpus or the IMP-000 package; where a concept exists, this instrument **references** it rather than restating it.

## 2. SUBORDINATION (MANDATORY)

- **SB-01** This Constitution and everything derived from it are **subordinate at all times** to the frozen constitutional corpus (`00-SOURCE/`, `99-FREEZE/`), its adjudicated determinations, STATUS-001, REG-AUTO-001, UCI-001, AUTH-INF-001, and all prior determinations.
- **SB-02** No model, engine, framework, or autonomous capability herein may **modify, reinterpret, override, or bypass** any frozen constitutional artifact or determination. The frozen corpus is **read-only**.
- **SB-03** No autonomous ("self-*") capability may alter constitutional authority. Constitutional change occurs **only** through the established constitutional amendment process, never through learning, optimization, evolution, or self-modification.
- **SB-04** Any statement in this instrument that conflicts with a superior authority is **void to the extent of the conflict**.

# PART II — THE UNIVERSAL MODELS

## 3. UNIVERSAL REQUIREMENT MODEL

- **URM-01** Every implementation SHALL declare its **requirements** as explicit, versioned, traceable statements before work begins; undeclared requirements are not in scope.
- **URM-02** Every requirement SHALL trace upward to an authorizing artifact (a determination, an architecture, a catalog, or an IMP-000 authorization) and downward to the evidence that satisfies it.
- **URM-03** A requirement is **objectively satisfiable** only when its acceptance evidence is machine-checkable; a requirement with no verifiable evidence is **not admissible** (see Admission Engine, §8).
- **URM-04** Requirements are **append-only and versioned**; a changed requirement is a new version linked to its predecessor, never a silent rewrite.

## 4. UNIVERSAL GOAL MODEL

- **UGM-01** Every implementation SHALL declare the **goals** it advances — the intended outcomes, expressed as measurable states, not activities.
- **UGM-02** Each goal SHALL bind to one or more requirements (§3) and to the measurement definitions (§10) that evidence its attainment.
- **UGM-03** A goal without a measurement definition is **inadmissible**; UCOS does not pursue unmeasurable goals.
- **UGM-04** Goals are traceable to the authorities they serve and may never assert an outcome that a superior authority prohibits.

## 5. UNIVERSAL TARGET MODEL

- **UTM-01** Every implementation SHALL declare **targets** — the objective thresholds (values, ranges, or predicates) at which each goal is deemed attained.
- **UTM-02** Targets SHALL be **deterministic and reproducible**: the same evidence evaluated against the same target yields the same verdict on every machine and every run.
- **UTM-03** A target SHALL never be relaxed to manufacture a passing verdict; target changes are versioned, justified, and recorded (see Version/Lineage, §26 via change intelligence).
- **UTM-04** Missing a target is a **first-class recorded outcome**, never hidden, and blocks completion (§9) until resolved or explicitly, auditably waived within the bounds a superior authority permits.

# PART III — THE CONSTITUTIONAL ENGINES

## 6. CONSTITUTIONAL PREREQUISITE PRINCIPLE

- **PR-01** **No implementation may begin unless ALL constitutional prerequisites are objectively satisfied.** A prerequisite is *objectively satisfied* only when its satisfying evidence is present, resolvable, and machine-checkable.
- **PR-02** Prerequisites include: declared requirements (§3), goals (§4), targets (§5), satisfied dependencies, a recorded authorization, and passing readiness (§7).

## 7. CONSTITUTIONAL READINESS ENGINE

- **RE-01** The Readiness Engine evaluates, before admission, whether an implementation's declared requirements, goals, targets, dependencies, and authorizations are **all present and resolvable**.
- **RE-02** Readiness is **fail-closed**: an unresolved dependency, an unmeasurable goal, or a missing authorization yields NOT-READY, and NOT-READY blocks admission.
- **RE-03** Readiness verdicts are evidence-bound, deterministic, and recorded; a readiness claim without evidence is void.

## 8. CONSTITUTIONAL ADMISSION ENGINE

- **AE-01** The Admission Engine admits an implementation into execution **only** when Readiness (§7) is READY and every constitutional prerequisite (§6) is objectively satisfied.
- **AE-02** Admission is **fail-closed and non-waivable** with respect to constitutional constraints: no admission may waive a frozen-corpus constraint, a determination, or an external gate.
- **AE-03** Every admission is recorded, attributed, and auditable; an unadmitted implementation that proceeds is a **governance breach** (§21).

## 9. UNIVERSAL COMPLETION ENGINE

- **CE-01** An implementation is **COMPLETE** only when every declared requirement is satisfied by evidence, every goal has met its target (§5), all quality gates pass, and registration/synchronization (§11–§12 via Frameworks) is complete.
- **CE-02** Completion is **objective**: it is computed from evidence, never asserted. A completion claim unsupported by evidence is **INVALID** (REG-AUTO-001 §7 Atomic Creation Law analogue).
- **CE-03** Completion of an artifact **enables but does not auto-authorize** its dependents.
- **CE-04** No completion may be claimed while an unresolved prerequisite, target miss, or unresolved reference is misrepresented as resolved.

## 10. UNIVERSAL MEASUREMENT ENGINE

- **ME-01** Every goal and target SHALL have a **measurement definition**: the deterministic function over authoritative evidence that yields its verdict.
- **ME-02** Measurements are **observations, not claims** — computed over authoritative sources (artifacts, ledger, signals, graph), never hand-entered.
- **ME-03** Measurements are **reproducible**: rebuilding the evidence base reproduces the verdict. Non-reproducible measurements are defects.
- **ME-04** Every measurement is cited to its evidence; a measurement without provenance is void (no fabrication).

# PART IV — THE UNIVERSAL FRAMEWORKS

*Each framework below is a binding constitutional article. Frameworks are the standing model; the IMP-000 package, the architecture constitutions, the runtime constitution, and the Master Book machinery are their concrete realizations. Frameworks add no authority and duplicate no existing concept — they compose existing ones into one operating model.*

## 11. UNIVERSAL EXCELLENCE FRAMEWORK
- **EX-01** Every implementation SHALL be held to the maximization mandate (§20); "acceptable" is never sufficient where "excellent" is objectively achievable and evidenced.
- **EX-02** Excellence is measured (§10), never asserted; excellence claims are evidence-bound.

## 12. UNIVERSAL VALIDATION FRAMEWORK
- **VA-01** Every implementation SHALL pass structural, schema, and semantic validation before completion.
- **VA-02** **No implementation may bypass validation.** Validation is fail-closed; a validation gap blocks completion.
- **VA-03** Validation is deterministic and reproducible (§10 ME-03).

## 13. UNIVERSAL CERTIFICATION FRAMEWORK
- **CF-01** Certification attests **engineering completeness and constraint-conformance only** — never constitutional validity (inherits IMP-000 CE-01).
- **CF-02** **No implementation may bypass certification.** Certification evaluates the integrity domains defined by the certification runtime over the real state.
- **CF-03** A certification that would require crossing a boundary (§21) is void and blocked. Certification is **non-terminal**: it is re-evaluated on every change.

## 14. UNIVERSAL QUALITY FRAMEWORK
- **QF-01** Changes ship with appropriate tests; critical paths require coverage; code conforms to conventions and passes lint/format/SAST in CI (inherits IMP-000 QR-*).
- **QF-02** **No implementation may reduce quality.** A change that lowers a measured quality signal without an authorized, recorded justification is blocked.
- **QF-03** Quality gates are automated where feasible and non-optional for release.

## 15. UNIVERSAL REPOSITORY INTEGRITY FRAMEWORK
- **RI-01** Every artifact SHALL obey "Artifact Creation = Artifact Registration" (REG-AUTO-001): creation is complete only when the atomic registration transaction succeeds.
- **RI-02** **No implementation may introduce repository drift.** The synchronized registers (registry, twin, control-tower, portal, knowledge graph, dependencies) SHALL agree with the artifacts on disk; drift is a guard failure.
- **RI-03** `00-SOURCE/` and `99-FREEZE/` are inviolable and read-only.

## 16. UNIVERSAL ENGINEERING FRAMEWORK
- **EG-01** Engineering conforms to the Technology Constitution's principles and the architecture/runtime constitutions; declarative sources of truth, provisional constitutional encodings, and registry-resolved values are mandatory (inherits TP-01/TP-02).
- **EG-02** **No implementation may introduce architectural degradation.** A change that violates an architecture constitution or a catalog contract is blocked.

## 17. UNIVERSAL GOVERNANCE FRAMEWORK
- **GF-01** Every implementation is subject to Readiness (§7), Admission (§8), Completion (§9), and the enforcement gates of REG-AUTO-001 / UMB-IMP-001.
- **GF-02** **No implementation may bypass governance, registration, or synchronization.** Enforcement is fail-closed and audited.

## 18. UNIVERSAL LEARNING FRAMEWORK
- **LF-01** UCOS SHALL derive learning **only** from authoritative evidence (ledger snapshot history, typed knowledge graph, signals, git causation) — never from fabricated or unattributed sources.
- **LF-02** Learning informs planning, optimization, and evolution but is **advisory to** — never overriding of — the constitutional engines and superior authority.

## 19. UNIVERSAL EVOLUTION FRAMEWORK
- **EV-01** UCOS evolves **append-only**: new capability is a new, versioned artifact/relationship, never a rewrite or renumber of an existing one (AUTH-INF-001 CR-INF-007).
- **EV-02** Evolution is **infinite-expansion compliant**: no fixed ceiling on artifacts, programs, categories, volumes, relationships, or domains; growth is by declaration, not redesign.
- **EV-03** Every evolutionary step is admitted (§8), measured (§10), validated (§12), certified (§13), and registered (§15) like any other implementation.

## 20. UNIVERSAL OPTIMIZATION FRAMEWORK
- **OP-01** Optimization SHALL improve measured signals (§10) without reducing quality (§14), degrading architecture (§16), or introducing drift (§15/§21).
- **OP-02** An optimization that cannot be evidenced as a net non-regression is **not admissible**.

## 21. UNIVERSAL SELF-HEALING FRAMEWORK
- **SH-01** UCOS MAY detect and remediate drift, broken references, and failed synchronization by **re-running deterministic, idempotent machinery** (e.g., the registration transaction) — never by silently mutating authoritative history.
- **SH-02** Self-healing is **bounded**: it may only restore conformance to the model; it may never relax a target, waive a constraint, or alter constitutional authority.

## 22. UNIVERSAL SELF-PLANNING FRAMEWORK
- **SP-01** UCOS MAY derive implementation plans from declared requirements, goals, targets, and the dependency graph, subject to Readiness (§7) and Admission (§8).
- **SP-02** Self-produced plans are **proposals**; they acquire force only through the same authorization discipline as any implementation.

## 23. UNIVERSAL SELF-AUDITING FRAMEWORK
- **SA-01** UCOS SHALL continuously self-audit: registration completeness, referential integrity, append-only invariants, determinism, and drift — via the enforcement and certification runtimes.
- **SA-02** Every audit outcome is recorded in the append-only audit trail, attributed and timestamped; a boundary breach (§25) is logged, escalated, and remediated.

## 24. UNIVERSAL SELF-KNOWLEDGE FRAMEWORK
- **SK-01** Every UCOS entity is a first-class, reachable node with identity and content; to exist in UCOS is to be **registered in and reachable through** the Master Book.
- **SK-02** UCOS's knowledge of itself is a **computed observation** over authoritative sources, refreshed deterministically — never a static or hand-entered claim.

## 25. UNIVERSAL INFINITE EXPANSION FRAMEWORK
- **IE-01** The model imposes **no numeric ceiling**; zero-padding is a sort-stability convenience that widens append-only, renumbering nothing (AUTH-INF-001 CR-INF-002).
- **IE-02** Every register the model presents is an **open set of unbounded cardinality** (CR-INF-010); a new member is an append, never a redesign.

## 26. UNIVERSAL FUTURE EVOLUTION FRAMEWORK
- **FE-01** Future capability SHALL be admissible **without re-architecture**: new programs, domains, relationship types, and formats are declared, discovered, and registered by the existing metadata-driven machinery.
- **FE-02** Change, version, and lineage of every artifact SHALL be **derivable** from the append-only history + typed edges + causation, so the future is always traceable to the past.

## 27. UNIVERSAL CIVILIZATION CONTINUITY FRAMEWORK
- **CC-01** The corpus SHALL remain **reconstructible**: rebuilding is a pure, deterministic function of `corpus + ledger + signal ledger`, so continuity does not depend on any single runtime, machine, or operator.
- **CC-02** No implementation may create a single point of failure for the knowledge, identity, or traceability of the ecosystem.

## 28. UNIVERSAL LONG-TERM SUSTAINABILITY FRAMEWORK
- **LS-01** All authoritative state is **append-only, versioned, and independently auditable**; nothing durable is destroyed, only superseded with lineage preserved.
- **LS-02** Machinery SHALL use standard, portable tooling and deterministic generation so the model remains operable and verifiable over the long term.

# PART VI — MAXIMIZATION MANDATE, AUTONOMY SUBORDINATION & AMENDMENT

## 29. MAXIMIZATION MANDATE

- **MX-01** Every implementation SHALL be **objectively measured against constitutional evidence** and SHALL maximize, to the extent objectively achievable and evidenced:
  completeness · correctness · quality · architecture · engineering · security · performance · scalability · maintainability · observability · traceability · determinism · extensibility · interoperability · reusability · composability · automation · future readiness · civilization-scale readiness.
- **MX-02** Each dimension of MX-01 SHALL have, or resolve to, a measurement definition (§10); a dimension that cannot be evidenced is reported as *unmeasured*, never as *satisfied*.
- **MX-03** Maximization is bounded by superior authority and the prohibitions (§Part V): no dimension may be advanced by crossing a boundary or relaxing a constitutional constraint.

## 30. AUTONOMY SUBORDINATION

- **AS-01** This Constitution establishes UCOS Ω∞ as a **self-learning, self-improving, self-validating, self-certifying, self-optimizing, self-healing, self-governing, self-planning, self-auditing, continuously-evolving** constitutional engineering ecosystem.
- **AS-02** **ALL autonomous behaviour remains subordinate to constitutional authority.** Every autonomous action passes the same Readiness, Admission, Completion, Validation, Certification, Registration, and Synchronization gates as any other implementation.
- **AS-03** No autonomous capability may create authority, waive a constraint, relax a target, alter frozen artifacts, or amend constitutional authority.

## 31. AMENDMENT

- **AM-01** This Constitution is **append-only**. It is amended by a new, versioned amendment artifact linked to it — never rewritten in place and never renumbered.
- **AM-02** An amendment SHALL itself pass every engine and framework herein and SHALL not introduce a duplicate constitutional concept or a conflict with superior authority.
- **AM-03** Amendment of the **constitutional corpus** is **out of scope** for this instrument and occurs only through the established constitutional amendment process.
