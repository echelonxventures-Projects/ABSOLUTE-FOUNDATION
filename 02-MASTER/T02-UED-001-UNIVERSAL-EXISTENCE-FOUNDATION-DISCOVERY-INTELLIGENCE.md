Each is assessed for its capacity to hold *existing forms + current implementations + future forms + unknown constructs (∞)* — grounded in the ratified infinite-expansion canon (`AUTH-INF-001` open/unbounded/non-terminal; UAF UEP-1…10; `ARCH-001` non-terminal; `UNIV-ENTITY-001` scale-invariance Particle→Civilization on one substrate).
