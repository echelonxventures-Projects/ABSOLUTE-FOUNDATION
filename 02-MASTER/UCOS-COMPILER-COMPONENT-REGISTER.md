> **WHY THIS EXISTS.** UAKOS-CLOSURE-009 measured these components as CANONICAL-OWNERSHIP-INFERRED.
> `UCOS-COMP` is referenced by 100 identifiers across the corpus and only two have ever had a document;
> the rest appeared solely in gap registers, implementation-wave lists and backlogs — records of what
> was missing. `01-UNREALIZED-CKO-INVENTORY.md` had recorded them as unrealized and located the source.
>
> **NOTHING HERE IS AUTHORED.** Each Purpose below is transcribed from the `Purpose` field the source
> states for that component, trimmed to its first sentence and to nothing else. No component is named,
> numbered, ordered, specified or interpreted by this register.
>
> **NINE IDENTIFIERS ARE DELIBERATELY ABSENT.** `UCOS-COMP-001000`, `-002000` … `-009000` end in `-000`
> and carry no `Purpose` in the source because they are section headings introducing each family, not
> components. A heading is not a component, and an identifier this register cannot transcribe is one it
> does not claim — the same rule that kept LAW Ω∞-000 out of the constitutional laws register.
