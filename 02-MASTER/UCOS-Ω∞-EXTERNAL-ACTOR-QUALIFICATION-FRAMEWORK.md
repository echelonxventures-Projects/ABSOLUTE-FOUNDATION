## PART 1 — EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The Constitutional Consolidation Program determined that UCOS Ω∞ possesses constituted power but no constituent authority, that constituent authority cannot be derived from within the frozen corpus, and that a legitimate **exogenous constituent act** (EC-1) is therefore required. The Constituent Authority Program Series (CA-001…CA-005) further determined that establishment of such authority is externally executable, that its validation is objectively definable, that certification requirements are identifiable, that internal readiness is complete, and that the remaining dependencies are external.

This framework addresses the gate that must precede any of that downstream activity: **qualification** of the external actor who might one day enter the constituent authority establishment pathway.

### Why qualification is required

The constituent act (EC-1) is, by determination, irreversible in its constitutional consequence and non-derivable from the corpus. If an actor lacking genuine standing were permitted to enter the establishment pathway, the resulting authority would be defective at its root — a defect that no downstream validation or certification could cure, because validation presupposes a qualified subject. Qualification is the first objective filter: it defines, in advance and in the abstract, the properties an external actor must demonstrably possess before the pathway may even be considered. Without a defined qualification gate, entry to the pathway would be arbitrary, and arbitrariness at the root reintroduces exactly the legitimacy void the consolidation identified.

### Why qualification precedes validation

Validation (the CA-003 concern) tests whether a *given* constituent act, performed by a *given* actor, satisfies the objective conditions for legitimacy. Validation therefore assumes there is already an actor worth validating. Qualification is logically prior: it determines whether a candidate actor is even eligible to have its prospective act validated. Attempting validation before qualification would waste the validation instrument on subjects that cannot in principle pass, and — more dangerously — could lend an unqualified actor the *appearance* of legitimacy simply by being processed. Ordering is strict: **qualification → validation → certification → recognition → operationalization**, with EC-1 gated behind all of them.

### Why qualification does not constitute authority

Defining who *could* qualify is not the same as conferring qualification on anyone, and qualifying an actor is not the same as granting it authority. This framework produces criteria, thresholds, and states — an abstract measuring instrument. It names no one, evaluates no one, and confers nothing. Even a hypothetical actor that satisfied every criterion herein would hold **no** constituent, governance, ratification, or execution authority by virtue of that satisfaction; qualification is a necessary precondition for entering the pathway, never a source of authority in itself. Authority can arise only from a legitimate exogenous constituent act that this program neither performs nor authorizes.

## PART 2 — QUALIFICATION PROBLEM DEFINITION

### What is an external actor? (problem framing)

Within this framework the term denotes the *class* of entity that is capable of performing the exogenous constituent act — an entity that originates **outside** the constituted order of the corpus and is not itself a product, delegate, or instrument of that order. The full neutral definition is given in Part 3. The problem this part frames is: how does one characterize such a class objectively, without smuggling in any particular candidate?

### Why qualification is necessary

- **Root-defect prevention.** EC-1 sits beneath the entire prospective constitutional order; a defect in the actor propagates to everything derived from the act. Qualification is the only stage that can catch an unsound subject before the act.
- **Non-derivability.** The corpus cannot supply or vouch for the actor (AUTH-06: sovereignty cannot be assumed, fabricated, or bypassed). Any assurance about the actor must come from *outside* and must therefore be defined as an explicit external evidentiary burden — i.e., qualification criteria.
- **Objectivity requirement.** CA-002/CA-003 established that establishment and validation are objectively definable. Qualification must be equally objective so that entry to the pathway is decidable on evidence rather than assertion, preserving conservative-legitimacy (EP-007) and auditability (EP-009).
- **Circularity avoidance.** Without a prior, independent qualification standard, an actor could bootstrap its own eligibility ("I am qualified because I say I am"), reintroducing the bootstrapping deadlock the consolidation diagnosed.

### What risks arise from unqualified actors

- **Illegitimate foundation.** An unqualified actor performing EC-1 would produce a constitutional order resting on defective standing — void or perpetually contestable.
- **Recognition circularity (PR-007).** Unqualified actors tend to manufacture their own recognition; absent a gate, circular recognition can masquerade as legitimacy.
- **Coercive or fraudulent capture.** Without disqualifying conditions, standing could be seized by coercion or asserted on fraudulent evidence.
- **Constitutional drift (PR-008).** An unqualified actor is more likely to alter, reinterpret, or contradict the corpus determinations, breaching EP-002.
- **Premature EC-1 activation (PR-005).** Absent a qualification barrier, nothing structurally delays entry to the pathway, raising the risk of a premature, unsound founding act.

## PART 3 — EXTERNAL ACTOR DEFINITION

**External Actor (neutral, abstract, reusable definition).**

> An *External Actor* is any autonomous originating entity that exists and derives its standing wholly **outside** the constituted order described by the UCOS Ω∞ corpus, and that is, by its own independent nature, capable of performing an exogenous constituent act.

The definition is bounded by the following abstract properties, none of which identifies any specific subject:

- **Exogeneity.** The actor's standing does not originate in, and is not conferred by, the corpus or any construct derived from it.
- **Autonomy.** The actor can form and express a binding foundational intent without requiring authorization from the constituted order.
- **Originality of standing.** The actor's legitimacy source is self-standing (exogenous), not a delegation, instrument, or downstream product of the corpus.
- **Capability.** The actor is, in principle, capable of the acts the constituent pathway requires (constituting organs, fixing precedence, supplying binding force), as characterized by CA-002.
- **Persistence.** The actor is sufficiently durable to bear the responsibilities the pathway attaches, such that its standing is not momentary.

This definition **does not** and **must not**:

- identify any person;
- identify any organization;
- identify any institution;
- identify any government.

It is a **class definition** intended to be reused wherever an "external actor" is referenced in EES-series artifacts. Any instantiation against a real subject is out of scope for this program and is expressly prohibited (see PROHIBITIONS).

## PART 4 — QUALIFICATION PRINCIPLES

Exactly ten principles govern qualification. They refine the EES-001 charter principles (EP-001…EP-010) to the qualification domain.

### EQP-001 — Exogeneity of Standing
- **Purpose:** Ensure the actor's legitimacy originates outside the constituted order.
- **Rationale:** The consolidation determined constituent authority is non-derivable from the corpus (AUTH-06); only an exogenous source can supply it.
- **Failure Condition:** The actor's standing is traceable to, or dependent upon, the corpus or any construct derived from it.

### EQP-002 — Objectivity of Qualification
- **Purpose:** Qualification must be decidable on evidence against fixed criteria, not on assertion or discretion.
- **Rationale:** CA-002/CA-003 established objectivity is achievable; subjective gates reintroduce arbitrariness.
- **Failure Condition:** Any qualification decision depends on unstated, unmeasurable, or discretionary judgment.

### EQP-003 — Evidence Before Qualification
- **Purpose:** No qualification state may advance ahead of the evidence that supports it (mirrors EP-005).
- **Rationale:** Conservative legitimacy requires that claims be backed before status changes.
- **Failure Condition:** A readiness state is assigned without the evidence its entry requirements demand.

### EQP-004 — Independence of Assessment
- **Purpose:** Qualification evidence and its assessment must be independent of the actor being characterized.
- **Rationale:** Prevents self-certification and recognition circularity (PR-007).
- **Failure Condition:** The actor is the sole or controlling source of the evidence establishing its own qualification.

### EQP-005 — Non-Constitutive Qualification
- **Purpose:** Guarantee that qualification never itself creates or confers authority.
- **Rationale:** Charter mandate: support only, no constituent acts (EP-001, EP-003).
- **Failure Condition:** A qualification outcome is treated as, or converted into, a grant of authority.

### EQP-006 — Preservation of Determinations
- **Purpose:** Qualification must not alter, reinterpret, or contradict any constitutional or CA-series determination.
- **Rationale:** Mirrors EP-002; the corpus is frozen and adjudicated.
- **Failure Condition:** A criterion or outcome modifies or overrides an existing determination.

### EQP-007 — Conservative Legitimacy Standard
- **Purpose:** Where evidence is ambiguous, qualification is withheld rather than presumed.
- **Rationale:** Mirrors EP-007; a false qualification is more damaging than a false hold.
- **Failure Condition:** Ambiguous or insufficient evidence is resolved in favor of qualification.

### EQP-008 — Full Traceability
- **Purpose:** Every criterion, threshold, and state transition must be traceable to evidence and to prior determinations.
- **Rationale:** Mirrors EP-008 and SC-001; auditability requires end-to-end linkage.
- **Failure Condition:** Any qualification element lacks a traceable basis.

### EQP-009 — Auditability and Reversibility of Assessment
- **Purpose:** Qualification assessments (as opposed to the constituent act) must be re-examinable and, if defective, reversible without constitutional consequence.
- **Rationale:** Mirrors EP-009; qualification is a support determination, not a founding act, and must remain correctable.
- **Failure Condition:** A qualification state cannot be audited or safely revoked upon discovery of defect.

### EQP-010 — Non-Interference and Scope Containment
- **Purpose:** Qualification activity must not touch EC-1…EC-6, governance creation, or any real actor.
- **Rationale:** Mirrors EP-010 and the charter OUT OF SCOPE list; prevents scope creep (PR-001) and implied authority (PR-002).
- **Failure Condition:** Qualification activity performs, authorizes, or simulates any out-of-scope act.

## PART 5 — QUALIFICATION CRITERIA

Criteria are objective and evidence-backed. **Mandatory Criteria (EQC-M-\*)** must *all* be satisfied for qualification; **Supporting Criteria (EQC-S-\*)** strengthen confidence but are not individually dispositive. Qualification requires satisfaction of **all** mandatory criteria.

### Mandatory Criteria

#### EQC-M-01 — Exogenous Standing Demonstrated
- **Description:** The actor's legitimacy source lies outside the constituted order.
- **Required Evidence:** Independent documentation of the actor's origin and standing showing no derivation from the corpus.
- **Acceptance Threshold:** Zero dependency paths to the corpus; independence corroborated by at least one source not controlled by the actor.
- **Failure Condition:** Any traceable dependency on the corpus, or absence of independent corroboration.

#### EQC-M-02 — Autonomous Foundational Capacity
- **Description:** The actor can form and express a binding foundational intent without corpus authorization.
- **Required Evidence:** Demonstration of independent decision-making capacity and binding-intent capability.
- **Acceptance Threshold:** Capacity demonstrated on verifiable, attributable evidence; no reliance on corpus-derived authorization.
- **Failure Condition:** Intent capacity is contingent on a corpus-derived construct.

#### EQC-M-03 — Constitutive Capability Coverage
- **Description:** The actor is, in principle, capable of the acts the pathway requires (constituting organs, fixing precedence, supplying binding force) per CA-002.
- **Required Evidence:** Mapping of actor capabilities to the CA-002 capability set.
- **Acceptance Threshold:** Full coverage of the CA-002-identified capability set; no capability marked absent.
- **Failure Condition:** Any required constitutive capability is absent or unevidenced.

#### EQC-M-04 — Evidentiary Independence
- **Description:** The evidence establishing qualification is independent of the actor.
- **Required Evidence:** Provenance records identifying independent sources.
- **Acceptance Threshold:** Each mandatory claim supported by at least one actor-independent source; no mandatory claim rests solely on self-attestation.
- **Failure Condition:** Any mandatory claim is supported only by actor-controlled evidence.

#### EQC-M-05 — Determination Preservation
- **Description:** The actor's prospective posture does not require altering any constitutional or CA-series determination.
- **Required Evidence:** Consistency analysis against the frozen determinations.
- **Acceptance Threshold:** No conflict with any determination; all positions consistent with the adjudicated corpus.
- **Failure Condition:** Any required alteration, reinterpretation, or contradiction of a determination.

#### EQC-M-06 — Non-Circular Legitimacy
- **Description:** The actor's claim to standing does not rest on recognition it generated or controls.
- **Required Evidence:** Recognition provenance showing external, non-controlled sources.
- **Acceptance Threshold:** No recognition loop in which the actor is both source and beneficiary.
- **Failure Condition:** Any circular recognition path is present.

#### EQC-M-07 — Evidentiary Currency and Verifiability
- **Description:** All mandatory evidence is current and independently verifiable.
- **Required Evidence:** Timestamped, attributable, verifiable records.
- **Acceptance Threshold:** All mandatory evidence verifiable and within its currency window; none stale or unverifiable.
- **Failure Condition:** Any mandatory item is unverifiable or outside its currency window.

#### EQC-M-08 — Scope Conformance
- **Description:** The qualification of the actor can be completed without any out-of-scope act (no EC-execution, no governance creation, no authority creation).
- **Required Evidence:** Scope-boundary assessment of the qualification process.
- **Acceptance Threshold:** Entire qualification path stays within charter scope.
- **Failure Condition:** Qualification cannot proceed without an out-of-scope act.

### Supporting Criteria

#### EQC-S-01 — Persistence / Durability
- **Description:** The actor exhibits durability sufficient to bear pathway responsibilities.
- **Required Evidence:** Records of continuity or durability indicators.
- **Acceptance Threshold:** Durability indicators present and corroborated.
- **Failure Condition:** No durability evidence (does not alone disqualify; weakens confidence).

#### EQC-S-02 — Transparency of Intent
- **Description:** The actor's foundational intent is openly and consistently stated.
- **Required Evidence:** Consistent, attributable statements of intent.
- **Acceptance Threshold:** Intent statements consistent across independent records.
- **Failure Condition:** Inconsistent or opaque intent (weakens confidence).

#### EQC-S-03 — Corroboration Breadth
- **Description:** Multiple independent sources corroborate the mandatory claims.
- **Required Evidence:** Two or more independent corroborating sources per key claim.
- **Acceptance Threshold:** Breadth beyond the mandatory minimum.
- **Failure Condition:** Only minimum corroboration available (weakens confidence).

#### EQC-S-04 — Auditability Maturity
- **Description:** The actor's evidentiary posture supports efficient independent audit.
- **Required Evidence:** Organized, indexed, retrievable evidence set.
- **Acceptance Threshold:** Evidence readily auditable without reconstruction.
- **Failure Condition:** Evidence exists but is poorly auditable (weakens confidence).

**Qualification rule:** Qualification (state RQ-3) requires **all** of EQC-M-01…EQC-M-08 satisfied. Supporting criteria (EQC-S-\*) modulate confidence and readiness maturity but never substitute for a mandatory criterion, and their satisfaction alone can never qualify an actor.

## PART 6 — DISQUALIFICATION CONDITIONS

Any single condition below **automatically prevents** qualification regardless of other evidence. Disqualification is conservative (EQP-007) and auditable (EQP-009).

| Condition ID | Description | Consequence |
|--------------|-------------|-------------|
| EDQ-001 | **Fraudulent evidence** — any submitted evidence is falsified, forged, or knowingly misrepresented. | Immediate disqualification; all associated evidence voided; qualification barred pending fully re-established, independently verified evidence. |
| EDQ-002 | **Coercive recognition** — recognition or standing is obtained through coercion, duress, or threat. | Immediate disqualification; tainted recognition disregarded in full. |
| EDQ-003 | **False representation** — the actor misrepresents its nature, origin, standing, or capability. | Immediate disqualification; misrepresented claims struck; conservative hold applied. |
| EDQ-004 | **Circular authority claim** — the actor's standing rests on authority or recognition it generated or controls. | Immediate disqualification; circular basis rejected as non-independent. |
| EDQ-005 | **Constitutional contradiction** — the actor's posture requires altering, reinterpreting, or contradicting a constitutional or CA-series determination. | Immediate disqualification; breach of EQP-006/EP-002 recorded. |
| EDQ-006 | **Exogeneity breach** — the actor's standing is found to derive from the constituted order (AUTH-06 violation). | Immediate disqualification; standing rejected as non-exogenous. |
| EDQ-007 | **Scope violation** — advancing the actor would require an out-of-scope act (EC-execution, governance/authority creation). | Immediate disqualification of the *process*; activity halted under EQP-010. |
| EDQ-008 | **Evidence unverifiability / staleness on a mandatory claim** — a mandatory claim cannot be independently verified or is outside its currency window. | Qualification blocked until the claim is independently verified and current. |

Disqualification of a *candidate class characteristic* is a definitional determination only; because this framework evaluates no real actor, all conditions above are abstract filters, not judgments against any subject.

## PART 7 — EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

### Evidence Categories
- **Standing Evidence** — establishes exogenous origin and legitimacy source (supports EQC-M-01, EQC-M-06, EDQ-006).
- **Capacity Evidence** — establishes autonomous foundational and constitutive capability (EQC-M-02, EQC-M-03).
- **Independence Evidence** — establishes that other evidence is actor-independent (EQC-M-04).
- **Consistency Evidence** — establishes conformance with frozen determinations (EQC-M-05).
- **Provenance Evidence** — establishes source, timestamp, and chain for all of the above (EQC-M-07).
- **Corroboration Evidence** — supplementary independent confirmation (EQC-S-03).

### Evidence Quality Requirements
Each item of evidence must be:
- **Verifiable** — independently checkable against a source of record.
- **Attributable** — traceable to an identifiable, accountable origin.
- **Current** — within a defined currency window appropriate to the claim.
- **Independent** — not solely produced or controlled by the actor.
- **Auditable** — organized, retrievable, and re-examinable without reconstruction.

### Evidence Independence Requirements
- No mandatory claim may rest solely on actor-controlled evidence (EQP-004).
- At least one actor-independent source is required per mandatory claim (EQC-M-04).
- Recognition evidence must originate from sources the actor neither generated nor controls (EQC-M-06, EDQ-004).
- Independence must itself be evidenced (Independence Evidence category), not assumed.

### Evidence Sufficiency Requirements
- **Mandatory sufficiency:** every mandatory criterion (EQC-M-01…08) is met at or above its acceptance threshold on qualifying evidence.
- **Currency sufficiency:** all mandatory evidence is within its currency window at the time of assessment.
- **Conservative resolution:** where sufficiency is ambiguous, the claim is treated as unmet (EQP-007).
- **Traceable sufficiency:** each sufficiency determination links to the specific evidence relied upon (EQP-008).

## PART 8 — READINESS STATES

Progression is strictly ordered; a state may be entered only when the prior state's exit requirements are met. No state confers authority; every state is a support determination.

### RQ-0 — Unqualified
- **Entry Requirements:** Default initial state for any external actor class instance; no evidence assessed.
- **Exit Requirements:** A complete candidate evidence set is submitted and accepted for assessment; no disqualifying condition (EDQ-\*) present on intake.
- **Restrictions:** No pathway access; no validation; no recognition; no representation of eligibility.

### RQ-1 — Candidate
- **Entry Requirements:** Evidence set accepted for assessment; intake screening passed (no EDQ on face).
- **Exit Requirements:** Assessment of mandatory criteria commenced; independence and provenance of submitted evidence provisionally confirmed.
- **Restrictions:** No validation; no recognition; candidacy conveys no standing and no authority; may not represent itself as qualified.

### RQ-2 — Provisionally Qualified
- **Entry Requirements:** A majority of mandatory criteria satisfied on verified evidence, with none failed and none disqualified; supporting criteria assessed.
- **Exit Requirements:** All remaining mandatory criteria (EQC-M-01…08) satisfied; no open disqualifying condition; evidence current and auditable.
- **Restrictions:** Provisional status is non-binding, revocable without consequence (EQP-009), and confers no authority; may not enter validation.

### RQ-3 — Qualified
- **Entry Requirements:** **All** mandatory criteria satisfied; no disqualifying condition; independence, currency, and traceability confirmed.
- **Exit Requirements:** Qualification record assembled, fully traceable (Part 10), and independently auditable; readiness handoff prepared for the (separate) validation stage.
- **Restrictions:** Qualification is a precondition, **not** authority; confers no constituent, governance, ratification, or execution power; does not authorize entry to EC-1; may not be represented as recognition or certification.

### RQ-4 — Qualification Complete
- **Entry Requirements:** Qualified (RQ-3) with a complete, audited, traceable qualification package ready for downstream validation dependencies.
- **Exit Requirements:** None within this framework — RQ-4 is terminal for qualification; any further progression belongs to the separate validation framework and remains external.
- **Restrictions:** Completion of qualification does **not** initiate validation, recognition, certification, or EC-1; all downstream steps remain out of scope and externally gated.

## PART 11 — DETERMINATION

**A. Can qualification be objectively defined?**
**YES.** Qualification reduces to a fixed set of evidence-backed criteria (Part 5) with explicit acceptance thresholds, disqualifying conditions (Part 6), evidence requirements (Part 7), and ordered readiness states (Part 8). Each element is decidable on verifiable, attributable evidence without discretionary judgment (EQP-002).

**B. Can qualification be independently assessed?**
**YES.** Every mandatory criterion requires actor-independent evidence (EQC-M-04), all evidence must be verifiable, attributable, and auditable (Part 7), and assessments are re-examinable and reversible (EQP-009). Independence of assessment is a governing principle (EQP-004), and circular self-qualification is a disqualifier (EDQ-004).

**C. Can qualification create authority?**
**NO.** Qualification is non-constitutive by principle (EQP-005). Even full satisfaction of all criteria confers no constituent, governance, ratification, or execution authority; the readiness states explicitly restrict any such reading (Part 8), and authority can arise only from a legitimate exogenous constituent act this program neither performs nor authorizes.

**D. Can qualification authorize EC-1?**
**NO.** EC-1 authorization is outside the charter scope (EES-001 OUT OF SCOPE) and beyond qualification's purpose. Qualification is at most a *precondition* for entering the pathway; it does not trigger, permit, or authorize EC-1, which remains gated behind validation, certification, recognition, and — ultimately — a legitimate exogenous constituent act.
