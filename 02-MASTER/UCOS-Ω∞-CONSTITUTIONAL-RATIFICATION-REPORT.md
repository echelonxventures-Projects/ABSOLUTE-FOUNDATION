## SECTION 1 — CONSTITUTIONAL CONFLICTS (enumerated)

### CONF-01 — Root axiom: BEING vs the EXISTENCE/RELATIONSHIP/TRANSFORMATION triad
- **Evidence.** (a) SRC-01/02/03/05/06 declare an Absolute Axiom "BEING" above EXISTENCE ("Nothing exists outside Being. Nothing supersedes Being."). (b) SRC-07 states the triad EXISTENCE→RELATIONSHIP→TRANSFORMATION is irreducible and that "no higher foundational layer exists above them."
- **Source traceability.** DUP-01; SUP-01; ONT-01; LAW-AX01 vs LAW-AX04. Sources: SRC-01, SRC-02, SRC-03, SRC-05, SRC-06 (BEING) vs SRC-07 (triad-top).
- **Competing interpretations.**
  1. *BEING is the supreme axiom* — EXISTENCE is the first manifestation of BEING; the triad sits beneath BEING.
  2. *EXISTENCE is the root* — BEING is either a synonym for EXISTENCE or a non-layer; the ontology bottoms out at the triad.
  3. *Reconcilable framing* — SRC-07 omits BEING as a **layer** without denying it as an **axiom** (SUP-01 reads this as "under-specification, not repudiation").
- **Consequences of each choice.**
  - Choosing (1): all derivation trees and the "Absolute Master Flow" keep BEING at the apex; SRC-07 must be annotated as under-specified. Widest source agreement (5 of 6).
  - Choosing (2): every BEING-rooted flow (SRC-01/02/03/05/06) and LAW Ω∞-001 "Everything Is Derived From Being" require re-grounding to EXISTENCE; high blast radius across the canon.
  - Choosing (3): no source is repudiated, but the constitution must state explicitly whether BEING is axiom-only (non-layer) or layer — ambiguity persists until worded.

### CONF-02 — Root ontology arity (3 vs 4 vs 5 primitives)
- **Evidence.** 5-primitive with SPACE-TIME in the chain (SRC-01/09/10); 4-primitive BEING→EXISTENCE→RELATIONSHIP→TRANSFORMATION (SRC-02/03/05/06); 3-primitive triad (SRC-07).
- **Source traceability.** ONT-05; SUP-07; matrix §2 row "Root Ontology" (⚠5 / ●4 / ⚠3); DUP-02.
- **Competing interpretations.** (1) SPACE-TIME is a root primitive; (2) SPACE-TIME is a coordinate, not a primitive; (3) triad-only (SPACE-TIME and BEING both excluded from the root).
- **Consequences of each choice.**
  - (1): Ω-LAW-02 "Every Existence Occupies Space-Time" remains a root law; timeless/abstract existences (which SRC-03 explicitly admits via TIME=Timeless) become constitutionally awkward.
  - (2): aligns with SRC-02/03 "Universal Coordinate Framework"; Ω-LAW-02 is demoted; SRC-01/09/10 chains must be re-drawn.
  - (3): smallest root; forces both BEING and SPACE-TIME into derived layers; maximal restatement across sources.

### CONF-03 — Invariant set identity (which 10 invariants are canonical)
- **Evidence.** Three distinct 10-element sets all titled "invariants": LAW-INV01 ("X Before Y", SRC-03/06/07), LAW-INV02 ("Eternal Invariants" Ω-001..010, SRC-02 BOOK X, **declared immutable/non-amendable**), LAW-INV03 (noun form "Identity Integrity…", SRC-02 DOC-0046).
- **Source traceability.** DUP-06; SUP-06; LAW-INV01/02/03.
- **Competing interpretations.** (1) LAW-INV02 governs by its self-declared immutability; (2) LAW-INV01 governs by breadth of agreement (3 sources) and shared membership; (3) all three are different *kinds* (ordering constraints vs integrity properties) and coexist at different layers.
- **Consequences of each choice.**
  - (1): "Constitution Before Governance / Sovereignty Before Federation / Audit Before Trust / Ratification Before Structural Change" become supreme; but an immutable set that was never ratified fixes content that the other two sets contradict in ordering.
  - (2): the most widely shared list wins, but overrides a set that calls itself immutable — a direct challenge to LAW-INV02's own claim.
  - (3): no set is discarded, but the constitution must define layering (ordering-invariants vs property-invariants), which is new structural wording a ratifier must author (out of scope here).

### CONF-04 — Sovereignty origin (sovereignty-as-source vs principles-as-origin)
- **Evidence.** AUTH-02/03: "Authority derives from sovereignty" (SRC-02 BOOK II, SRC-03, SRC-07). AUTH-12/GOV-10: "Sovereignty Origin = Invariant Principles," authority flow "Principles > Constitutions > Governance > … > Executions, never the reverse" (SRC-08, phrased "I would formalize").
- **Source traceability.** DUP-08; SUP-05; AUTH-02, AUTH-03, AUTH-12, GOV-10.
- **Competing interpretations.** (1) Sovereignty is the origin of legitimate authority (majority, frozen sources); (2) Invariant Principles are the origin and sovereignty is derived from them (SRC-08, advisory); (3) two-level model: principles constrain sovereignty, sovereignty sources authority (a synthesis a ratifier could author).
- **Consequences of each choice.**
  - (1): AUTH-08 downward authority flow (EXISTENCE→…→SOVEREIGNTY→INVARIANTS→META-CONSTITUTION→GOVERNANCE) stands; SRC-08 stack is filed as advisory only.
  - (2): elevates a document that self-labels as a recommendation to constitutional force; inverts the sovereignty/invariants ordering in AUTH-08.
  - (3): preserves both, but requires new constitutional text defining the principle→sovereignty relationship — not producible in this phase.

### CONF-05 — Evolution gate: "governance" vs "ratification"
- **Evidence.** LAW Ω∞-018 "Evolution Requires Governance" (SRC-02) vs LAW-013 "Evolution Requires Ratification" (SRC-08); INVARIANT Ω-010 "Ratification Before Structural Change" (SRC-02 BOOK X); BOOK IX Art. IX-6 "Evolution requires ratification."
- **Source traceability.** GOV-07; LAW-R04 (Ω∞-018), LAW-R06 (LAW-013), LAW-INV02 (Ω-010).
- **Competing interpretations.** (1) Governance approval suffices for evolution; (2) formal ratification is required for structural change; (3) tiered: routine evolution needs governance, structural evolution needs ratification (supported by the coexistence of Ω∞-018 and Ω-010 within SRC-02 itself).
- **Consequences of each choice.**
  - (1): lighter change process; risks structural drift without ratification.
  - (2): heavier process; every evolution blocked on a ratification body that is currently undefined (see RAT-11).
  - (3): internally consistent with SRC-02, but requires a definition of "structural" vs "routine" that the sources do not crisply provide.

### CONF-06 — Space & Time as root law (Ω-LAW-02) vs coordinate framework
- **Evidence.** Ω-LAW-02 "Every Existence Occupies Space-Time" (SRC-01/09/10) vs SRC-02/03 treating SPACE and TIME as two of five coordinate axes (ONT-06/07) with TIME including "Timeless / Infinite."
- **Source traceability.** DUP-02; SUP-02; LAW-R01 (Ω-LAW-02); ONT-05/06/07. (Related to CONF-02 but distinct: this is the *law-level* obligation, CONF-02 is the *ontology-arity* question.)
- **Competing interpretations.** (1) Occupancy of space-time is a universal law binding all existences; (2) space-time is an optional coordinate; timeless/abstract existences are valid and need not "occupy" space-time.
- **Consequences of each choice.**
  - (1): abstract/timeless/hypothetical existences (explicitly admitted by SRC-01 Article Ω-2 and SRC-03 TIME=Timeless) conflict with a law that forces space-time occupancy — internal tension.
  - (2): Ω-LAW-02 is retired as a law; SRC-01/09/10 lose one of their ten "Absolute Laws," renumbering their set.

### CONF-07 — Document supremacy (two "supreme" constitutions)
- **Evidence.** SRC-01 Ratification: "This Constitution is the supreme governing authority of UCOS Ω∞." SRC-02: "supreme governing authority… Nothing supersedes the Constitution." Both are self-declarations.
- **Source traceability.** DUP-07; SUP-14 (**UNRESOLVED**); AUTH-11.
- **Competing interpretations.** (1) SRC-02 is senior (81-doc superset, frozen, contains SRC-06); (2) SRC-01 is senior (declares the DOC-01..07 lineage and predates the canon as the "Absolute Constitution"); (3) they are the same constitutional intent expressed at different completeness levels and neither supersedes — requiring an explicit ordering clause.
- **Consequences of each choice.**
  - (1): matrix §4 working precedence becomes ratified; SRC-01's supremacy clause is demoted.
  - (2): the entire matrix §4 precedence (which places SRC-02 first) must be re-ordered; SRC-02's "nothing supersedes" clause is demoted.
  - (3): both supremacy clauses must be rewritten to cross-reference each other — new constitutional text, out of scope; the conflict remains formally open.
- **Status.** This is the **keystone conflict**: because it decides which document's rules win, every other consequence that names an "authoritative source" is provisional until CONF-07 is ratified.

### CONF-08 — Authority-stack model (flat downward flow vs layered meta-governance)
- **Evidence.** AUTH-08 downward flow EXISTENCE→RELATIONSHIP→AUTHORITY→SOVEREIGNTY→INVARIANTS→META-CONSTITUTION→GOVERNANCE→EXECUTION (SRC-03/07) vs GOV-10 "Principle-Centric Adaptive Meta-Governance" seven-layer stack (Sovereignty Origin→Invariant Principles→Meta-Constitution→Governance Generation→Polycentric→Federated Domain→Autonomous Execution) (SRC-08, advisory).
- **Source traceability.** AUTH-08; GOV-10; AUTHORITY-REGISTER Part C.
- **Competing interpretations.** (1) The frozen downward-flow model is canonical; (2) the SRC-08 layered model supersedes it; (3) the SRC-08 model is an implementation refinement subordinate to the flow model.
- **Consequences of each choice.**
  - (1): SRC-08 stack remains advisory; polycentric/federated layers are not constitutional.
  - (2): introduces "Governance Generation," "Polycentric," "Federated Domain" as constitutional layers sourced from an advisory document.
  - (3): SRC-08 stack is retained as a non-binding elaboration; no constitutional change.

## SECTION 4 — LAW IDENTIFIER COLLISIONS (enumerated)

### LIDC-01 — `LAW Ω∞-0xx` denotes two different law-sets
- **Evidence.** LAW-R04 (SRC-02/06): `LAW Ω∞-001` = "Everything Is Derived From Being," 20 laws. LAW-R05 (SRC-07): `LAW Ω∞-001` = "Everything Is An Existence," 15 laws. Same IDs, different texts and different set sizes.
- **Traceability.** DUP-03; SUP-03; LAW-R04 vs LAW-R05.
- **Competing interpretations.** (1) SRC-02's 20-law set owns the `LAW Ω∞` namespace; SRC-07's set is renumbered; (2) SRC-07's 15-law set is the "universal laws" and SRC-02's is renamed (e.g., "Absolute Constitutional Laws"); (3) both retain the prefix but are namespaced (e.g., `Ω∞-A-0xx` vs `Ω∞-U-0xx`).
- **Consequences.** (1)/(2): one source's citations across the corpus must be rewritten; (3): all existing `LAW Ω∞-0xx` citations become ambiguous until re-tagged. Any choice requires a citation-rewrite pass; none can be done without first settling CONF-07 (whose numbering is senior).

### LIDC-02 — Same law under four ID schemes
- **Evidence.** "Authority precedes action" appears as `Ω-LAW-06` (SRC-01/09/10), `LAW Ω∞-005` (SRC-02), `LAW Ω∞-005` (SRC-07, but that set is different — see LIDC-01), `LAW-005` (SRC-08). Analogous multi-ID mapping for Registry-Driven, Governance-Before-Execution, Discovery≠Execution, Evolvable/Auditable.
- **Traceability.** DUP-04; SUP-04; LAW-R01/R04/R05/R06.
- **Competing interpretations.** (1) Adopt one canonical scheme (`LAW Ω∞` per SRC-02) and map the others to it; (2) keep per-document schemes with a concordance table; (3) mint a fresh scheme (constitutes new content — out of scope).
- **Consequences.** (1): single citation space, but the other three schemes' documents read as "legacy IDs." (2): no rewrite, but every reader needs the concordance to know two IDs are one law. (3): excluded by mandate (no new constitutional content).

### LIDC-03 — `LAW Ω∞-005` cross-set semantic clash
- **Evidence.** In LAW-R04 (SRC-02) `LAW Ω∞-005` = "Authority Before Change"; in LAW-R05 (SRC-07) `LAW Ω∞-005` = "Authority Precedes Action." Near-synonymous here, but the *sets* diverge at other indices (e.g., `-006` = "Evidence Before Truth Claims" in SRC-02 vs "Governance Precedes Execution" in SRC-07), so identical IDs carry different meaning across the two sets.
- **Traceability.** DUP-03; LAW-R04 vs LAW-R05 (index-by-index divergence).
- **Competing interpretations.** (1) Treat coincidentally-aligned indices as equivalent and only fix the divergent ones; (2) treat the entire two sets as disjoint namespaces regardless of coincidental alignment.
- **Consequences.** (1): risk of silent misreference where indices happen to align but sets differ; (2): safest but forces full renumber of one set (folds into LIDC-01).

### LIDC-04 — Domain law numbering reuse (`LAW 01..07`) and family-prefix space
- **Evidence.** SRC-04 commerce laws `LAW 01..07` reuse a bare `LAW NN` form that overlaps visually with SRC-02's per-family `LAW <PREFIX>-0NN` catalog (~65 families: R, C, H, RK, CM, …). No cross-family ID clash detected *within* SRC-02, but the bare `LAW 01` (SRC-04) and e.g. `LAW O-001`/`LAW A-001` share the token "LAW".
- **Traceability.** DUP-05; SUP-04; LAW-R08 (SRC-04); LAW-REGISTER Part D family index.
- **Competing interpretations.** (1) Commerce laws are a DOMAIN namespace (`LAW-COMM-0NN`) subordinate to universal law — benign specialization; (2) leave as-is (bare `LAW NN`), accepting that "LAW 02" is ambiguous between commerce and any unprefixed reference.
- **Consequences.** (1): clean separation, requires re-tagging commerce IDs; (2): low effort, but ambiguity persists in any unqualified "LAW 0N" citation. No conflict *of content* — this is a namespacing hygiene decision.
