| CONSTITUTIONAL BASIS | UCKP-ART-19 (lossless assimilation) and UCKP-ART-11 (documents are generated views of an object — here, of rows the corpus already states). CMG-000001 VIII.1 Knowledge Once: no row is worded, reordered or renumbered by this document. |

> **WHY THIS EXISTS.** `00-MASTER/UAKOS-PHASE-001A-R1/01-AUTHORITATIVE-ORIGIN-REGISTER.md` states each of these forty-two concepts with a verified authoritative origin (verification PASS on every row) — and that register lives in operational memory, which this repository's own eligibility configuration excludes from canonical-home standing. The result, measured by `platform.universal_ownership.cli homing`: forty-two subjects carried a stated origin and no declared home, and therefore no owner. A concept whose definition exists only where definitions cannot live is exactly the unanswerable question UCKP-ART-06 forbids. This register closes that gap the same way `UCOS-ABSOLUTE-CONSTITUTIONAL-LAWS-REGISTER.md` and `02-MASTER/EC-3-BAND-REALIZATION-UNIT-REGISTER.md` closed theirs: by installing the definitional home in an eligible zone and transcribing the already-stated knowledge into it. Nothing is authored here; every row below is carried verbatim from the origin named beside it.
> Reproduce the measurement: `CLOSURE_SKIP_CORPUS=1 python3 00-MASTER/UAKOS-CLOSURE-002/closure_engine.py` then `python3 -m platform.universal_ownership.cli homing`.
