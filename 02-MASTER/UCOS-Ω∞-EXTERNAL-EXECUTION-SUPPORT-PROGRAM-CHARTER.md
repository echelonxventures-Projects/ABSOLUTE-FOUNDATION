## MISSION

The Constitutional Consolidation Program and the Constituent Authority Program Series have concluded.

The purpose of the External Execution Support Program is to support future external actors who may seek to establish, validate, recognize, certify, and operationalize a legitimate constituent authority consistent with the determinations of the UCOS Ω∞ constitutional corpus.

This program is supportive only.

This program possesses no constituent authority.

This program possesses no ratification authority.

This program possesses no governance authority.

This program cannot authorize EC-1.

---

## PURPOSE

Provide a controlled framework for:

* Evidence collection
* Evidence organization
* Recognition assessment
* Qualification assessment
* Validation preparation
* Certification preparation
* External stakeholder support

without performing any constituent act.

---

## BACKGROUND

The Constitutional Consolidation Program determined:

* Constituted authority exists within the corpus.
* Constituent authority does not exist within the corpus.
* Constituent authority cannot be derived from the corpus.
* External constituent action is required.

The Constituent Authority Program Series determined:

* Establishment is externally executable.
* Validation is objectively definable.
* Certification requirements are identifiable.
* Internal readiness is complete.
* Remaining dependencies are external.
