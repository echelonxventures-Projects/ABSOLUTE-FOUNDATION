## 1. PROGRAM OVERVIEW

The UCOS Ω∞ Constitutional Consolidation Program reconciled a frozen, heterogeneous corpus of 13 founding documents into a single, traceable, decision-ready basis — **without** authoring new constitutional content, merging documents, or enacting anything.

Across nine phases the program: froze and hash-fixed the corpus (Phase 0); reconciled all content into registers (Phase 1); enumerated every conflict (Phase 2); adjudicated each to a position drawn from existing sources (Phase 3); registered the decisions (Phase 4); diagnosed the governance gaps (Phase 5); scoped the minimal remediation (Phase 6); determined the constituent-authority requirement (Phase 7); certified readiness (Phase 8); and formally closed the program (Phase 9). A plain-language stakeholder handoff brief was then produced.

**Program outcome:** Analytically complete. All substantive constitutional decisions are made, consistent, and registered. Finalization is blocked by a single, documented, out-of-corpus requirement — a legitimate exogenous constituent act — which this program cannot and does not perform.

**Closure status:** CLOSED WITH CONDITIONS. **Readiness:** CONDITIONALLY READY FOR SYNTHESIS.
