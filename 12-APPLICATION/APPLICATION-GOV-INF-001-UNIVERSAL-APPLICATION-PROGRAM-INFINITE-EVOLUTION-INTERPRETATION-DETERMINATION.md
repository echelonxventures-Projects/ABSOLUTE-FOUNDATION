## SECTION 0 — EVIDENCE DISCIPLINE

Per the mission, every finding is classified as one of:

- **[DIRECT EVIDENCE]** — a verbatim or near-verbatim citation from a physically-existing authority.
- **[INTERPRETATION]** — the reading of that evidence under AUTH-INF-001's binding reading rules (Part V).
- **[DERIVED DETERMINATION]** — the conclusion that follows from evidence + interpretation.

No conclusion is drawn where evidence is absent. Where the authorities are silent on a mechanic (e.g. the concrete act of registering `APPLICATION-019`), the interpretation states the *permission* only and defers the *mechanic* to REG-AUTO-001 / UCI-001, exactly as AUTH-INF-001 CR-INF-009.3 requires.

**[DIRECT EVIDENCE]**
- APPLICATION-GOV-999 §5: *"Certification determination: CERTIFIED (DOMAIN-D architecture certification)."*
- APPLICATION-GOV-999 preamble: *"Certification is a DOMAIN-D evaluative judgment over physically existing artifacts; it asserts no operational, UI, screen, deployment, or production certification of any running application."*

Under CR-INF-011, that judgment attests integrity/consistency/completeness-**of-current-scope**/readiness of exactly that set, and asserts nothing about maximum scope or the exhaustion of the domain.

**[DERIVED DETERMINATION]** **APPLICATION-GOV-999 certifies the completed, currently-authorized APPLICATION-001…018 roadmap scope (Option B) — NOT the entire Application Domain (Option A).** It is a roadmap-scope architecture certification, not a domain-terminality certification.

## SECTION 2 — IS AF-3 A ROADMAP BOUNDARY OR A DOMAIN BOUNDARY? (Determination 2)

**[DIRECT EVIDENCE]**
- APPLICATION-018 (AF-3 closure): *"the program is closed as AF-3 = {APPLICATION-001…018} REGISTERED. No roadmap artifact remains. Future application concerns are admitted additively without renumbering (UCI-001)."*

## SECTION 3 — DOES APPLICATION-GOV-999 CREATE A TERMINAL STATE? (Determination 3)

**[DIRECT EVIDENCE]**
- APPLICATION-GOV-999 PROGRAM POSITION uses the word *"Terminal certification envelope"*; Title/§8 use *"FINAL"*; §1/§8 use *"CLOSED / CLOSURE"*.
- APPLICATION-GOV-EVOL-001 §3 binding gloss: "Terminal" = *"last-in-sequence position … not a terminal, non-evolvable, or end-state condition of the domain"*; "FINAL" = *"conclusive for the current certification pass … not 'last-ever'"*; "CLOSED/CLOSURE" = *"roadmap/scope closure … not permanent closure, not domain closure, and not closure of evolution."*
- APPLICATION-GOV-EVOL-001 §4: *"APPLICATION-GOV-999 PASSES the constitutional test. It closes scope, not evolution."*
- AUTH-INF-001 CR-INF-001.3: *"'Final', 'complete', 'closed', 'terminal', and equivalent tokens … SHALL be read as complete for current scope, never as incapable of future extension."*

**[INTERPRETATION]** The prohibited-looking words in GOV-999 are lexical, not substantive. GOV-EVOL-001 §2 already found (across 8 questions) that GOV-999 imposes "NO terminal state, NO finite ceiling, and NO prohibition on evolution." AUTH-INF-001 CR-INF-001 makes the non-terminal reading of those tokens repository-wide law.

**[DERIVED DETERMINATION]** **APPLICATION-GOV-999 does NOT create a terminal state.** No clause substantively imposes terminality, finality, or permanent closure; the resemblant tokens are read as scope/sequence-conclusive, per binding law.
