## DELIVERABLE 1 — EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The Engineering Program (ENG) is the branch of UCOS Ω∞ that establishes the **permanent, implementation-independent engineering foundation** of the system. ENG-000 is its root governing document: the canonical **Master Index & Execution Constitution** that organizes, governs, and manages every engineering artifact.

ENG-000 rests on a small set of durable commitments:

1. **One root of engineering authority.** Every ENG artifact derives its authority, numbering, dependency rules, traceability, lifecycle, and freeze discipline from ENG-000. There is exactly one engineering-program constitution.
2. **A stable layer model + open roadmap.** Engineering artifacts are organized into permanent **Engineering Layers** (governance → existence primitives → structure & semantics → state & behavior → knowledge & data → assurance & control → persistence & evolution → realization bridge). The roadmap within those layers is **open and additive** — new artifacts append; none is ever renumbered.
3. **Sequential, non-reusable numbering.** ENG artifacts are numbered `ENG-NNN` sequentially and permanently; numbers are never reused, renumbered, or reassigned (mirroring the ENG-001 identity discipline).
4. **A ten-state lifecycle with freeze baselines.** Every artifact moves Draft → Review → Validated → Approved → Frozen → Released → Deprecated → Superseded → Archived → Retired, under change control, quality gates, and freeze baselines.
5. **Acyclic dependency governance.** The engineering dependency graph is acyclic with clear layer boundaries; an artifact depends only on lower or equal, already-established layers — never on unbuilt or higher layers.
6. **Traceability by construction.** Every ENG artifact is a registered, identified Object (ENG-002) with a Universal Identity (ENG-001), traceable to its parents, dependencies, layer, and the determinations it honors.
7. **Cross-program integration without modification.** ENG integrates with ARCH/CAT/REF/GEN/IMP through **traceability and dependency relationships only** — it modifies no canonical identity, numbering, or determination of any prior program.
8. **Pure governance; authority-neutral.** ENG-000 produces no code, no APIs, no schemas, no technology choices; every artifact it governs is technical and non-constitutive (ID-01, AUTH-06), and it creates no constituent/governance/ratification/EC-series authority.

The result is a governing framework under which **every future engineering document is registered, traceable, versioned, governed, reviewed, frozen, and managed** — permanently, at unlimited scale, without redesign, and in full compatibility with all existing UCOS programs.

## DELIVERABLE 4 — ENGINEERING PHILOSOPHY

The Engineering Program is guided by a disciplined philosophy (engineering stance only; it touches no constitutional primitive — RAT-01/02/03 honored):

- **Foundations before features.** Nothing is built until the engineering foundation it depends on is defined, validated, and (where required) frozen.
- **Independence before realization.** Engineering meaning is fixed implementation-independently first; technology is chosen only later, by downstream programs, and may be swapped without disturbing the engineering foundation.
- **Reuse over redefinition.** Each artifact reuses the primitives defined before it and never duplicates, replaces, modifies, or redefines them (the ENG-001→ENG-002 discipline, generalized).
- **Extension over redesign.** Growth is additive; the model never has to be torn down to admit something new.
- **Traceability over assertion.** Every artifact, dependency, and decision is recorded and traceable; nothing is true merely because it is asserted.
- **Governance over improvisation.** Lifecycle, change, quality, and freeze are governed processes, not ad-hoc acts.
- **Permanence over expedience.** The program optimizes for a foundation valid for the lifetime of UCOS, not for short-term convenience.
- **Non-constitutive engineering.** Engineering artifacts are technical; they never claim or confer constitutional/sovereign/governance/constituent standing (ID-01, AUTH-06).

## DELIVERABLE 10 — ENGINEERING LAYER MODEL

Engineering artifacts occupy **permanent horizontal layers (EL-0…EL-7)**. Layers are stable for the lifetime of UCOS; artifacts are added *within* layers by extension. A layer number is program metadata — it does **not** appear in or constrain the sequential ENG-NNN identifier (Deliverable 22).

### 10.1 The layer stack (lowest → highest)

| Layer | Name | Purpose | Depends only on |
|-------|------|---------|-----------------|
| **EL-0** | **Program Governance** | Governs and indexes the whole program. | — (root) |
| **EL-1** | **Existence Primitives** | What it is to exist and be referenced: identity, object, relationship/reference, type. | EL-0 |
| **EL-2** | **Structure & Semantics** | How objects are structured and mean: composition/hierarchy, metadata/attribute, classification/taxonomy, semantics/vocabulary, naming/addressing, namespace/federation. | EL-0…EL-1 |
| **EL-3** | **State & Behavior** | How objects change and act: state/lifecycle, behavior/capability, event/signal, process/workflow, interaction/interface. | EL-0…EL-2 |
| **EL-4** | **Knowledge & Data** | How objects carry knowledge/data/context: data, knowledge/ontology, time/temporal, measurement/unit, localization/i18n. | EL-0…EL-3 |
| **EL-5** | **Assurance & Control** | How objects are trusted/governed: security, trust, policy/governance, validation, integrity, audit/provenance, certification, observability. | EL-0…EL-4 |
| **EL-6** | **Persistence & Evolution** | How objects endure and change over time: registry, versioning/evolution, configuration, extension/plugin, recovery/continuity. | EL-0…EL-5 |
| **EL-7** | **Realization Bridge** | Implementation-independent handoff to downstream programs: generation, runtime, agent/autonomy, simulation/digital-twin, cross-program integration. | EL-0…EL-6 |

### 10.2 Layer rules

- **Downward-only dependencies (ENG-L-05/06):** an artifact depends only on artifacts in lower or equal, already-established layers. Upward or cross-cyclic dependencies are prohibited.
- **Permanent set, additive members:** the layer *set* (EL-0…EL-7) is stable; new *artifacts* are added within layers; a new *layer* may be appended only by governed, additive program evolution (Deliverable 28) and never inserted so as to renumber existing layers.
- **One primary layer per artifact (ENG-L-06):** an artifact may reference higher layers only as *downstream consumers* (informational), never as dependencies.

## DELIVERABLE 15 — LIFECYCLE MODEL

Every engineering artifact follows one **ten-state lifecycle**. Transitions are governed, authorized, versioned, and audited (ENG-L-07). The identifier is allocated at first registration and is never released (ENG-001 reuse).

| # | State | Meaning | Entry gate |
|---|-------|---------|-----------|
| 1 | **Draft** | Authoring in progress; registered with ID reserved. | Registered under ENG-000 (Deliverable 20). |
| 2 | **Review** | Under formal review against quality criteria. | Draft complete; reviewers assigned. |
| 3 | **Validated** | Passed content/consistency/dependency/traceability validation. | Review complete; quality gates Q1–Q4 passed (Deliverable 17). |
| 4 | **Approved** | Accepted for the program; eligible for freeze. | Validated; dependencies at least Validated; acceptance recorded. |
| 5 | **Frozen** | Content fixed at a baseline; changes only via controlled change management. | Approved; assigned to a freeze baseline (Deliverable 26). |
| 6 | **Released** | Published as part of a released baseline. | Frozen; release recorded (ENG-Rn). |
| 7 | **Deprecated** | Discouraged for new dependence; still valid; successor may exist. | Governed deprecation decision. |
| 8 | **Superseded** | Replaced by a new artifact (linked by SUPERSEDED-BY; new ID). | Successor Approved/Released. |
| 9 | **Archived** | Preserved for record/recovery. | Governed archival decision. |
| 10 | **Retired** | No longer active; **identifier and record remain permanently valid** (never reused). | Governed retirement decision. |

**Lifecycle rules:** transitions are one-directional along the governed path except explicitly reversible ones (e.g., Review→Draft on rejection; Deprecated→Approved on reinstatement) which preserve full history (ENG-001 append-only reuse). ENG-001 and ENG-002 are currently **ACTIVE** (Approved/operational); they advance to **Frozen** at baseline **ENG-BL-1**. Retirement never releases an ENG identifier (ENG-L-03).

## DELIVERABLE 16 — CHANGE MANAGEMENT

All changes to registered artifacts are classified, impact-analyzed, reviewed, and approved. No change bypasses this model (ENG-L-13).

### 16.1 Change classes

| Class | Definition | Requirement |
|-------|-----------|-------------|
| **Minor** | Editorial/clarifying; no semantic change. | Reviewer approval; version patch increment (Deliverable 22.3). |
| **Major** | Semantic addition/refinement that is backward-compatible. | Full review + impact analysis; version minor increment. |
| **Breaking** | A change that would alter/invalidate existing dependents or determinations. | Full review + cross-program impact assessment + **supersession** (new artifact/version), never in-place mutation of frozen semantics; version major increment. |
| **Controlled** | A change to a **Frozen** artifact. | Freeze-exception process (Deliverable 26.3); Freeze Authority approval; new frozen version. |
| **Emergency** | An urgent correction (e.g., a discovered integrity/secret defect). | Expedited review; retrospective full impact analysis; audit-logged; still no canon modification (ENG-L-14). |

### 16.2 Change workflow

1. **Raise** a change request (identified object; ENG-002).
2. **Classify** (minor/major/breaking/controlled/emergency).
3. **Impact analysis** — traverse dependents (Deliverable 19) and cross-program traceability (Deliverable 27) to compute blast radius.
4. **Review & approve** per class (Deliverable 25).
5. **Apply** as an append-only new version (never erasing history; ENG-001 reuse).
6. **Re-baseline / re-freeze** if the artifact was Frozen (Deliverable 26).
7. **Record** the change, its evidence, and its cross-program impact (audit).

### 16.3 Change invariants

- **Backward compatibility preferred:** breaking changes require supersession, not silent redefinition (ENG-L-04/10).
- **No canon modification (ENG-L-14/15):** no change may alter another program's identity, numbering, or determination.
- **Cross-program impact assessed:** any change touching an EL-7 traceability edge triggers a cross-program impact assessment (Deliverable 27) — assessment only, never modification.
