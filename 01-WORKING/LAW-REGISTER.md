# UCOS Ω∞ LAW REGISTER

Phase 1 — Constitutional Reconciliation. Every law extracted from `00-SOURCE/` with traceability.
Source identifiers (SRC-01..SRC-13) are defined in `CONSOLIDATION-MATRIX.md § 1`.
No law is invented, merged, or rewritten here — laws are recorded verbatim (as extracted) and grouped for reconciliation.

Scale note: the corpus contains ~1,268 discrete law/invariant/axiom/article statements. These fall into (A) a small set of **recurring foundational law-sets** that appear across multiple documents and require reconciliation — transcribed in full below; and (B) **architectural / phase law families** that are largely single-source enumerations (~65 families in SRC-02, plus phase families) — catalogued by family with full traceability so every law is accounted for.
