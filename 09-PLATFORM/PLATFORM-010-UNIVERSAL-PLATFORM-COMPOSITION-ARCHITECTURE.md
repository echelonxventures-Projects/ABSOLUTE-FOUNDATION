## SECTION 3 — COMPOSITION DEFINITION

> **Composition** is a **well-founded, typed combination** of capabilities, components, and/or services into a higher-order platform construct. A composition is an ENG-002 Object bearing an ENG-001 Identity, classified by an ENG-004 Type, related via ENG-005 (POR-04), whose coordination behavior is a RUNTIME construct referenced through POR-08 (POB-04 composition-coordination via RUNTIME orchestration). A composition references (does not absorb) its members' identities; it is the **structure of combination**, not the members themselves.

## SECTION 7 — WELL-FOUNDEDNESS MODEL

A composition is **WELL-FOUNDED** iff: (W1) the founding graph over POR-04 is a **DAG** (no cycle); (W2) every edge connects allowed meta-classes; (W3) each member is typed and identified; (W4) the composite is closed (its result is an allowed meta-class, PCO-06); (W5) no member composes an ancestor (no transitive self-composition). Well-foundedness is **decidable** from records (PCO-09; PTH-12); a violation is a Gap Report (UPL-10). This is the architectural guarantee that platform structures terminate and are reducible to foundation constructs.
