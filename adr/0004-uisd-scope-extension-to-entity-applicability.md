## Context

UISD-000001 declares infinite expansion capacity over ten named subject kinds (object, capability, universe, registry, dictionary, relationship, lifecycle, constitution, engine, technology). The principle as stated by the authority extends to every entity — including APIs, UI, UX, frontend, backend, infrastructure, platforms and future unknown constructs. Measured, ISD-L-07 scans six roots covering 1,892 of 5,989 eligible files (31.6%); the delivery layers are unmeasured.

## Decision

We extend the **subject** of the UISD principle statement to Entity-based applicability, and widen `freeze_scan.roots` to the delivery layers. UISD's `standing` remains **`PERVASIVE PROPERTY, NOT AUTHORITY LAYER`**, unchanged. Reading it as an authority would install a rival supreme instrument over CMG-000001 and UCIC-001, refused under CAA-INV-04, CAA-INV-07 and CMG Art LXXVI.6.

## Consequences

Positive: the property becomes measurable where it is currently only declared. Negative: widening the scan will surface findings in previously unmeasured layers; that is the intent. Reversible: both changes are data edits to one declaration.
