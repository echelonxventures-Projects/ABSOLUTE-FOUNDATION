## Context

The principle requires a continuous cycle through observation, discovery, assimilation, classification, validation, verification, certification, learning, adaptation, evolution and capability expansion, with no architectural replacement. Measured: `EVOLUTION_CYCLE` declares fifteen stages, no stage is terminal, and the last returns to the first (ISD-L-05). ISD-L-04 holds that the stage graph admits a stage without an engine change. UAUE legislates no stage and holds no copy of the vocabulary.

## Decision

We record self-learning and self-evolution as **represented by existing canonical capability**. Any additional stage — `ADAPTATION` among them — enters as a declaration against the UCL stage manifest, never as an engine change.

## Consequences

Positive: no second evolution engine. Neutral: whether `ADAPTATION` should be a named stage is left open; the mechanism to add it exists and is measured.
