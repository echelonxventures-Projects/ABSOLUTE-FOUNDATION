## 5. Key distinction (derived vs canonical)

The **registries and projections in `00-BOOK`** are frequently cited as if canonical because they are tracked and named "registry". Under the repository's own authority mechanism they are **derived** — regenerable from the authoring engines + registration transaction. The **authored engines, constitutions, catalogs, schemas** (see `02`) are the canonical sources they project. Losing a `00-BOOK` projection is recoverable by regeneration; losing a constitution is not.
