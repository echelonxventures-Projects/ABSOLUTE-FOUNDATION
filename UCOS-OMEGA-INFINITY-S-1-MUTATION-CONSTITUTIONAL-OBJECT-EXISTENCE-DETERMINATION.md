| Kind | Definition under the law | May it be an object? | May it hold authority? |
|---|---|---|---|

| **PROJECTION** | a representation of objects into a file, register or view — `ART-04`, `ART-11` | **NO** | **NEVER** |
| **OBSERVATION** | a measurement of state at a moment — a result, a count, a set | **NO** | **NEVER**, and it is not even a durable thing |
