## The equivalence dimensions a mapping must prove

| # | Criterion | The question it answers | Clause | Dimensions covered |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **functional equivalence** | Does a located artifact actually perform the behaviour the decision requires — not merely describe or partially resemble it? | `CEP-002 Art 28.13(b)` | 28/42 |
| 2 | **architectural equivalence** | Is the behaviour held at the architectural level the decision requires, by the single owner that owns that concern? | `CEP-002 Art 28.13(b) · CMG-000001 CMG-INV-02` | 41/42 |
| 3 | **governance equivalence** | Is the behaviour governed — legislated by a located instrument and enforced by a located gate, rather than left to convention? | `CEP-002 Art 28.17–28.21 · CEP-001 VII.2` | 42/42 |
| 4 | **lifecycle equivalence** | Is the behaviour carried through a located lifecycle — admission, validation, certification, amendment — rather than existing as a one-off? | `CEP-002 Art 28.8–28.12 · CEP-009` | 42/42 |
| 5 | **extensibility equivalence** | Can the behaviour absorb a future case as DATA, without redesign and without a code change? | `LAW Ω∞ admission test · CXL-02 Bounded Extension` | 40/42 |

> A dimension is covered only where the decision names at least one located artifact for it and every artifact it names resolves against the repository. A dimension for which nothing is named is not covered. Coverage is therefore measured, never asserted. Scope: decision class `architectural`.
