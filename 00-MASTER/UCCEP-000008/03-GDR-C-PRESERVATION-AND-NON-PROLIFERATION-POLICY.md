### Constitutional Authority

**`GOV-INT-001`** (`CMG-DLG-16`) §2.14 — sole canonical owner for this decision, as the instrument that owns the inheritance mechanism. `CEP-007` (`CMG-DLG-07`) Articles XI and XIII are cited as the **governing route for the supersession branch**, which this decision expressly does not exercise; citing a route is not co-ownership, and `CEP-002` 14.3 is not engaged.

### Decision

**The consolidation binds by REFERENCE, universally, and declares NO artifact "reference-only" as a new status.**

Four terms are fixed:

1. **Binding is by citation.** Every consolidation artifact that relies on a located instrument **cites** it — path plus clause — and does not copy, restate, paraphrase into normative form, or summarize it as operative text. This is `GOV-INT-001` §2.14's mechanism applied without exception.
2. **No new status is created.** "Reference-only" is **not** introduced as an artifact state. The located lifecycle states remain exactly `REG-AUTO-001` §5's (`DRAFT → GENERATED → REGISTERED → ACTIVE → CERTIFIED → FROZEN → ARCHIVED`) and `CEP-007`'s freeze states. A consolidation act SHALL NOT mark any artifact "reference-only", because doing so would add a state to a closed model owned elsewhere.
3. **Scope is total.** Every located instrument the consolidation touches is referenced, not absorbed: the `00-CEP/` constitutions, `00-CMG/CMG-000001`, the four `00-BOOK/CONTROL-TOWER/` standards, the located registers, and every other programme's outputs.
4. **Displacement, if ever required, is supersession — and none is exercised here.** Where a located artifact's operative role would be displaced, the only lawful route is `CEP-007` XIII: a successor is created, lineage is recorded, the predecessor is **not modified**, and it is retained and marked SUPERSEDED (XI.3), remaining immutable, reproducible and discoverable (XIII.3). **This decision exercises no supersession, proposes none, and names no candidate.** Any supersession is an act of `CEP-007`'s owner under its Article XX authorization, not of the consolidation.

### Rationale

The question Output 16 poses — *which* programmes become reference-only — presupposes that reference status is a property conferred on selected artifacts. Repository Truth does not support that reading. `GOV-INT-001` §2.14 describes reference as the property of the **relationship** between a citing programme and a cited Owner, not a state of the cited artifact: *"a program/phase cites UCI-001; it does not copy it."* Nothing in the clause marks `UCI-001` as reference-only; it constrains everyone who inherits from it.

Read correctly, the decision therefore has no selection to make and one binding to record: the consolidation is on the **citing** side of every such relationship, for every located instrument, without exception. That is a universal application rather than a named list, and it is strictly stronger than any list would be — a list would leave unnamed instruments unconstrained.

Declining to bind reference-only would not create latitude; it would create duplicate governance. If a consolidation artifact restated an Owner's rule as its own operative text, the corpus would hold two texts of one rule, which is exactly the *"no duplicate governance"* condition §2.14 exists to satisfy and the parallel instrument `CMG-L-14` prohibits. This is also why clause 1 forbids paraphrase-into-normative-form specifically: a faithful copy and a drifting paraphrase are the same defect, and the paraphrase is the more dangerous.

The supersession branch is included because Output 16 names `CEP-007` as co-governing, and excluded from exercise because exercising it is not this programme's act. Recording the route without walking it is what keeps the decision a recognition rather than a freeze or supersession determination — those are `GD-10`'s subject and are rejected there.

### Constitutional Authority

**`CEP-007`** (`CMG-DLG-07` — freeze operation: baselines, immutability, drift protection, supersession mechanics, version lineage, historical preservation, freeze registry), by Articles I, II, III, IV, V. Resolution terminates at `CMG-000001` XVII.2 Step 1. `CEP-006` / `VAC-01` / `CMG-OQ-02` are cited for the unavailability of ratification.

### Decision

**REJECTED. The consolidation declares NO new freeze and extends freeze to NO further programme, artifact, or surface.**

1. **The freeze surface is exactly what Output 6 §4 records as already declared**, by the owners that declared it — and this decision neither adds to it, subtracts from it, nor re-baselines it. `application/**` remains FROZEN at Band-12 baseline `beff9ed3…`; the frozen `data/**`/`service/**` band surfaces remain frozen; `engine/**`·`platform/**` remain freeze-**gated**, not frozen; `infrastructure/**` U01…U11 remain CERTIFIED-not-frozen with U12's freeze **DEFERRED by its own owner**.
2. **No baseline is declared** by the consolidation. No commit — including the OA-1 anchor `1c6e750e…` and HEAD `9de85ad` — is constituted as a freeze baseline by this decision.
3. **The rejection is on eligibility, not on preference.** No artifact currently satisfies `CEP-007` IV.1, because its ratification limb cannot be satisfied while Tier T1 is vacant. The rejection therefore holds regardless of any view about the desirability of freezing.
4. **Nothing is unfrozen.** Existing freezes stand, immutable under IX.1 and retained under XI.3. This is a refusal to extend, not a withdrawal.

### Rationale

Freeze is the one policy in this family that **cannot** be adopted by recognition, because unlike reference-binding or append-only it is not a constraint on behaviour but an **act** with statutory preconditions. `CEP-007` V.5 states that freeze *"SHALL NOT proceed while any precondition is unsatisfied"*, and V.1's precondition set is conjunctive. Measured against Repository Truth, at least three limbs fail simultaneously:

- **Ratification.** III.2 makes ratification an entry condition to the freeze lifecycle and IV.1 makes it an eligibility condition. Tier T1 is VACANT (`VAC-01`, `located: false`), `CEP-006` names no competent authority, and `UCCEP-000006` records the dependency as an external constituent act with the express finding that *"No programme may self-ratify."* No in-repository act can supply it.
- **Active certification.** IV.1 requires certification active under `CEP-005`. The aggregate verdict is capped at `CERTIFIED-PROVISIONAL` and 31 of 43 recognized artifacts are PROVISIONAL, by the same vacancy.
- **Rooted-and-closed traceability.** V.1 imports `CEP-001` XVIII. `DG-5` measures traceability RED across 1,198/1,198 registered artifacts.

Proceeding anyway would not produce a weak freeze; it would produce a **void** one. IV.4 makes an attempted freeze of an ineligible artifact void, II.4 makes a freeze outside authorization void and prohibited, and IX.5 places the Program in **HALTED** for any act that would mutate a frozen artifact. Recording "the consolidation freezes X" would therefore create a nullity that later work might rely on — the most damaging outcome available.

There is also a positive argument from Repository Truth: the freeze owner has already **deferred** a freeze it was closer to earning. `EC3-B13-U12` is recorded DEFERRED with U01…U11 CERTIFIED. A consolidation programme extending freeze to programmes with weaker standing than U12 would be asserting an authority the actual freeze owner has visibly declined to exercise, contrary to I.3 (single freeze authority) and I.5 (never self-conferred).

Rejection is accordingly the only lawful disposition, and it is a *decided* matter rather than a held one: unlike `GD-08` and `GD-21`, the answer is determinate from located clauses, so LVII.3's holding route does not apply.

**`CEP-007`** (`CMG-DLG-07` — freeze operation, immutability rules, historical preservation), by Articles IX, XI, XIII, XV — sole canonical owner for this decision. `CEP-002` 28.15 / 27.5 / 18.1 / 8.6 and `REG-AUTO-001` L5 / P4 (`CMG-DLG-13`) and `CEP-009` III.3 / XV are cited as the concurrent expressions of the same rule in their own domains.

### Decision

**ADOPTED, by reference, over the entire consolidation scope, in its strongest located form: append-only, forward-only, no deletion, no overwrite, no renumbering, no history rewrite.**

1. **Nothing is deleted.** No artifact, record, entry, disposition, finding, deferral, observation, gap, risk, seal, or evidence reference is deleted or removed by any consolidation act.
2. **Nothing is overwritten in place where it carries a determination.** A changed determination is recorded as a **new** determination that retains the prior (`CEP-002` 28.15). Correction is by append, never by edit.
3. **Nothing is renumbered or reordered.** Identity is permanent (`REG-AUTO-001` P4); no page number, universal ID, decision ID, `GD-nn`, `DEF-nn`, or located identifier is reused, renumbered, or reordered.
4. **History is not rewritten.** No consolidation act performs a history-rewriting or destructive repository operation. Divergence is repaired by re-running registration and appending (`REG-AUTO-001` L5). The rollback anchor `1c6e750e…` remains an ancestor of HEAD.
5. **Displacement is supersession, retaining the predecessor** in an immutable, reproducible, discoverable SUPERSEDED state (`CEP-007` XI.3, XIII.2, XIII.3) — and, per `GD-09-C3`, no supersession is exercised by the consolidation.
6. **Omission counts as deletion.** A matter is not disposed of by being left out: `CMG-000001` XV.7 / `CMG-P-06` prohibit concealing a vacancy, and LII.6 makes an undispositioned finding certification-blocking. Accordingly **every** DDI is carried forward with an explicit outcome, and the two held matters are recorded as first-class entries rather than dropped.

### Rationale

Restatement as the consolidation's own policy is warranted precisely because the located law is **domain-scoped**: `CEP-007` protects frozen artifacts, `CEP-002` 28.15 protects dispositions, `CEP-002` 27.5 protects deferral entries, `REG-AUTO-001` L5 protects the registers and identity, `CEP-009` III.3 protects predecessors under amendment. Each is complete in its own domain, and none of them, individually, covers a consolidation act operating across all five. Binding the union over the consolidation's whole scope closes the seams between them — which is the entire lawful content here, and is a recognition of five existing rules rather than a sixth rule.

Clause 6 is the least obvious and the most useful. The other clauses prevent destruction; clause 6 prevents **quiet loss**, which is the failure mode a consolidation is actually prone to. A programme that resolves nineteen matters and silently omits two has deleted two matters as effectively as if it had removed a file, and `CMG-P-06` and LII.6 both treat that as prohibited rather than merely untidy. This is the constitutional basis for the mission's own validation requirement that **no DDI disappears**, and it is discharged mechanically in `09-TRACEABILITY-VERIFICATION.md` §1.

Clause 4 also fixes the meaning of the rollback anchor: recovery is a checkout of an ancestor commit, never a reconstruction of rewritten history. That is only true while history is append-only, so the anchor's value and this policy stand or fall together.
