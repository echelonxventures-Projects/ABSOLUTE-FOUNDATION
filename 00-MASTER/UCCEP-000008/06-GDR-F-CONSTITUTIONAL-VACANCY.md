### Rationale

There is no version of this matter that a repository artifact can lawfully settle, and the reasons compound rather than merely accumulate.

**The tier is empty of any candidate.** `VAC-01` records `located: false` with evidence: the presupposed Tier 1 Constitution exists only as frozen, expressly **non-normative** `.docx` source material. There is no artifact to recognize, so even the most permissive reading has nothing to work with.

**Filling it is prohibited twice over.** XVII.4 states the procedure *"SHALL NOT skip the tier and SHALL NOT promote a lower instrument into it"* — which disposes of every in-corpus candidate, since all located instruments sit below T1. XVII.8 disposes of self-nomination. Between them, the set of lawful in-repository moves is empty.

**The competence itself is absent, not merely unexercised.** This is the point that distinguishes `GD-21` from `GD-10`. In `GD-10` the freeze authority exists and its preconditions fail; the matter is decidable (rejected) because a located owner and a located test exist. Here `CEP-006` names no competent authority at all, and `UCCEP-000006` classifies the dependency as `ED-1` — external, *not manufacturable*. `UCCEP-F-004` is correspondingly the one blocking finding in the register with **no work package**, because there is no work any programme could be assigned.

**Holding is therefore the required act, and it is an act.** LVII.3 makes recording-and-referring the response to a matter undecidable through vacancy, and XV.7 / `CMG-P-06` make recording mandatory — *"Concealing a vacancy IS PROHIBITED"*, and IX.6, *"A missing superior authority is recorded, not assumed."* The alternative to holding is not resolution; it is either a void promotion or a concealment, both prohibited.

**The cost is recorded rather than minimized.** 31 of 43 recognized artifacts stand PROVISIONAL; aggregate certification is capped at `CERTIFIED-PROVISIONAL`; meta-constitutional readiness is `READY-PROVISIONAL`; and `CMG-000001`'s own state in its own registry is PROVISIONAL. Step (b) above accepts the full consequence for this register: **nothing in it is final.** That is the honest position, and asserting otherwise would breach `CMG-L-12` directly.

**Adjacent questions are not swept in.** `CMG-OQ-01` (competence to ratify `CMG-000001`) and `CMG-OQ-03` (T1M/T2 orthogonality) remain open and are neither resolved nor bundled here. `CMG-OQ-05` (program-completion ceremony ownership) and `CMG-OQ-07` (corpus-wide adoption of `CMG-INV-01…12`) likewise remain their owners'. Conflating them would be exactly the re-scoping `UCCEP-000007` Output 15 §4 declines and this programme equally declines.

**NO `CEP-002` 28.13 DISPOSITION — HELD under `CMG-000001` XVII.4 and LVII.3, and `CEP-002` Article 27.**

Recorded as deferral entry **`DEF-02`**, state `RECORDED`.

Why this is lawful and why it does not close the Implementation Evidence Gate:

- `CEP-002` 28.16 provides that recording a matter in the Deferral Register *"IS **not** a disposition under 28.13"*. A matter so recorded is therefore **not a dispositioned decision** — and equally **not an undispositioned decision**, because it is not a *decision*: no competent authority has agreed it, and here none exists to agree it, so `CEP-002` 28.8's CONSTITUTIONAL AGREEMENT stage is never reached and the lifecycle is never entered.
- `CEP-002` 28.18 closes the Gate while a *"previously ratified or recorded decision IS undispositioned"*. `GD-21` is neither ratified — it could not be, which is the whole matter — nor registered as a decision. `GD-21-C4` and `GD-08-C4`'s analogue forbid registering it, so this remains true.
- `CMG-000001` LVII.3 makes holding the required response to a matter undecidable through vacancy; XV.7 / `CMG-P-06` make recording it mandatory; XVII.4 supplies the procedure discharged above.
