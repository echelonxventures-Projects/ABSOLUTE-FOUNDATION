## PART A — THE UNIVERSAL CONSTITUTIONAL LAW OF INTEGRATION (LAW USIS-00)

**LAW USIS-00 (supreme program law).**
> *Every present and future science, discipline, intelligence paradigm, computational model, learning methodology, psychological framework, cognitive science, behavioral science, biological model, engineering discipline, autonomous capability, and any currently unknown knowledge domain SHALL be integrated into UCOS Ω∞ through constitutional registration, canonical ownership, ontology, taxonomy, registry, governance, validation, certification, and traceability — WITHOUT requiring architectural redesign.*

Corollaries (binding):
- **C-00.1** Integration is by *registration*, never by redesign. Adding a science/domain/capability is an append operation, never a schema/engine change.
- **C-00.2** Every integrated entity has exactly one canonical owner and one canonical home (Knowledge-Once).
- **C-00.3** Every integrated entity carries ontology + taxonomy + registry + governance + validation + certification + traceability (the seven integration facets). Absence of any ⇒ NOT integrated (fail-closed).
- **C-00.4** The set of sciences/domains/paradigms is **open and recursively extensible**; no compiled ceiling exists (Directives D3/D8/D9).

## PART B — Inherited & program laws

Inherited (restated for locality): **LAW P20-001/002/003** (explainable, governed-autonomy, provenance), **LAW P21-001/002/003** (governed/traceable/reversible learning).

Program-specific:

- **LAW USIS-01 — Universality of science & intelligence.** USIS admits *all* sciences and *all* intelligence paradigms — human, artificial, hybrid, collective, biological, cognitive, behavioral, environmental, civilizational, and unknown/future — as first-class, observer-relative universes/domains.
- **LAW USIS-02 — Realization, not duplication.** USIS realizes universes U16/U24/U25/U26/U28 + Parts 19/20/21. It SHALL NOT create a competing universe, catalog, ontology, taxonomy, or registry where a canonical one exists (Reuse-First, Zero-Duplication).
- **LAW USIS-03 — Registration over redesign.** (= LAW USIS-00 operationalized) every capability enters by registration; architecture never changes to admit one.
- **LAW USIS-04 — Technology neutrality.** No present-day vendor, cloud, framework, model, language, database, or algorithm is named in architecture; all are *registered content* behind agnostic registries, swappable without lifecycle change.
- **LAW USIS-05 — Canonical ownership / No-Orphan.** Every artifact has exactly one home and one owning family; zero orphans (GOV-001-T3).
- **LAW USIS-06 — Governed autonomy & self-evolution.** Self-* capabilities and autonomous agents operate within governed bounds; self-modification is simulate-then-adopt and reversible-or-justified (Part 21).
- **LAW USIS-07 — Explainable provenance.** Every inference, analytic, learned change, decision, and self-evolution step emits a provenance-and-explanation trace; absence ⇒ NOT-DONE (TRACK-001).
- **LAW USIS-08 — Meta-model conformance.** Every capability conforms to the Universal Capability Meta-Model (USIS-004) end-to-end (Science→…→Lifecycle).
- **LAW USIS-09 — Recursive extensibility.** A universe may contain universes; a discipline may spawn sub-disciplines; the substrate is self-similar at every level and admits new levels by registration.
