## 1 — What USIS is

USIS is the substrate beneath every science and every intelligence paradigm. It is composed of **21 constitutional universes** (see USIS-002 Universe Catalog), of which the former Universal Intelligence Platform is exactly one (the **Universal Intelligence Universe**). USIS:

- is **not** a rename of UIP — it is a superset: UIP ⊂ USIS.
- is **not** an AI/ML module or an algorithm collection — concrete algorithms/models/frameworks are *registered content* (LAW USIS-04).
- **realizes** universes already admitted by the Master Implementation Plan (`UCOS-MIP-000002`): Knowledge (U24), Intelligence (U25), Analytics (U16), Simulation (U26), Evolution (U28), and the Learning architecture (Part 21). USIS introduces **no duplicate sovereign universe**; it makes them implementation-ready and adds an explicit **science** framing over them.
