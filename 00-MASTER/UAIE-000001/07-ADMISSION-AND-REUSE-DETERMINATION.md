## Basis

00-CMG/CMG-000001 Article LXXVI.2(b) requires a candidate concept to be classified meta-constitutional or substantive and states that a substantive concept SHALL NOT be admitted into CMG. Architectural reasoning over repository artifacts governs no constitution and amends no constitutional process: it is substantive. Article LXXVII.2 therefore routes it to (a) REUSE where a located owner already holds the concern and (b) EXTEND where the concern lies inside a located owner's declared scope and is unaddressed there. Every faculty below takes one of those two outcomes or COMPOSE over several located owners. CREATE was rejected for all ten faculties: each is already realised, which is recorded per faculty in its reuse rationale. Consistent with this determination and with the observed content of the meta-constitutional registry — which recognizes only meta and family constitutional instruments and no substantive programme — this programme is deliberately absent from that registry, and the engine measures that absence rather than asserting it.

## Namespace

No namespace token is claimed in the meta-constitutional registry. Article XLI.5 forbids an EXTEND from introducing a new authority, registry, lifecycle or namespace, and Article LXXVI reserves namespace admission to the meta layer for meta-constitutional constructs only. This programme's identifier is a programme home under 00-MASTER/, which is the convention every other substantive programme in this repository already follows.

## Alternatives rejected

- CREATE a new architectural reasoning engine in engine/ or platform/ — rejected: the eleven located owners bound below already realise all ten faculties, and a new engine would be a parallel authority over architectural reasoning, which 00-CMG/CMG-000001 CMG-INV-02 forbids.
- CREATE a new analysis registry for architectural intelligence — rejected: 00-MASTER/UCOS-UAR-001 is the located owner of 'which analyses exist and who owns them' and is open by registration, so the contributed analyses below are entries in it rather than a second registry.
- ADMIT this programme into the meta-constitutional registry — rejected under Article LXXVI.2(b): the concept is substantive.
- REDESIGN the architectural layering of engine/graph, platform/repository_intelligence or engine/knowledge/integration — rejected: 00-MASTER/UCEF-000001 records that architectural redesign is not a stage of the constitutional evolution lifecycle, and a traversal requiring it has failed at reuse analysis and must return there.

## Reuse summary

Of 10 faculties: 8 bound by REUSE, 0 by EXTEND, 2 by COMPOSE, and 0 by creation. 18 analyses already registered elsewhere are reused, and 3 are contributed — each an entry in the located analysis registry rather than a registry of this programme's own.

The measured absence of `UAIE-000001` from the meta-constitutional plane is
the mechanical form of the no-parallel-authority rule: a substantive programme that
appeared there would be claiming meta-constitutional standing it does not hold, and
the gate closes on the mismatch in either direction.
