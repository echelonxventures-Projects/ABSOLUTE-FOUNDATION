## 8 · Closing determination

The engineering substrate of this repository is materially better than its own governance record
claims. 246,066 LOC with 8,307 tests, zero duplicate implementations, zero architectural cycles, zero
orphans, zero unowned units, verified determinism, and a capability disposition set that is computed
rather than asserted — across 1,204 registered artifacts — is a real foundation.

Its weaknesses are equally specific, and they share one shape: **the gates are excellent and their
boundary is drawn too small.** Coverage is measured over 57% of the code. Closure is measured over the
repository but not the corpus. Traceability is measured and then demoted to advisory. Registration is
enforced but its atomic-commit rule is not. In each case the discipline exists and the perimeter does
not enclose the thing it claims to.

That is a fortunate class of defect. It is remediable by extending declarations rather than rewriting
systems — which is why the determination is **CONDITIONALLY READY** rather than **NOT READY**, and why
the architecture is recommended for freeze while the baseline is not.
