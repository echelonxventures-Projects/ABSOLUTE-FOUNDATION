### UIS-F-001 — Permanent corpus identities are held by paths the admission authority now excludes

The identity ledger records permanent universal identities for paths that lie inside a currently excluded location — operational memory and derived intelligence — so a class that RFP-5 says may never consume a permanent corpus identity is holding one. These are legacy admissions: they were minted before the exclusion authority reached its present form, and AIF A/G7 grandfathers the imported corpus with frozen ordinals.

*Why it is not repaired here:* UIL-13 and AIF-L17 forbid erasing or editing a recorded identity; retired identities are never reissued and the past is never rewritten. Deleting these entries would be the very defect this programme measures. The divergence is therefore bounded so it cannot grow, and referred.

### UIS-F-002 — Recorded identities whose path no longer resolves

A number of recorded identities denote paths that are absent from the working tree, so UIL-08 resolvability cannot be satisfied for them. Because the ledger is the RECORDED plane, the absence of the file does not invalidate the identity — the identity outlives the artifact by design.

*Why it is not repaired here:* AIF-L17 forbids deletion of Recorded Truth and UIL-05 forbids reuse, so an identity whose artifact is gone must remain recorded and must never be reallocated. Bounded and referred.

### UIS-F-003 — Live category namespaces exist with no declared classification rule

UMB-004 §5 obliges a new entity class to receive BOTH a category namespace and a classification rule. A majority of the live category namespaces hold minted identities but match no declared rule: they were derived automatically from the artifact's own filename by the allocator's fallback, so the namespace exists without an authority that governs it.

*Why it is not repaired here:* Declaring a classification rule is an act of the nomenclature owner, and the namespaces are already bound to permanent identities that UIL-06 forbids renumbering. This measurement may not legislate a rule without becoming a second nomenclature authority.

### UIS-F-004 — The namespace-derivation width narrowed, leaving ambiguous namespace pairs on the record

AIF-L24 pins the identifier width as a parameter that widens append-only. The allocator's derived-category width is now narrower than the width under which some recorded namespaces were minted, so the record contains namespaces longer than the current parameter admits, and pairs of distinct namespaces that differ only inside the truncation window — the ambiguity UMB-004 §6 addresses.

*Why it is not repaired here:* Widening or narrowing a pinned parameter is a witnessed act of the parameter's owner under AIF-L24, and the affected identities may not be renumbered under UIL-06. Disclosed for the parameter owner.

### UIS-F-005 — One identity law is declared but not decidable from the located artifact-plane data

Reflexivity (UIL-11) ranges over the internal constructs of the located code-plane engines rather than over registered artifacts. This measurement reports it as DECLARED-NOT-MEASURABLE rather than passing it silently, so the coverage of the measurement is itself visible and a future binding is an append-only edit to this declaration.

*Why it is not repaired here:* Asserting reflexivity would require this measurement to read or mint a code-plane identity it holds no authority over.

### UIS-F-006 — Self-description is measured by resolution, because parseable self-description would contradict identifier opacity

The requirement that every identifier be constitutionally self-describing admits two readings. Read as 'the token can be parsed for its meaning', it contradicts two located laws: AIF-L02 makes durable identity OPAQUE, and ENG-001 UIS-P-06 states that a UID's internal structure carries no semantics consumers may depend upon, meaning being obtained by resolution rather than parsing. Read as 'resolving the identifier answers every constitutional question about the object', it is exactly what UMB-003 §3 declares as the identity facet set and §5 as resolvability. This measurement adopts the second reading and measures it: every declared bookkeeping obligation must bind to a located field of a located source and to a member of the located closed facet set.

*Why it is not repaired here:* Adopting the parsing reading would require amending AIF-L02 and UIS-P-06, which is an act of their owners and not of a measurement. The reading is disclosed so it can be overturned by the owner rather than silently assumed here.
