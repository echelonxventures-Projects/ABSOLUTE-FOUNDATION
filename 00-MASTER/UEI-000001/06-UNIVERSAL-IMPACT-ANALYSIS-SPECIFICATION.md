## UEI-CAP-05 — Universal Impact Analysis

- **Mandate** — ANALYSE IMPACT
- **Objective** — Determine the full consequence of a proposed change before it is made, so no evolution is executed with an unknown blast radius.
- **Coverage** — **COVERED**
- **Governed** — YES
- **Reuse (Zero Duplication)** — The architecture-intelligence layer already realises the impact engine, the blast-radius engine and the normalised Affects closure over the certified knowledge graph (EPIC-010, EPIC-002); the knowledge intelligence facade already realises transitive impact analysis; the meta-governance impact instrument already owns the constitutional repository impact analysis. This programme binds them; it introduces no new impact analyser.
- **Open gap** — none

**Constitutional contract — required content/behaviour**

- the union blast radius across a whole change set, de-duplicated
- impacted artifacts ranked by propagation distance, nearest first
- the architectural layers and capabilities the change reaches
- the certified, frozen or final surface the change disturbs
- a bounded, deterministic risk score with a severity band
- analysis is read-only over the graph and mutates no artifact
- an unanalysable change set is a fail-closed refusal, not a zero-risk verdict
