## UEI-CAP-03 — Universal Optimization

- **Mandate** — OPTIMIZE
- **Objective** — Improve the platform's artifacts and measures without changing their meaning, without inventing behaviour, and without losing traceability.
- **Coverage** — **COVERED**
- **Governed** — YES
- **Reuse (Zero Duplication)** — The compiler already realises the semantics- and traceability-preserving optimization stage with post-transform provenance re-assertion (EPIC-003, IMP-007 §9); the measurement and coverage platforms already realise the located, computed measures an optimization must move; the pre-implementation optimization determination already governs the optimization posture. This programme binds them; it introduces no new optimizer.
- **Open gap** — none

**Constitutional contract — required content/behaviour**

- every transform is semantics-preserving
- every transform is traceability-preserving — the provenance chain survives
- no derivable behaviour is removed and no behaviour is invented
- optimization output is byte-stable, so optimizing twice is a no-op
- the provenance chain is re-asserted after every transform
- the measure being optimized is located and computed, never estimated
- an optimization that cannot be proven is not applied
