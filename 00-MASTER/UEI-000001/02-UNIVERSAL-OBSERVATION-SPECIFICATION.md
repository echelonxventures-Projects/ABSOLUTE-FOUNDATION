## UEI-CAP-01 — Universal Observation

- **Mandate** — OBSERVE
- **Objective** — Continuously observe the platform, the corpus and the execution substrate so that every subsequent inference is grounded in measured reality rather than assertion.
- **Coverage** — **COVERED**
- **Governed** — YES
- **Reuse (Zero Duplication)** — The observability platform already realises metrics, traces, logs, alerting, audit and health (EC2-EPIC-013); the engine foundation already realises execution telemetry; the advancement connectors already ingest external signals into an append-only signal store. This programme binds them; it introduces no new observation mechanism.
- **Open gap** — none

**Constitutional contract — required content/behaviour**

- metric observation with declared units and deterministic ordering
- distributed trace observation across execution boundaries
- structured log observation with no secret disclosure
- threshold alerting with a located owner for every alert
- immutable audit observation of every governed action
- external-signal ingestion appended, never overwritten
- health observation of every registered subsystem
- observation is read-only over its subject and mutates nothing it observes
