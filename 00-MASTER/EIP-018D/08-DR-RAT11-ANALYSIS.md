## 3 — The precise gap

The single authority that **cannot** be established from repository evidence is the **constituent authority to declare absolute constitutional finality (FINALIZED)** — the exogenous founding act that constitutes a ratifier (identity, membership, quorum, amendment procedure). It is, by CEP-006 Art I.4, an **out-of-corpus** authority that is *superior for finality* and that the corpus explicitly does **not** hold and must **not** fabricate.

This is distinct from **all** the authorities the corpus *does* hold (engineering, governance-provisional, repository). The corpus can validate, certify, freeze, register, and **provisionally** ratify — it simply cannot **finalize**.

The authority that **cannot** be established from repository evidence is exactly **External Constituent Authority → absolute Constitutional Finality (FINALIZED)**.
