## 2. KNOWLEDGE ONCE AT ADMISSION

The platform's entire operative contribution to the knowledge graph is four determinations across two engines.

| Determination | Stage | Engine | Located gate | On failure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| does this knowledge already exist? | `CIOS-S-03` | `E-03` | `G-02` Knowledge Assimilation Gate | non-admission |
| recurrence disposition — `NEW` \| `EXTEND` \| `REJECT` | `CIOS-S-04` | `E-03` | `G-03` Reuse Gate | non-admission; **an unresolvable match never resolves to `NEW`** |
| canonical home — exactly one | `CIOS-S-05` | `E-04` | `G-06` Repository Truth Gate | non-admission on 0 or >1 home |
| canonical owner — exactly one | `CIOS-S-06` | `E-04` | `G-06` | non-admission |

**The `NEW`-refusal rule in row 2 is the load-bearing one.** A recurrence engine that resolved ambiguity to `NEW` would manufacture duplicate knowledge at exactly the moment the check exists to prevent it. `CIOS-05` `P-06` fixes the behaviour: unresolvable match ⇒ non-admission, **never** `NEW`.

## AUTHORITY BOUNDARY (MANDATORY)

This framework binds one located knowledge graph. **It creates no graph, no store, no edge vocabulary and no edge instance; it regenerates no closure truth and repairs no home.** Six edge types are **proposed** for the located vocabulary's owner and are not admitted. Traceability closure is expressly **not** claimed. Derived intelligence is never persisted as fact. Every owner named is located in an instrument existing independently at `b26c5bb`. Where this framework and a located canonical instrument disagree, **the located instrument governs and this framework SHALL be corrected**. It confers no authority on itself and authorizes no execution.
