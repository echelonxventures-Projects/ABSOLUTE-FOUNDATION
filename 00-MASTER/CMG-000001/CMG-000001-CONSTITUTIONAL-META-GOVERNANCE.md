## SECTION 5: CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT PROCESS

### 5.1 Amendment Types

**Type 1: Additive Amendment**
Adds new capabilities, dimensions, or mechanisms without changing existing definitions.

**Approval Required:** Governance Authority consensus

**Type 2: Clarification Amendment**
Clarifies ambiguous language without changing semantics.

**Approval Required:** Governance Authority simple majority

**Type 3: Breaking Amendment**
Changes existing semantics, requiring version increment.

**Approval Required:** Governance Authority supermajority + impact assessment

### 5.2 Amendment Process

1. **Proposal:**
   Amendment proposed with rationale and impact analysis

2. **Review:**
   Constitutional review committee evaluates:
   - Principle compliance
   - Dependency impact
   - Boundary violations
   - Implementation impact

3. **Evidence:**
   Amendment evidence created documenting proposal

4. **Validation:**
   Amendment validated against constitutional principles

5. **Approval:**
   Governance Authority votes per amendment type requirements

6. **Implementation:**
   Amendment applied to constitutional instrument

7. **Preservation:**
   Amendment evidence preserved in UCKP

8. **Notification:**
   All dependent constitutional instruments notified

### 5.3 Emergency Amendment

**When immediate amendment required (security vulnerability, critical error):**

1. Emergency amendment authority activated
2. Amendment applied immediately
3. Retrospective validation within 30 days
4. If validation fails, amendment reverted

**Attack 4: Implementation Substitution**
