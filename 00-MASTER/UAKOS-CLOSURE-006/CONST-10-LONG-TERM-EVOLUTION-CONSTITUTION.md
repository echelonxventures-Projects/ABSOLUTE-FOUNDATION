Define the permanent, constitutional policy for how *any* future architectural idea may enter

## 3. Prohibitions (frozen)

- **No direct insertion** into Repository Truth.
- **No bypass** of any stage in the path above.
- **No competing authority** may admit knowledge outside this path.
- **No architectural redesign** to accommodate a single idea — ideas conform to the path.

## 4. Entry Rule

All future ideas enter ONLY through Conversation → Knowledge Ingestion. This is the single ingress.
Uploads and vision documents are ingested through the same funnel.

## 5. Evolution Without Redesign

Because the path and pipeline (CONST-08) are frozen, the architecture never needs redesign to
evolve. New knowledge scales through the same deterministic, fail-closed funnel — "infinitely
scalable, constitutionally governed."

## 6. DETERMINATION

The long-term evolution policy is frozen: a single ingress, a fixed ordered path, no direct
insertion, no bypass. This prepares the repository for future execution waves without further
architectural redesign.
