## 1. Headline determination

**The implementation is real. The verification of it is partial.**

Two facts, both measured, define the assessment:

1. **Code substance is genuine.** 2051 `.py` files, ~558 000 LOC across eight roots. Only **44 `raise NotImplementedError`** in the entire non-test corpus, nearly all abstract-base markers annotated `# pragma: no cover`. **Zero TODO/FIXME** in seven of eight roots (the 3 in `platform` are rule *definitions about* TODOs plus a test fixture). No stub packages, no placeholder scaffolding.
2. **Verification reaches roughly half of it.** The coverage gate names **59 `--cov=` targets**, all under `engine.*`/`platform.*`. `pytest testpaths` names **3 roots**. Consequently **3879 test functions and ~130 000 LOC of implementation are outside both the collection set and the coverage denominator** — not failing, simply never measured.
