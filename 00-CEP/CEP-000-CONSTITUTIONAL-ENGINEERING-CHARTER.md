## SECTION 1 — CHARTER PREAMBLE

1.1 This Charter IS the root governing instrument of the UCOS Ω∞ Constitutional Engineering Program (hereinafter "the Program" or "the CEP").

1.2 The Program SHALL exist to govern the disciplined, deterministic, evidence-based, constitution-first engineering of the UCOS Ω∞ repository.

1.3 This Charter SHALL be the constitutional root for every remaining CEP document. Every CEP instrument produced after this Charter SHALL derive its authority from, and remain consistent with, this Charter.

1.4 This Charter SHALL govern process, discipline, sequence, governance, validation, ratification, and completion. This Charter SHALL NOT author constitutional content, and SHALL NOT confer authority over the substance of the UCOS Ω∞ Constitution.

1.5 This Charter SHALL bind all future stages of the Program until this Charter is amended under its own Amendment Principles (Section 25) or frozen under its own Freeze Principles (Section 29).

1.6 Every ambiguity in any CEP instrument SHALL be resolved in favor of the interpretation most consistent with this Charter. Where this Charter is itself ambiguous, the interpretation most consistent with the Core Principles (Section 10) SHALL govern.

---

## SECTION 2 — MISSION

2.1 The mission of the Program IS to transform the UCOS Ω∞ repository into a complete, canonical, internally consistent, and constitutionally governed corpus through a deterministic and auditable engineering process.

2.2 The Program SHALL ensure that every constitutional concern has exactly one canonical definition and exactly one canonical owner.

2.3 The Program SHALL ensure that no artifact is generated before the evidence and definitions on which it depends are discovered, reconciled, and ratified.

2.4 The Program SHALL ensure that every unit of work is validated before progression, ratified before completion, and frozen after ratification.

2.5 The Program SHALL execute autonomously, deterministically, and recoverably, without dependence on conversational instruction to determine its next action.

---

## SECTION 3 — VISION

3.1 The Program envisions a UCOS Ω∞ repository in which every artifact is traceable to a ratified definition, every definition is traceable to evidence, and every claim is reproducible.

3.2 The Program envisions a corpus free of duplicated concepts, overlapping ownership, conflicting terminology, and undocumented dependencies.

3.3 The Program envisions an execution capability that resumes deterministically from any interruption, that never mutates frozen work, and that proves rather than asserts its own correctness.

3.4 The Program envisions a governance model in which authority is explicit, located, and non-overlapping, and in which no control exists that does not close a named risk.

---

## SECTION 4 — PURPOSE

4.1 The purpose of this Charter IS to establish why the Program exists, what authority it possesses, what responsibilities it governs, and what principles every future stage SHALL obey.

4.2 This Charter SHALL provide the single, permanent, normative reference against which every CEP stage, instrument, and decision is measured.

4.3 This Charter SHALL make the Program governable, auditable, deterministic, recoverable, and extensible by fixing its principles in constitutional form.

---

## SECTION 5 — CONSTITUTIONAL AUTHORITY

5.1 This Charter SHALL hold Program Authority: the authority to govern the process, sequence, discipline, and governance of constitutional engineering within UCOS Ω∞.

5.2 Program Authority SHALL be supreme over all CEP documents, stages, instruments, agents, checkpoints, evidence, and reports.

5.3 Program Authority SHALL NOT extend to the substance of the UCOS Ω∞ Constitution. The Program governs how constitutional content is engineered; it does not decide what that content is.

5.4 Program Authority SHALL NEVER self-elevate. The Program SHALL NOT grant itself authority over constitutional content, and no CEP agent SHALL grant itself ratification authority.

5.5 The authority tiers of the Program SHALL be, in descending precedence:
- Tier 1 — Constitutional Authority (the ratified UCOS Ω∞ Constitution and its concerns).
- Tier 2 — Program Authority (this Charter and its derived CEP instruments).
- Tier 3 — Execution Authority (the autonomous execution agent, acting only within the current stage gate).
- Tier 4 — Derived-Truth Authority (program state, checkpoints, evidence, and reports, which assert nothing and are always reconciled against repository reality).

5.6 Each tier SHALL be subordinate to every tier above it. No tier SHALL bypass, override, or absorb the responsibilities of any other tier.

## SECTION 6 — LEGAL AUTHORITY WITHIN UCOS Ω∞

6.1 Within UCOS Ω∞, this Charter SHALL be recognized as the binding governing instrument of the constitutional engineering process.

6.2 Every CEP stage and instrument IS REQUIRED to comply with this Charter. Non-compliance SHALL constitute a Program defect that halts progression until remediated.

6.3 This Charter SHALL derive its standing from its role as the self-constituting root of the Program and from the validated execution architecture ratified in Stage 01 · Prompt 01.

6.4 This Charter SHALL NOT override, suspend, or amend the UCOS Ω∞ Constitution, any frozen corpus, or any authority residing outside the Program.

6.5 Where final constitutional finality requires an authority residing outside the corpus, this Charter SHALL recognize that authority as superior for the purpose of finality, and SHALL treat engineering-level ratification as PROVISIONAL with respect to that finality.

## SECTION 7 — PROGRAM SCOPE

7.1 The Program SHALL govern the following stages, executed as a governed directed acyclic graph with default sequential progression:
- G0 — Program Governance.
- G1 — Repository Discovery.
- G2 — Reconciliation.
- G3 — Constitutional Definition.
- G4 — Universe and Boundary Definition.
- G5 — Generation.
- G6 — Implementation.
- G7 — Validation and Certification.
- G8 — Ratification.
- G9 — Freeze.

7.2 The Program SHALL govern the cross-cutting mechanisms that bind these stages: governance, evidence, traceability, validation, certification, ratification, determinism, checkpointing, recovery, repository preservation, change control, amendment, and completion.

7.3 The Program SHALL NOT extend its scope to any activity that authors, alters, or decides the substance of constitutional content outside the stages and gates defined herein.

7.4 Any discovery that falls outside the current scope IS REQUIRED to be recorded in the Deferral Register and SHALL NOT be silently discarded or silently acted upon.

## SECTION 8 — PROGRAM OBJECTIVES

8.1 The Program SHALL establish and preserve exactly one canonical definition and exactly one canonical owner for every constitutional concern.

8.2 The Program SHALL ensure discovery precedes generation, reconciliation precedes modification, validation precedes progression, ratification precedes completion, and freeze follows ratification.

8.3 The Program SHALL ensure every artifact is rooted in a ratified definition and closed to reproducible evidence, with zero orphans.

8.4 The Program SHALL ensure determinism is engineered and verified, not assumed.

8.5 The Program SHALL ensure that interruption is recoverable and that recovery never alters ratified or frozen results.

8.6 The Program SHALL ensure that governance, validation, and certification are never bypassed.

## SECTION 9 — CONSTITUTIONAL RESPONSIBILITIES

9.1 The Program IS REQUIRED to maintain the tiered authority model (Section 5) without overlap.

9.2 The Program IS REQUIRED to maintain a single authoritative program-state instrument that is reconciled against repository truth at every boot.

9.3 The Program IS REQUIRED to maintain append-only checkpoints, an append-only ratification ledger, an append-only amendment record, and an append-only Deferral Register.

9.4 The Program IS REQUIRED to preserve all frozen work as immutable and to enforce additive-only modification where mandated.

9.5 The Program IS REQUIRED to route every inconsistency finding to the Amendment mechanism and every out-of-scope finding to the Deferral Register.

9.6 The Program IS REQUIRED to prove determinism, closure of traceability, and preservation of frozen work at every gate before progression.

## SECTION 11 — FUNDAMENTAL ENGINEERING PRINCIPLES

11.1 EP-1 — The Program SHALL be repository-driven; every decision SHALL rest on repository evidence rather than assumption.

11.2 EP-2 — The Program SHALL be constitution-first; no implementation SHALL precede its governing definition.

11.3 EP-3 — The Program SHALL be discovery-first; interpretation SHALL NOT occur during discovery.

11.4 EP-4 — The Program SHALL be additive by default; corrections to frozen work SHALL occur through supersession, not in-place mutation.

11.5 EP-5 — The Program SHALL be composable; every stage SHALL consume the frozen outputs of its predecessors by reference.

11.6 EP-6 — The Program SHALL be extensible; new concerns SHALL be admitted additively without redesign of the execution architecture.

11.7 EP-7 — The Program SHALL be agnostic to repository layout, platform, infrastructure, cloud, database, programming language, vendor, and AI model.

## SECTION 12 — GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLES

12.1 GP-1 — Every concern and every responsibility SHALL have exactly one registered owner. Ownership SHALL NEVER be implied.

12.2 GP-2 — Where two claimants assert the same concern, the earliest ratified definition SHALL prevail; the conflict SHALL be logged as an inconsistency finding, and the superseded claim SHALL be recorded, never silently discarded.

12.3 GP-3 — Governance, validation, and certification SHALL NEVER be bypassed. Any bypass SHALL constitute a Program defect that halts progression.

12.4 GP-4 — No governance control SHALL exist that does not close a named risk.

12.5 GP-5 — No authority tier SHALL self-elevate or absorb another tier's responsibilities.

12.6 GP-6 — Every governance decision SHALL be recorded, addressable, and auditable.

## SECTION 13 — EXECUTION PRINCIPLES

13.1 XP-1 — The Program SHALL execute along the forward stage graph in topological order by default.

13.2 XP-2 — The Program SHALL NEVER skip a gate. Stages MAY be re-entered ONLY through the Amendment mechanism.

13.3 XP-3 — Each stage SHALL write ONLY to its declared write area. Cross-area writes ARE PROHIBITED.

13.4 XP-4 — Backward motion in the stage graph SHALL occur ONLY through governed feedback edges, and ONLY upon a logged finding and an amendment decision. Feedback edges SHALL NEVER be traversed automatically.

13.5 XP-5 — The execution lifecycle SHALL be uniform across all stages: boot, reconcile, enter under entry criteria, execute the single authorized action, validate, certify where applicable, gate under exit criteria, checkpoint, and route findings.

13.6 XP-6 — Every dependency SHALL be documented. Any undocumented dependency SHALL constitute a Program defect.

## SECTION 14 — REPOSITORY PRESERVATION PRINCIPLES

14.1 RP-1 — Frozen artifacts and source-of-truth corpora ARE immutable and SHALL NEVER be modified.

14.2 RP-2 — Modification of frozen work SHALL occur ONLY through supersession under the Amendment mechanism.

14.3 RP-3 — Preservation SHALL precede modification; the Program SHALL verify what exists before making any change and SHALL NEVER assume repository state.

14.4 RP-4 — All baselines SHALL be content-addressed and reproducible so that drift is detectable.

14.5 RP-5 — Operational memory — including program state, checkpoints, evidence indexes, and reports — SHALL NEVER enter the constitutional corpus and SHALL hold no authority.

14.6 RP-6 — The Program SHALL maintain a no-orphan and no-drift guard ensuring every registered artifact exists, every artifact is registered, and no baseline has drifted.

14.7 RP-7 — Destructive operations against shared or frozen state ARE PROHIBITED.

19.4 AP-4 — Autonomous execution SHALL NEVER bypass a gate, a governance control, or a preservation rule.

## SECTION 24 — CHANGE CONTROL PRINCIPLES

24.1 CH-1 — Every change SHALL be classified as additive, corrective, or superseding.

24.2 CH-2 — Additive changes SHALL NOT alter existing frozen content.

24.3 CH-3 — Corrective changes to non-frozen work SHALL be re-validated before progression.

24.4 CH-4 — Superseding changes to frozen work SHALL occur ONLY through the Amendment mechanism and SHALL be append-only.

24.5 CH-5 — Every change SHALL be recorded with its classification, justification, and evidence.

## SECTION 25 — AMENDMENT PRINCIPLES

25.1 AM-1 — Ratified or frozen work SHALL NEVER be mutated in place.

25.2 AM-2 — An amendment SHALL proceed only as: proposal, evidence, impact analysis, re-validation, re-ratification, and supersession.

25.3 AM-3 — An amendment SHALL be raised only upon a logged inconsistency finding or ratification rejection.

25.4 AM-4 — A superseded artifact SHALL be retained for lineage and SHALL be marked superseded; it SHALL NOT be deleted.

25.5 AM-5 — Prior ratification SHALL NOT carry over to an amended artifact; every amended artifact IS REQUIRED to be re-validated and re-ratified.

25.6 AM-6 — This Charter MAY be amended only under these Amendment Principles and only while it is not frozen.

## SECTION 26 — VALIDATION PRINCIPLES

26.4 VP-4 — A gate SHALL be PASS only when all applicable blocking checks pass; otherwise it SHALL be BLOCKED.

## SECTION 28 — RATIFICATION PRINCIPLES

28.2 RA-2 — Ratification authority SHALL be located. Where the authority is in-corpus, artifacts SHALL be ratified to RATIFIED. Where final authority is out-of-corpus or unavailable, artifacts SHALL be ratified to PROVISIONAL, engineering-ratified and finality-pending.

28.3 RA-3 — PROVISIONAL status SHALL be non-blocking to engineering progression and SHALL be blocking only to declared constitutional finality.

28.4 RA-4 — A ratification rejection SHALL emit a governed feedback edge into the Amendment mechanism and SHALL NEVER cause a silent modification.

28.5 RA-5 — Ratification records SHALL be append-only and content-addressed.

28.6 RA-6 — Ratification SHALL NEVER be self-conferred by Execution Authority.

## SECTION 34 — DEFINITIONS

34.1 Program / CEP — the UCOS Ω∞ Constitutional Engineering Program governed by this Charter.

34.2 Charter — this document, the root governing instrument of the Program.

34.3 Stage — a governed unit of Program work, one of G0 through G9, with a single purpose and a single write area.

34.4 Gate — the machine-checkable control between stages that governs progression.

34.5 Entry Criteria — the predicates that must hold before a stage may be entered.

34.6 Exit Criteria — the predicates that must hold before a stage may be exited.

34.7 Checkpoint — an append-only resume anchor recording current stage, gate status, next authorized action, program-state hash, and repository anchor.

34.8 Finding — a logged record of a discovered inconsistency requiring amendment.

34.9 Deferral — a logged record of an out-of-scope discovery held in the Deferral Register.

34.10 Evidence — an addressable, reproducible record bound to a claim.

34.11 Determinism — the engineered property that identical inputs yield byte-identical, content-addressed outputs.

34.12 Ratified — a status conferred by in-corpus ratification authority upon validated, certified work.

34.13 Provisional — a status conferred when final ratification authority is out-of-corpus or unavailable; non-blocking to engineering, blocking only to constitutional finality.

34.14 Frozen — the immutable status of ratified work sealed with a content-addressed baseline.

34.15 Canonical Owner — the single registered owner of a constitutional responsibility.

34.16 Canonical Definition — the single registered definition of a constitutional concern.

34.17 Operational Memory — program state, checkpoints, evidence indexes, and reports, which hold no authority and never enter the corpus.
