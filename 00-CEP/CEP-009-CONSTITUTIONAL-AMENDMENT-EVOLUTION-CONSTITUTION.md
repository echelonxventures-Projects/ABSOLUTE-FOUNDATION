## PREAMBLE

P.1 This instrument IS the permanent constitutional amendment and evolution instrument of the UCOS Ω∞ Constitutional Engineering Program.

P.2 Amendment SHALL be the governed change of a ratified or frozen artifact; evolution SHALL be the ordered succession of artifacts across time through amendment.

P.3 This instrument SHALL guarantee that UCOS Ω∞ can evolve infinitely without mutating frozen truth, destroying history, breaking lineage, or bypassing governance, validation, certification, or ratification.

P.4 This instrument refines the general Amendment Model of CEP-001 Article XV and the Change Control Principles of CEP-000 §24, and SHALL remain consistent with both; it consumes the freeze and supersession mechanics of CEP-007 and the lineage, evidence, and traceability substrate of CEP-008 by reference.

P.5 This instrument SHALL bind every amendment and evolution act of the Program until amended or frozen under its own rules.

## ARTICLE I — AMENDMENT AUTHORITY

I.1 Amendment Authority SHALL be the authority to admit an amendment, authorize successor creation, and record evolution; it SHALL confer no acceptance, certification, ratification, or freeze itself.

I.2 Amendment Authority SHALL evolve only through successor creation; it SHALL modify no frozen artifact and SHALL introduce no constitutional content of its own.

I.3 Amendment Authority SHALL be single per artifact lineage; two authorities SHALL NOT own the evolution of the same artifact. A contested authority SHALL be resolved under CEP-002 Article 23.

I.4 Amendment Authority SHALL NOT govern, execute, validate, certify, ratify, freeze, or create evidence, and SHALL NOT exercise a power reserved to another constitution.

I.5 Amendment Authority SHALL NEVER be self-conferred by Execution Authority and SHALL act only upon a logged inconsistency finding, a ratification rejection, a governed evolution request, or a deprecation determination.

---

## ARTICLE II — EVOLUTION SCOPE

II.1 This instrument SHALL legislate amendment authority, change lifecycle, change classification, eligibility, evolution state machine, impact assessment, compatibility, backward and forward compatibility, successor creation, migration, deprecation, retirement, lineage preservation, registry, records, traceability, audit, enforcement, recovery, and closure.

II.2 This instrument SHALL NOT legislate governance, execution, validation, certification, ratification, freeze, or evidence creation.

II.3 Amendment SHALL be non-mutating with respect to frozen truth; it SHALL produce successors and SHALL never alter a predecessor.

II.4 An amendment or evolution act outside a defined authorization IS PROHIBITED and SHALL be void.

II.5 Amendment and evolution jurisdiction SHALL be bounded to this instrument alone and SHALL NOT overlap the jurisdiction of ratification (CEP-006), freeze (CEP-007), or evidence and traceability (CEP-008); those constitutions are consumed by reference.

---

## ARTICLE III — CHANGE LIFECYCLE

III.1 The change lifecycle SHALL be: propose, classify, assess impact, admit or reject, create successor, and complete the succession, followed where applicable by deprecation and retirement of the predecessor.

III.2 A change SHALL be proposed only upon a logged inconsistency finding, a ratification rejection, a governed evolution request, or a deprecation determination.

III.3 A change SHALL produce a successor artifact; it SHALL NEVER mutate the predecessor.

III.4 A successor artifact SHALL undergo validation (CEP-004), certification (CEP-005), and ratification (CEP-006), and SHALL be eligible for freeze (CEP-007), before it supersedes its predecessor.

III.5 The predecessor SHALL transition to SUPERSEDED only upon the successor reaching a frozen, ratified state, and SHALL remain immutable and historically available thereafter.

---

## ARTICLE IV — CHANGE CLASSIFICATION

IV.1 Every change SHALL be classified as additive, corrective, superseding, deprecating, or retiring.

IV.2 An additive change SHALL introduce a new artifact and SHALL NOT alter any existing frozen artifact.

IV.3 A corrective change to a non-frozen artifact SHALL be re-validated before progression; a corrective change to a frozen artifact SHALL proceed only as a superseding change.

IV.4 A superseding change SHALL create a successor that supersedes a frozen predecessor without mutating it.

IV.5 A deprecating change SHALL mark an artifact for phase-out while preserving its historical availability; a retiring change SHALL end an artifact's active use while preserving its historical availability.

IV.6 Classification SHALL be decidable and SHALL assign exactly one primary class to each change.

---

## ARTICLE V — AMENDMENT ELIGIBILITY

V.1 An artifact SHALL be eligible for amendment only when it is ratified or frozen and its lineage is intact.

V.2 Eligibility SHALL be decidable: an artifact is either eligible or not eligible, with no intermediate condition.

V.3 Eligibility SHALL require a valid amendment trigger (Article I.5) and a satisfiable impact assessment (Article VII).

V.4 An ineligible amendment SHALL NOT proceed; an attempted amendment of an ineligible artifact SHALL be void.

V.5 An amendment SHALL NOT be raised on grounds identical to a previously rejected amendment of the same artifact, ensuring deterministic termination.

---

## ARTICLE VI — EVOLUTION STATE MACHINE

VI.1 The evolution states of an artifact SHALL be: CURRENT, UNDER_AMENDMENT, SUPERSEDED, DEPRECATED, RETIRED.

VI.2 The initial state SHALL be CURRENT; the terminal state SHALL be RETIRED.

VI.3 The legal evolution transitions SHALL be exactly:
- CURRENT → UNDER_AMENDMENT.
- UNDER_AMENDMENT → CURRENT.
- UNDER_AMENDMENT → SUPERSEDED.
- CURRENT → DEPRECATED.
- SUPERSEDED → DEPRECATED.
- DEPRECATED → RETIRED.

VI.4 Any transition not enumerated in VI.3 IS PROHIBITED and SHALL be treated as an illegal transition that places the Program in HALTED.

VI.5 Every evolution state SHALL be defined; an undefined evolution state IS PROHIBITED. Every non-terminal state SHALL have at least one defined outgoing transition, and every state SHALL be reachable from CURRENT.

VI.6 The transition UNDER_AMENDMENT → CURRENT SHALL occur when an amendment is rejected or withdrawn, leaving the predecessor unchanged and current.

VI.7 The transitions to SUPERSEDED, DEPRECATED, and RETIRED SHALL record status and lineage only and SHALL NOT modify the artifact, which SHALL remain immutable and historically available.

---

## ARTICLE VII — IMPACT ASSESSMENT MODEL

VII.1 Every proposed change SHALL undergo an impact assessment before admission.

VII.2 Impact assessment SHALL determine the artifacts, lineages, dependencies, and compatibility affected by the change.

VII.3 Impact assessment SHALL be deterministic and SHALL resolve to a decidable set of affected elements.

VII.4 A change whose impact cannot be determined SHALL NOT be admitted.

VII.5 Impact assessment SHALL introduce no content and SHALL modify no artifact.

---

## ARTICLE VIII — COMPATIBILITY MODEL

VIII.1 Every change SHALL declare its compatibility impact as compatible, backward-compatible-only, forward-compatible-only, or breaking.

VIII.2 Compatibility impact SHALL be determined by the impact assessment and SHALL be machine-verifiable.

VIII.3 A change SHALL NOT be admitted while its compatibility impact is undetermined.

VIII.4 A breaking change SHALL require a migration model (Article XII) before completion.

---

## ARTICLE IX — BACKWARD COMPATIBILITY

IX.1 A successor SHALL declare whether it preserves backward compatibility with its predecessor's dependents.

IX.2 Where backward compatibility is not preserved, the successor SHALL declare the breakage and SHALL require migration for affected dependents.

IX.3 Backward compatibility SHALL be verified against the predecessor's declared contracts by reference; it SHALL NOT be assumed.

IX.4 A successor SHALL NOT silently break backward compatibility; an undeclared breakage IS PROHIBITED.

---

## ARTICLE X — FORWARD COMPATIBILITY

X.1 A successor SHALL declare the forward-compatibility expectations it places upon future successors.

X.2 Forward compatibility SHALL be expressed as declared, referenceable expectations and SHALL NOT constrain future evolution beyond those declarations.

X.3 Forward compatibility SHALL NOT be used to prohibit lawful future amendment.

X.4 An undeclared forward-compatibility expectation SHALL NOT be enforced.

---

## ARTICLE XI — SUCCESSOR ARTIFACT MODEL

XI.1 A successor artifact SHALL be a new artifact with its own canonical identity, related to its predecessor by an explicit lineage link.

XI.2 A successor SHALL NOT reuse, overwrite, or inherit the identity of its predecessor.

XI.3 A successor SHALL undergo validation, certification, and ratification, and SHALL be eligible for freeze, before it supersedes its predecessor (no bypass).

XI.4 A successor SHALL reference its predecessor by identity and SHALL NEVER modify it.

XI.5 A successor SHALL preserve the predecessor's history in the lineage established under CEP-008.

---

## ARTICLE XII — MIGRATION MODEL

XII.1 A migration SHALL be the declared path by which dependents move from a predecessor to its successor.

XII.2 A migration SHALL be required for any breaking change and SHALL be declared before the change completes.

XII.3 A migration SHALL be deterministic, referenceable, and reversible in declaration such that its effect on dependents is auditable.

XII.4 A migration SHALL NOT mutate the predecessor and SHALL NOT destroy any historical version.

XII.5 A migration SHALL introduce no constitutional content; it SHALL declare movement only.

---

## ARTICLE XIII — DEPRECATION MODEL

XIII.1 Deprecation SHALL mark an artifact for phase-out while preserving its historical availability and reproducibility.

XIII.2 A deprecated artifact SHALL remain discoverable and traceable and SHALL NOT be deleted.

XIII.3 Deprecation SHALL declare the reason and, where applicable, the successor that replaces the deprecated artifact.

XIII.4 Deprecation SHALL NOT modify the deprecated artifact and SHALL be recorded as a status and lineage transition only.

XIII.5 A deprecated artifact MAY be retired only under the Retirement Model (Article XIV).

---

## ARTICLE XIV — RETIREMENT MODEL

XIV.1 Retirement SHALL end an artifact's active use while preserving its historical availability and reproducibility.

XIV.2 A retired artifact SHALL remain discoverable and traceable and SHALL NEVER be deleted.

XIV.3 Retirement SHALL occur only from DEPRECATED and SHALL be recorded as a status and lineage transition only.

XIV.4 A retired artifact SHALL NOT be relied upon for active constitutional operation but SHALL remain part of the historical record.

XIV.5 Retirement SHALL NOT modify the retired artifact.

---

## ARTICLE XV — LINEAGE PRESERVATION

XV.1 Every amendment SHALL establish a lineage link from predecessor to successor through the lineage model of CEP-008.

XV.2 Lineage SHALL be a deterministic, acyclic chain; a lineage that would form a cycle IS PROHIBITED.

XV.3 Lineage SHALL be append-only; a recorded lineage link SHALL NOT be altered or deleted.

XV.4 Every version in a lineage SHALL remain individually reproducible and discoverable, enabling infinite evolution without historical destruction.

---

## ARTICLE XVI — EVOLUTION REGISTRY

XVI.1 The Evolution Registry SHALL be the single canonical record of all amendments, evolution states, classifications, compatibility declarations, migrations, deprecations, and retirements.

XVI.2 The Evolution Registry SHALL enforce uniqueness: every amendment SHALL have exactly one canonical identity, and no duplicate amendment authority per lineage SHALL be admitted.

XVI.3 The Evolution Registry SHALL be append-only, content-addressed, and reconciled against repository truth at boot.

XVI.4 An amendment absent from the Registry SHALL be deemed non-existent, and reliance upon it IS PROHIBITED.

XVI.5 The Evolution Registry SHALL be operational memory and SHALL hold no authority over constitutional content.

### B.5 Open-World Evolution

B.5.2 **No finite architectural boundary SHALL exist.** Expansion IS unbounded in count (`CMG-000001` LXXVI.5); the sixteen unboundedness axes are certified at `03-CONSTITUTIONAL-UNBOUNDEDNESS-CERTIFICATION.md` §2 and the twenty-three neutrality axes at `04-HIDDEN-FINITE-ASSUMPTION-CERTIFICATION.md` §2.

B.5.3 The **only** bound upon growth SHALL be **evidence**: a construct exists constitutionally when it is reduced, validated, registered, and certified (`05-CONSTITUTIONAL-CLOSURE-CERTIFICATION.md` §2). This bounds *legitimacy*, never *quantity*, and SHALL NOT be cited as a finite ceiling.

B.5.4 This Addendum SHALL NOT assume the continued existence of any present technology, repository, language, model, organization, or civilization (`CMG-000001` LXXVII.7). Every obligation it states is expressed in terms of authority, concern, identity, and record.
