> This document is normative constitutional law. It defines HOW the Program operates. It derives from CEP-000 (which defines WHY the Program exists) and SHALL NOT repeat the Charter. Where a principle is established in CEP-000, this Constitution references it and legislates its operation rather than restating it.

---

## PREAMBLE

P.1 This Constitution IS the supreme operational governing instrument of the UCOS Ω∞ Constitutional Engineering Program.

P.2 This Constitution derives its authority from CEP-000 and SHALL remain subordinate to CEP-000 on all matters of authority, purpose, and constitutional-content boundary.

P.3 This Constitution SHALL be supreme over every CEP stage, instrument, agent, checkpoint, ledger, and artifact on all matters of operation, execution, and enforcement.

P.4 This Constitution SHALL establish binding constitutional law for every remaining Stage of the Program.

---

## ARTICLE I — CONSTITUTIONAL LAWS

The following Constitutional Laws ARE supreme within the operation of the Program. No article, power, duty, or instrument SHALL contravene them.

I.1 LAW-1 (Precedence) — Authority SHALL flow in the order established by CEP-000 §5.5. No operation SHALL invert, bypass, or collapse this order.

I.2 LAW-2 (Evidence) — No operation SHALL assert as fact any claim not bound to reproducible evidence.

I.3 LAW-3 (Sequence) — No stage SHALL be entered before its entry criteria hold, and no stage SHALL be exited before its exit criteria hold.

I.4 LAW-4 (Single Canonicity) — Every constitutional concern SHALL have exactly one canonical definition and exactly one canonical owner at all times.

I.5 LAW-5 (Non-Bypass) — Governance, validation, and certification SHALL NEVER be bypassed.

I.6 LAW-6 (Governed Motion) — Forward motion SHALL be sequential along the governed graph; backward motion SHALL occur ONLY through the Amendment Model.

I.7 LAW-7 (Preservation) — Frozen work SHALL be immutable; it SHALL be changed ONLY by supersession.

I.8 LAW-8 (Determinism) — Every reproducible artifact SHALL regenerate byte-identically and SHALL be content-addressed.

I.9 LAW-9 (Recoverability) — Every interruption SHALL be recoverable without altering ratified or frozen results.

I.10 LAW-10 (Finality) — No work SHALL be declared final beyond the authority that ratified it; where finality authority is out-of-corpus, ratification SHALL be PROVISIONAL.

I.11 LAW-11 (Enforceability) — Every Law SHALL be enforceable at a gate, and a Law that cannot be checked SHALL be treated as unmet until proven met.

---

## ARTICLE II — CONSTITUTIONAL ARTICLES OF OPERATION

II.1 The Program SHALL operate exclusively through the stages, gates, and cross-cutting models defined in this Constitution.

II.2 Every operational act SHALL be attributable to exactly one stage and one write area.

II.3 Every operational act SHALL be recorded such that it is auditable and reproducible.

II.4 No operational act SHALL occur outside a stage that is in the EXECUTING state (Article VIII).

II.5 Every operational act SHALL conclude by advancing program state and emitting exactly one next authorized action.

---

## ARTICLE III — CONSTITUTIONAL POWERS

Powers are what the authorities of the Program MAY exercise. A power not granted herein DOES NOT exist.

III.1 Program Authority MAY define, sequence, and gate stages; register canonical definitions and owners; classify changes; and issue governed determinations.

III.2 Execution Authority MAY perform the single next authorized action within a stage that is EXECUTING, and MAY write only to that stage's declared write area.

III.3 Ratification Authority MAY confer the RATIFIED or PROVISIONAL status upon validated and certified work, and MAY reject work by emitting a finding.

III.4 Any authority MAY raise a finding, MAY record a deferral, and MAY halt the Program upon a failed criterion or a discovered contradiction.

III.5 No authority MAY exercise a power reserved to a higher tier, and no authority MAY create a new power for itself.

---

## ARTICLE IV — CONSTITUTIONAL DUTIES

Duties are what the authorities MUST discharge.

IV.1 Execution Authority MUST reconcile program state against repository truth at every boot, and MUST correct program state where it diverges, before any other act.

IV.2 Execution Authority MUST perform exactly the single next authorized action, MUST validate before progression, and MUST checkpoint at every gate transition.

IV.3 Program Authority MUST maintain the canonical register, MUST resolve ownership conflicts deterministically, and MUST ensure no control exists that closes no named risk.

IV.4 Every authority MUST route inconsistency findings to the Amendment Model and out-of-scope findings to the Deferral Register.

IV.5 Every authority MUST preserve frozen work as immutable and MUST prove preservation at every gate.

IV.6 Every authority MUST prove, not assert, determinism, traceability closure, and completeness before any exit, ratification, or freeze.

---

## ARTICLE V — CONSTITUTIONAL RIGHTS

Rights are guarantees that operation SHALL uphold.

V.1 Every stage SHALL have the right to halt and report rather than proceed when an entry criterion fails or a contradiction is discovered.

V.2 Every artifact SHALL have the right to reproducible evidence and to traceability rooting and closure.

V.3 Every ratified or frozen artifact SHALL have the right to immutability and to supersession-only change.

V.4 Every out-of-scope discovery SHALL have the right to be preserved in the Deferral Register and SHALL NOT be silently discarded.

V.5 Every superseded artifact SHALL have the right to retention for lineage and SHALL NOT be deleted.

V.6 Every authority SHALL have the right to refuse an act that would violate a Constitutional Law, and such refusal SHALL NOT constitute a defect.

---

## ARTICLE VI — CONSTITUTIONAL RESTRICTIONS

VI.1 It IS PROHIBITED to author, alter, or decide constitutional content within any governance instrument (per CEP-000 §32.1).

VI.2 It IS PROHIBITED to select, mandate, or embed any technology, vendor, product, platform, database, cloud, programming language, or AI model.

VI.3 It IS PROHIBITED to skip a gate, bypass a control, or traverse a feedback edge without a logged finding and an amendment decision.

VI.4 It IS PROHIBITED to mutate frozen work in place, to delete superseded lineage, or to perform destructive operations against shared or frozen state.

VI.5 It IS PROHIBITED to treat operational memory as constitutional corpus.

VI.6 It IS PROHIBITED to declare completion, ratification, or freeze while any blocking finding, orphan, drift, or unresolved deferral exists.

---

## ARTICLE VII — CONSTITUTIONAL INVARIANTS

Invariants SHALL hold at every point of Program execution.

VII.1 INV-1 — Program state matches repository truth, or the Program is halted for reconciliation.

VII.2 INV-2 — Exactly one canonical definition and one canonical owner exist per concern.

VII.3 INV-3 — The forward stage graph is acyclic and every dependency is documented.

VII.4 INV-4 — No frozen artifact has been mutated in place.

VII.5 INV-5 — Every emitted claim is bound to reproducible evidence.

VII.6 INV-6 — Exactly one next authorized action is defined at every checkpoint.

VII.7 INV-7 — No blocking finding is unrouted; no out-of-scope finding is unrecorded.

VII.8 INV-8 — Every reproducible artifact is content-addressed and regenerates byte-identically.

---

## ARTICLE VIII — CONSTITUTIONAL STATE MODEL

VIII.1 Artifact states SHALL be: DRAFTED, VALIDATED, CERTIFIED, RATIFIED, PROVISIONAL, FROZEN, SUPERSEDED, DEFERRED.

VIII.2 Legal artifact transitions SHALL be:
- DRAFTED → VALIDATED (upon Validation Model pass).
- VALIDATED → CERTIFIED (upon Certification Model pass).
- CERTIFIED → RATIFIED (in-corpus ratification) OR CERTIFIED → PROVISIONAL (out-of-corpus finality pending).
- RATIFIED → FROZEN, and PROVISIONAL → FROZEN (engineering baseline), upon Freeze Model preconditions.
- RATIFIED, PROVISIONAL, or FROZEN → SUPERSEDED (via Amendment Model only).
- DRAFTED → DEFERRED (out-of-scope, recorded in the Deferral Register).

VIII.3 Any transition not enumerated in VIII.2 IS PROHIBITED.

VIII.4 Stage states SHALL be: NOT_ENTERED, ENTERED, EXECUTING, VALIDATED, GATED, CHECKPOINTED, EXITED, RE_ENTERED.

VIII.5 A stage SHALL advance NOT_ENTERED → ENTERED (entry criteria hold) → EXECUTING → VALIDATED → GATED (exit criteria hold) → CHECKPOINTED → EXITED; RE_ENTERED SHALL be reachable ONLY from the Amendment Model.

VIII.6 Program states SHALL be: INITIALIZED, ADVANCING, HALTED, AMENDING, COMPLETE. HALTED SHALL be entered on any failed criterion or contradiction and SHALL be exited only upon remediation.

---

## ARTICLE IX — CONSTITUTIONAL EXECUTION MODEL

IX.1 Execution SHALL follow one uniform lifecycle for every stage: boot and reconcile; enter under entry criteria; execute the single authorized action within the declared write area; validate; certify where applicable; gate under exit criteria; checkpoint and emit the next authorized action; route findings and deferrals.

IX.2 Execution SHALL be autonomous: the next authorized action SHALL be a function of program state, not of conversational instruction (per CEP-000 §19).

IX.3 Execution SHALL be single-threaded per action: no second action SHALL begin before the current action is validated, gated, and checkpointed.

IX.4 Execution SHALL halt and report upon any failed entry criterion, failed exit criterion, or discovered contradiction, and SHALL NOT proceed until the Program returns to ADVANCING.

---

## ARTICLE X — CONSTITUTIONAL GOVERNANCE MODEL

X.1 Governance SHALL operate through the tiered authority of CEP-000 §5 with the decision partition of CEP-000 §20.

X.2 Ownership conflicts SHALL be resolved by the earliest ratified definition prevailing; the conflict SHALL be logged and the loser superseded or deferred.

X.3 Governance decisions SHALL be recorded as governed determinations that are addressable, reproducible, and auditable.

X.4 No governance control SHALL exist that closes no named risk, and no control SHALL be waived under operational pressure.

---

## ARTICLE XI — CONSTITUTIONAL VALIDATION MODEL

XI.1 Validation SHALL verify completeness, consistency, evidence-binding, traceability closure, determinism, preservation, and scope conformance (operationalizing CEP-000 §26).

XI.2 A gate SHALL yield exactly PASS or BLOCKED; a non-blocking observation SHALL be recorded separately and SHALL NEVER convert BLOCKED to PASS.

XI.3 Validation SHALL be re-run in full upon any amendment of the validated artifact.

XI.4 No stage SHALL advance while any blocking validation check against it fails.

---

## ARTICLE XII — CONSTITUTIONAL CERTIFICATION MODEL

XII.1 Certification SHALL rest exclusively on validation evidence and determinism proof and SHALL introduce no new content.

XII.2 Certification SHALL be content-addressed, reproducible, and append-only.

XII.3 Certification SHALL be revoked upon amendment of the certified artifact and SHALL be re-established only through re-validation.

XII.4 No artifact SHALL be certified while any blocking validation check against it fails.

---

## ARTICLE XIII — CONSTITUTIONAL RATIFICATION MODEL

XIII.1 Ratification SHALL consume only validation and certification evidence and SHALL introduce no new content.

XIII.2 Ratification authority SHALL be located; in-corpus authority SHALL confer RATIFIED; out-of-corpus or unavailable finality SHALL confer PROVISIONAL, non-blocking to engineering and blocking only to declared constitutional finality (per CEP-000 §28).

XIII.3 Ratification rejection SHALL emit a governed feedback finding into the Amendment Model and SHALL NEVER cause a silent modification.

XIII.4 Ratification records SHALL be append-only and content-addressed, and ratification SHALL NEVER be self-conferred by Execution Authority.

---

## ARTICLE XIV — CONSTITUTIONAL FREEZE MODEL

XIV.1 Freeze SHALL be established only when completeness, validation, certification, determinism, traceability closure, and ratification preconditions are satisfied.

XIV.2 Freeze baselines SHALL be content-addressed and reproducible so that drift is detectable.

XIV.3 Frozen work SHALL be immutable and SHALL be changed ONLY by supersession under the Amendment Model.

XIV.4 Freeze SHALL NEVER be applied to work carrying an unresolved blocking finding.

---

## ARTICLE XV — CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT MODEL

XV.1 Amendment SHALL be the sole lawful path for changing ratified or frozen work.

XV.2 Amendment SHALL proceed as proposal, evidence, impact analysis, re-validation, re-ratification, and supersession, and SHALL be append-only.

XV.3 Amendment SHALL be raised only upon a logged inconsistency finding or a ratification rejection.

XV.4 A superseded artifact SHALL be retained and marked superseded; prior ratification SHALL NOT carry over to the amended artifact.

XV.5 Amendment SHALL trigger a RE_ENTERED stage state for every stage whose frozen output is affected.

---

## ARTICLE XVI — CONSTITUTIONAL COMPLIANCE MODEL

XVI.1 Every stage and instrument IS REQUIRED to comply with this Constitution and with CEP-000.

XVI.2 Compliance SHALL be verified at every gate and proven by evidence and traceability, never asserted.

XVI.3 Non-compliance SHALL place the Program in HALTED until remediated or superseded.

XVI.4 No stage SHALL declare completion while an unresolved compliance failure exists against it.

---

## ARTICLE XVII — CONSTITUTIONAL AUDIT MODEL

XVII.1 Every governed act, finding, deferral, determination, ratification, and freeze SHALL be recorded in an append-only, content-addressed record.

XVII.2 Audit records SHALL be sufficient to reconstruct the Program's state and history deterministically.

XVII.3 Audit records SHALL be operational memory and SHALL NEVER enter the constitutional corpus.

XVII.4 An act that cannot be audited SHALL be treated as if it did not lawfully occur.

---

## ARTICLE XVIII — CONSTITUTIONAL TRACEABILITY MODEL

XVIII.1 Every artifact SHALL trace upward to its authorizing ratified definition and downward to its reproducible evidence (operationalizing CEP-000 §16).

XVIII.2 Traceability SHALL be rooted and closed with zero orphans and SHALL be verified at every gate, never deferred to freeze.

XVIII.3 Traceability links SHALL be typed and addressable.

XVIII.4 A discovered orphan SHALL place the Program in HALTED until the orphan is rooted, closed, or deferred.

---

## ARTICLE XIX — CONSTITUTIONAL EVIDENCE MODEL

XIX.1 Evidence SHALL be addressable, reproducible, content-addressed, and bound to the claim it substantiates (operationalizing CEP-000 §15).

XIX.2 Evidence SHALL reside in a declared evidence area and SHALL be referenced by claims, never inlined into constitutional content.

XIX.3 Evidence gathered during discovery SHALL be interpretation-free; interpretation SHALL occur only during reconciliation and definition.

XIX.4 A claim lacking bound evidence SHALL be marked unverified and SHALL NOT be treated as fact.

---

## ARTICLE XX — CONSTITUTIONAL DETERMINISM MODEL

XX.1 Determinism SHALL be engineered through content addressing, canonical serialization, and canonical ordering (operationalizing CEP-000 §18).

XX.2 Every validation gate SHALL require reproducible outputs to regenerate byte-identically.

XX.3 Regeneration SHALL be idempotent; it SHALL NEVER change an artifact's content or address.

XX.4 A determinism failure SHALL place the Program in HALTED until the non-determinism is eliminated.

---

## ARTICLE XXI — CONSTITUTIONAL RECOVERY MODEL

XXI.1 On every boot, program state SHALL be reconciled against repository truth, and repository truth SHALL prevail on divergence.

XXI.2 Interruption SHALL be classified and resolved: pre-write resumes without rollback; mid-write is discarded or completed deterministically and never left partial as truth; post-write and pre-checkpoint advances the checkpoint without regeneration.

XXI.3 Only unratified, unfrozen work MAY be rolled back; ratified and frozen work SHALL be changed only by supersession.

XXI.4 Recovery SHALL be non-destructive and SHALL prefer detect-and-continue over delete-and-redo.

---

## ARTICLE XXII — CONSTITUTIONAL COMPLETION MODEL

XXII.1 Stage completion SHALL require satisfaction of that stage's exit criteria and SHALL be distinct from readiness.

XXII.2 Program completion SHALL require satisfaction of the Success Definition of CEP-000 §31.

XXII.3 No completion SHALL be declared while any blocking finding, orphan, drift, or unresolved deferral remains.

XXII.4 Every declaration of completion SHALL be provable by evidence and traceability.

---

## ARTICLE XXIII — CONSTITUTIONAL ENFORCEMENT MODEL

XXIII.1 Enforcement SHALL occur at gates; a gate SHALL refuse progression when any applicable Law, Invariant, or blocking criterion is unmet.

XXIII.2 A violation SHALL place the Program in HALTED, emit a finding, and require remediation or supersession before ADVANCING resumes.

XXIII.3 Enforcement SHALL NEVER be waived, deferred, or overridden by any authority tier.

XXIII.4 An unenforceable obligation SHALL be treated as unmet until it is made enforceable.

XXIII.5 Enforcement records SHALL be append-only, content-addressed, and auditable.

---

## ARTICLE XXIV — SUPREMACY, DERIVATION, AND CONFLICT

XXIV.1 This Constitution IS supreme over every subordinate CEP instrument and over all execution.

XXIV.2 This Constitution derives its authority from CEP-000 and IS subordinate to CEP-000 on matters of authority, purpose, and constitutional-content boundary.

XXIV.3 Where this Constitution conflicts with CEP-000, CEP-000 SHALL govern. Where a subordinate CEP instrument conflicts with this Constitution, this Constitution SHALL govern.

XXIV.4 This Constitution SHALL bind every remaining Stage of the Program until amended or frozen under its own Models.

---

## ARTICLE XXV — VERSIONING

XXV.1 This Constitution carries an explicit version; the present version IS 1.0.

XXV.2 Any change SHALL occur only through the Amendment Model and SHALL increment the version.

XXV.3 A superseded version SHALL be retained for lineage and marked superseded; a frozen version SHALL be immutable and content-addressed.

XXV.4 Every subordinate CEP instrument SHALL cite the Constitution version under which it was produced.
