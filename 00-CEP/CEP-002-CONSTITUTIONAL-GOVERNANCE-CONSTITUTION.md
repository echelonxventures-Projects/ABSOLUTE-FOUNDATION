> This document is normative constitutional law legislating governance only. It inherits and references CEP-000 and CEP-001 and SHALL NOT rewrite, duplicate, or restate them. It SHALL NOT legislate execution, validation, certification method, implementation, repository discovery, or engineering methods; those are reserved to CEP-001 and to later constitutions.

---

## PREAMBLE

P.1 This instrument IS the permanent constitutional governance instrument of the UCOS Ω∞ Constitutional Engineering Program.

P.2 This instrument SHALL legislate how governance operates, and SHALL NOT legislate why the Program exists (reserved to CEP-000) or how the Program executes (reserved to CEP-001 and later constitutions).

P.3 This instrument SHALL bind every governance act of the Program until amended or frozen under its own rules.

---

## ARTICLE 1 — GOVERNANCE AUTHORITY

1.1 Governance Authority SHALL vest in Program Authority as defined by CEP-000 §5 (Tier 2).

1.2 Governance Authority SHALL be the sole authority empowered to assign jurisdictions, register owners, issue governed determinations, and resolve governance disputes and conflicts.

1.3 Governance Authority SHALL NOT extend to constitutional content, to execution, or to any matter reserved to another tier or a later constitution.

1.4 Governance Authority SHALL NEVER self-elevate, and SHALL NEVER create a governance power not established by this instrument.

---

## ARTICLE 2 — GOVERNANCE HIERARCHY

2.1 The governance hierarchy SHALL be strictly ordered and acyclic.

2.2 The governance hierarchy SHALL be, in descending precedence: Constitutional Authority; Governance Authority; delegated Governance Stewards; Execution Authority acting within a governed determination.

2.3 A lower level of the hierarchy SHALL NOT govern, overrule, or bind a higher level.

2.4 No cycle SHALL exist in the governance hierarchy; a governance relationship that would form a cycle IS PROHIBITED.

2.5 Every governance role SHALL occupy exactly one position in the hierarchy.

---

## ARTICLE 3 — CONSTITUTIONAL AUTHORITY MODEL

3.1 Constitutional Authority, as defined by CEP-000 §5.5 Tier 1, SHALL be recognized within governance as supreme on matters of constitutional content and finality.

3.2 Governance SHALL exercise no power over constitutional content and SHALL treat Constitutional Authority as superior on all such matters.

3.3 Where final constitutional finality requires an authority residing outside the corpus, governance SHALL recognize that authority as superior for finality and SHALL treat governance rulings dependent on it as PROVISIONAL per CEP-001 Article XIII.

3.4 Governance SHALL NOT assert, simulate, or substitute for Constitutional Authority.

---

## ARTICLE 4 — GOVERNANCE JURISDICTIONS

4.1 A jurisdiction SHALL be a bounded area of governance responsibility assigned to exactly one authority or steward.

4.2 Every jurisdiction SHALL be registered in the Governance Registry (Article 19) with its boundary, its owner, and its authorizing determination.

4.3 Jurisdictions SHALL NOT overlap. Two jurisdictions asserting the same responsibility SHALL be resolved under Article 23.

4.4 An act performed outside the actor's jurisdiction IS PROHIBITED and SHALL be void.

4.5 A jurisdiction without a registered owner SHALL be inactive, and no act SHALL be performed under it until an owner is registered.

---

## ARTICLE 5 — GOVERNANCE DOMAINS

5.1 The domains subject to governance SHALL be: canonical ownership; jurisdiction assignment; scope determination; finding disposition; deferral disposition; the Governance Registry; stewardship; dispute and conflict resolution; and governance change control.

5.2 Each domain SHALL fall within exactly one jurisdiction.

5.3 Governance SHALL NOT extend a domain to encompass execution, validation, certification method, implementation, discovery, or engineering method.

5.4 A matter that does not fall within a registered domain SHALL be recorded as a deferral and SHALL NOT be governed until a domain and jurisdiction are established.

---

## ARTICLE 6 — GOVERNANCE RESPONSIBILITIES

6.1 Governance SHALL be responsible for maintaining exactly one canonical owner and one canonical definition per concern, per CEP-000 §21 and CEP-001 LAW-4.

6.2 Governance SHALL be responsible for maintaining the Governance Registry as accurate, complete, and reconciled against repository truth.

6.3 Governance SHALL be responsible for disposing of every finding and every deferral routed to it.

6.4 Governance SHALL be responsible for resolving every dispute and conflict deterministically and for recording each resolution.

6.5 Governance SHALL be responsible for ensuring no governance control exists that closes no named risk.

---

## ARTICLE 7 — GOVERNANCE POWERS

7.1 Governance MAY assign, re-assign, activate, and deactivate jurisdictions.

7.2 Governance MAY register, transfer, and retire canonical owners and definitions.

7.3 Governance MAY issue governed determinations, and MAY halt the Program upon a governance violation.

7.4 Governance MAY delegate a power under Article 13, and MAY revoke any delegation.

7.5 A power not granted by this Article DOES NOT exist for governance.

---

## ARTICLE 8 — GOVERNANCE RESTRICTIONS

8.1 Governance SHALL NOT author, alter, or decide constitutional content.

8.2 Governance SHALL NOT legislate or perform execution, validation, certification method, implementation, discovery, or engineering method.

8.3 Governance SHALL NOT create overlapping jurisdictions, duplicate ownership, or conflicting terminology.

8.4 Governance SHALL NOT act outside a registered jurisdiction and domain.

8.5 Governance SHALL NOT waive, defer, or override enforcement.

8.6 Governance SHALL NOT delete registry, determination, dispute, or audit lineage.

---

## ARTICLE 9 — GOVERNANCE DUTIES

9.1 Governance MUST record every governance act as a governed determination that is addressable, reproducible, and auditable.

9.2 Governance MUST reconcile the Governance Registry against repository truth at every boot and MUST correct divergence before any governance act.

9.3 Governance MUST resolve ownership contests by the deterministic rule of Article 14 and MUST record the outcome.

9.4 Governance MUST route matters beyond its scope to the Deferral Register and MUST NOT act upon them.

9.5 Governance MUST prove, not assert, that no jurisdiction overlaps and no ownership is duplicated at every governance gate.

---

## ARTICLE 10 — DECISION AUTHORITY MODEL

10.1 Decision authority SHALL be partitioned per CEP-000 §20 without overlap.

10.2 A decision SHALL be made at the lowest hierarchy level competent for its domain, and SHALL rise only by escalation (Article 12).

10.3 Every decision SHALL resolve to exactly one recorded outcome.

10.4 A decision made outside the decider's authority SHALL be void and SHALL be re-decided by the competent authority.

10.5 No decision SHALL be left unresolved once raised; an unresolved decision SHALL place the Program in HALTED until decided or deferred.

---

## ARTICLE 11 — DECISION HIERARCHY

11.1 Decisions SHALL be classified as routine, governance, constitutional, or finality.

11.2 Routine decisions SHALL be decided by Execution Authority within a governed determination; governance decisions by Governance Authority; constitutional decisions by Constitutional Authority; finality decisions by Ratification Authority per CEP-001 Article XIII.

11.3 A decision of a higher class SHALL NOT be decided by a lower authority.

11.4 A decision spanning multiple classes SHALL be decided at the highest class it touches.

---

## ARTICLE 12 — ESCALATION MODEL

12.1 An unresolved or contested decision SHALL escalate to the next higher competent authority in the governance hierarchy.

12.2 Escalation SHALL be recorded with its trigger, its origin level, and its destination level.

12.3 Escalation SHALL terminate at the highest competent authority; where that authority is out-of-corpus, the matter SHALL be recorded PROVISIONAL and SHALL be non-blocking to engineering per CEP-001 Article XIII.

12.4 Escalation SHALL NOT skip a level, and SHALL NOT descend.

12.5 An escalation that cannot terminate within the corpus SHALL be recorded as an open matter and SHALL be surfaced at every readiness assessment.

---

## ARTICLE 13 — DELEGATION MODEL

13.1 Delegation SHALL flow only downward in the governance hierarchy.

13.2 A delegated power SHALL be bounded, recorded, and revocable, and SHALL NOT exceed the delegator's own power.

13.3 Ultimate accountability SHALL NOT be delegated; a delegator SHALL remain accountable for delegated acts.

13.4 A delegate SHALL NOT sub-delegate beyond the bounds recorded by the delegator.

13.5 Revocation of a delegation SHALL be recorded and SHALL take effect upon record.

---

## ARTICLE 14 — CANONICAL OWNERSHIP GOVERNANCE

14.1 Every concern SHALL have exactly one canonical owner registered in the Governance Registry.

14.2 Ownership SHALL be assigned by governed determination and SHALL NEVER be implied.

14.3 An ownership contest SHALL be resolved by the earliest ratified definition prevailing; the contest SHALL be logged and the superseded claim recorded, never silently discarded (per CEP-000 §21, CEP-001 Article X).

14.4 Ownership transfer SHALL occur only by governed determination, SHALL be recorded, and SHALL preserve the prior owner in lineage.

14.5 A concern without a registered owner SHALL NOT progress.

---

## ARTICLE 15 — STEWARDSHIP MODEL

15.1 Stewardship SHALL be the delegated custody of a jurisdiction, register, ledger, or determination record.

15.2 A steward SHALL preserve the integrity, completeness, and reconciliation of the entrusted record and SHALL NOT alter it beyond the steward's recorded jurisdiction.

15.3 Stewardship SHALL be assignable and revocable only by Governance Authority.

15.4 A steward SHALL NOT confer authority, decide constitutional content, or exceed delegated bounds.

15.5 Loss or ambiguity of stewardship SHALL place the affected record under Governance Authority until stewardship is re-established.

---

## ARTICLE 16 — CONSTITUTIONAL REVIEW MODEL

16.1 Governance instruments SHALL be subject to review for internal consistency, authority consistency, cross-reference consistency, and terminology consistency.

16.2 Review SHALL be triggered at every governance gate and upon any amendment of a governance instrument.

16.3 Review SHALL be evidentiary; a review conclusion SHALL be provable by reference and SHALL NOT be asserted.

16.4 A review that discovers an inconsistency SHALL emit a finding and SHALL place the affected matter in HALTED until resolved or superseded.

16.5 Review SHALL introduce no new governance content; it SHALL only verify.

---

## ARTICLE 17 — GOVERNANCE COMPLIANCE MODEL

17.1 Every governance act IS REQUIRED to comply with this instrument, CEP-001, and CEP-000.

17.2 Governance compliance SHALL be verified at every governance gate and proven by evidence and traceability, never asserted.

17.3 A governance non-compliance SHALL place the Program in HALTED until remediated or superseded.

17.4 No governance matter SHALL be declared complete while an unresolved governance compliance failure exists against it.

---

## ARTICLE 18 — GOVERNANCE AUDIT MODEL

18.1 Every governed determination, jurisdiction change, ownership change, delegation, escalation, dispute, conflict resolution, and review SHALL be recorded in an append-only, content-addressed record, per CEP-001 Article XVII.

18.2 Governance audit records SHALL be sufficient to reconstruct the governance state and history deterministically.

18.3 Governance audit records SHALL be operational memory and SHALL NEVER enter the constitutional corpus.

18.4 A governance act that cannot be audited SHALL be treated as if it did not lawfully occur.

---

## ARTICLE 19 — GOVERNANCE REGISTRY MODEL

19.1 The Governance Registry SHALL be the single canonical record of jurisdictions, canonical owners, canonical definitions, delegations, and stewardships.

19.2 The Governance Registry SHALL enforce uniqueness: no duplicate owner, no overlapping jurisdiction, and no conflicting terminology SHALL be admitted.

19.3 The Governance Registry SHALL be append-only in lineage, content-addressed, and reconciled against repository truth at every boot.

19.4 An entry absent from the Registry SHALL be deemed ungoverned; an act under an ungoverned entry IS PROHIBITED.

19.5 The Governance Registry SHALL be operational memory and SHALL hold no authority over constitutional content.

---

## ARTICLE 20 — GOVERNANCE CHANGE CONTROL

20.1 Every change to a governance instrument, jurisdiction, ownership, delegation, or stewardship SHALL be classified as additive, corrective, or superseding, per CEP-000 §24.

20.2 Additive governance changes SHALL NOT alter existing frozen governance content.

20.3 Corrective governance changes to non-frozen governance content SHALL be re-reviewed before progression.

20.4 Superseding governance changes to frozen governance content SHALL occur only through the Governance Amendment Process (Article 21) and SHALL be append-only.

20.5 Every governance change SHALL be recorded with its classification, justification, and evidence.

---

## ARTICLE 21 — GOVERNANCE AMENDMENT PROCESS

21.1 Amendment of a governance instrument SHALL follow the general Amendment Model of CEP-001 Article XV, specialized for governance content.

21.2 Governance amendment SHALL proceed as proposal, evidence, impact analysis, governance review, re-ratification, and supersession, and SHALL be append-only.

21.3 Governance amendment SHALL be raised only upon a logged inconsistency finding, a dispute ruling, or a conflict resolution requiring change.

21.4 A superseded governance instrument SHALL be retained for lineage and marked superseded; prior ratification SHALL NOT carry over.

21.5 This instrument MAY be amended only under this Article and only while it is not frozen.

---

## ARTICLE 22 — GOVERNANCE DISPUTE RESOLUTION

22.1 A dispute SHALL be a contested matter between parties or claims within governance.

22.2 A dispute SHALL be raised as a recorded finding and SHALL be resolved by the lowest competent authority, escalating under Article 12 where unresolved.

22.3 Dispute resolution SHALL be deterministic and evidence-based and SHALL resolve to exactly one recorded outcome.

22.4 A dispute SHALL NOT be closed by silence, by lapse, or by unilateral action of a party to it.

22.5 An unresolved dispute SHALL place the affected matter in HALTED until resolved or deferred.

---

## ARTICLE 23 — GOVERNANCE CONFLICT RESOLUTION

23.1 A conflict SHALL be an inconsistency between governance instruments, jurisdictions, or rules.

23.2 Conflicts SHALL be resolved by precedence: CEP-000 over CEP-001 over this instrument over subordinate governance instruments; and, among instruments of equal rank, the earliest ratified definition prevailing.

23.3 Overlapping jurisdictions SHALL be resolved by re-assignment such that exactly one owner remains; the superseded assignment SHALL be recorded.

23.4 Conflict resolution SHALL be deterministic and SHALL resolve to exactly one recorded outcome.

23.5 A conflict that cannot be resolved by precedence SHALL escalate under Article 12.

---

## ARTICLE 24 — GOVERNANCE ENFORCEMENT

24.1 Enforcement of governance rulings SHALL occur at governance gates through the general Enforcement Model of CEP-001 Article XXIII.

24.2 A governance violation SHALL place the Program in HALTED, emit a finding, and require remediation or supersession before ADVANCING resumes.

24.3 A void act (an act outside jurisdiction, authority, or domain) SHALL have no effect and SHALL be recorded as void.

24.4 Governance enforcement SHALL NEVER be waived, deferred, or overridden by any authority tier.

24.5 Governance enforcement records SHALL be append-only, content-addressed, and auditable.

---

## ARTICLE 25 — GOVERNANCE COMPLETION RULES

25.1 Governance for a stage SHALL be complete when every jurisdiction, owner, and definition it requires is registered; every finding and deferral routed to governance is disposed; and every dispute and conflict against it is resolved or deferred.

25.2 Governance completion SHALL be distinct from stage completion and SHALL NOT authorize a successor stage.

25.3 No governance completion SHALL be declared while an overlapping jurisdiction, duplicated owner, unresolved dispute, unresolved conflict, or unrouted finding exists.

25.4 Every declaration of governance completion SHALL be provable by evidence and traceability.

---

## ARTICLE 26 — SUPREMACY, DERIVATION, AND VERSIONING

26.1 This instrument IS supreme over all governance operation and over every subordinate governance instrument.

26.2 This instrument derives from CEP-000 and CEP-001 and IS subordinate to both; conflicts resolve in favor of the higher instrument per Article 23.2.

26.3 This instrument carries an explicit version; the present version IS 1.2 (amended from 1.0 by CEP-002-AMD-001 and from 1.1 by CEP-002-AMD-002 under Article 21, both recorded in the AMENDMENT RECORDS below), and any change SHALL occur only through Article 21 and SHALL increment the version.

26.4 A superseded version SHALL be retained for lineage; a frozen version SHALL be immutable and content-addressed.

26.5 Every subordinate governance instrument SHALL cite the version of this instrument under which it was produced.

## ARTICLE 27 — DEFERRAL REGISTER LIFECYCLE

> Added by **CEP-002-AMD-001** under Article 21. This Article IS the operative expression of the deferral-disposition domain of Article 5.1, the routing duty of Article 9.4, and the recording obligation of Article 5.4. It creates no register, no store, and no authority.

### Ownership

27.1 The **Deferral Register lifecycle** — its entry states, entry conditions, exit conditions, review cadence, and custody — IS a governance concern within the deferral-disposition domain of Article 5.1, and IS owned by this instrument. This Article IS its single canonical definition; no other instrument SHALL define it (CEP-001 LAW-4, INV-2).

27.2 The **canonical owner** of the concern IS Governance Authority (Article 1.2), which assigns and registers owners by governed determination (Articles 7.2, 14.2). Day-to-day custody SHALL be held by a **Governance Steward** under Article 15, assignable and revocable only by Governance Authority (15.3). Loss or ambiguity of stewardship SHALL place the Register under Governance Authority until stewardship is re-established (15.5).

27.3 This Article creates **no new register and no new storage**. The Deferral Register IS the single append-only record already established and referenced by the located corpus — CEP-000 §7.4, §9.3, §9.5, §34.9, §35.4 and CEP-001 VIII.2, XVII.1, XVIII.4, XXII.3 — and IS operational memory under Article 18.3 and CEP-001 XVII.3. It SHALL be append-only in lineage, content-addressed, and reconciled against repository truth at every boot (19.3, 9.2). A second deferral register, deferral store, deferral pipeline, or deferral gate IS PROHIBITED (8.3, CEP-001 VII.2).

### Entry record

27.4 Every Deferral Register entry SHALL record, at minimum: its identity; the matter deferred; the deferring authority and the jurisdiction under which it acted; the basis for deferral; the **deferring condition** whose persistence justifies continued deferral; the owner to whom the matter returns on resolution; the **declared review point**; the current entry state; and the reference to its bound evidence (9.1, CEP-001 XIX.1). An entry missing any of these IS incomplete and SHALL be treated as undispositioned.

27.5 An entry SHALL NOT be deleted, overwritten, or silently withdrawn (8.6, 18.1). Exit terminates effect; the record persists and remains traversable by lineage.

### Entry conditions

27.6 A matter SHALL be entered in the Deferral Register when, and only when, one of the following holds:
- (a) it IS a discovery or finding outside the scope of the current stage (CEP-000 §7.4, §9.5; CEP-001 INV-7);
- (b) it falls within no registered governance domain (5.4);
- (c) it IS a matter knowingly not decided, or a decision raised and not resolved (10.5, 22.5);
- (d) it IS a review or dispute finding dispositioned as deferred rather than accepted or rejected (16.4, 22.5);
- (e) it IS an orphan routed for deferral rather than rooting or closure (CEP-001 XVIII.4);
- (f) an artifact transitions `DRAFTED → DEFERRED` under CEP-001 VIII.2.

27.7 Where an entry arises under 27.6(f), the entry and the deferred artifact SHALL be **in one-to-one correspondence** (CEP-001 VIII.7). An artifact in DEFERRED without an entry, or an entry claiming a DEFERRED artifact that is in another state, IS a governance non-compliance under 17.3.

27.8 A matter knowingly not decided IS a **deferral**; a matter decided to depart from a rule IS an **exception**. The two SHALL NOT be conflated, and an exception SHALL NOT be recorded in the Deferral Register in place of the disposition its own owner requires. Nothing in this Article permits governance to waive, defer, or override enforcement (8.5, 24.4).

### Entry states and transitions

27.9 The Deferral Register entry states SHALL be exactly: **RECORDED**, **UNDER-REVIEW**, **RESOLVED**, **WITHDRAWN**. The initial state SHALL be RECORDED. The terminal states SHALL be RESOLVED and WITHDRAWN.

27.10 The legal entry transitions SHALL be exactly:
- `RECORDED → UNDER-REVIEW` — the declared review point is reached, or a governance gate, stage boundary, or readiness assessment triggers review (27.13).
- `UNDER-REVIEW → RECORDED` — review completes and the deferral IS upheld because the deferring condition still holds; a **new** declared review point SHALL be recorded in the same act, and the prior review SHALL be retained.
- `UNDER-REVIEW → RESOLVED` — review determines the deferring condition no longer holds; the matter returns to its recorded owner.
- `UNDER-REVIEW → WITHDRAWN` — review determines the matter IS no longer required, or IS displaced by a later canonical definition.

27.11 Any entry transition not enumerated in 27.10 IS PROHIBITED. In particular, no entry SHALL pass from RECORDED directly to RESOLVED or WITHDRAWN: **every exit IS reviewed**. Every non-terminal state has at least one outgoing transition, and every state IS reachable from RECORDED.

27.12 Every exit SHALL be a **governed determination** — addressable, reproducible, auditable, and resolving to exactly one recorded outcome (9.1, 10.3). An entry SHALL NOT exit by silence, by lapse of time, by unilateral action of the deferring party, or by absence of objection (22.4, and by the standard of 24.4 that enforcement IS never waived). Where the entry corresponds to a deferred artifact under 27.7, the exit SHALL be accompanied by the paired artifact transition of CEP-001 VIII.2: `DEFERRED → DRAFTED` on RESOLVED, and `DEFERRED → SUPERSEDED` on WITHDRAWN.

### Review cadence and detection

27.13 Every entry in RECORDED SHALL carry a declared review point, and review SHALL occur no later than the **earliest** of: the declared review point; the next governance gate (16.2); the next stage boundary; and the next readiness assessment (12.5).

27.14 A passed review point without a recorded review SHALL be **detected, not merely scheduled**. Detection SHALL occur at the governance gates already established by Article 24.1 and CEP-001 XXIII, and SHALL emit a finding that places the affected matter in HALTED until the review is performed (17.3, 24.2). No new gate, scheduler, or daemon IS created for this purpose.

27.15 Review SHALL be performed by an entity other than the deferring party where the deferring party is also the reviewer; in that case the review SHALL escalate one level under Article 12 (and by the standard of 22.4 that a party SHALL not close its own matter).

### Disposition, completion, and enforcement

27.16 An entry IS **disposed** for the purposes of 6.3, 9.4, and 25.1 when, and only when, it IS in RESOLVED or WITHDRAWN. An entry in RECORDED or UNDER-REVIEW IS an **unresolved deferral** for the purposes of CEP-001 XXII.3 and CEP-000 §31.7 (SD-6).

27.17 Governance completion for a stage SHALL NOT be declared while any entry routed to governance IS unresolved (25.1, 25.3), and no completion SHALL be declared while any unresolved deferral remains (CEP-001 XXII.3). This Article makes those obligations **satisfiable and decidable**: every entry has exactly two defined exits and a mandatory review that reaches them.

27.18 This Article legislates governance operation only. It SHALL NOT legislate the artifact state model (reserved to CEP-001 Article VIII), execution, validation, certification method, ratification, implementation, discovery, or engineering method (8.1, 8.2, P.2). Where this Article conflicts with CEP-001 or CEP-000, the higher instrument governs (23.2, 26.2) and the conflicting clause IS void to the extent of the conflict; severability IS structural.

## ARTICLE 28 — CONSTITUTIONAL DECISION ASSIMILATION

> Added by **CEP-002-AMD-002** under Article 21. This Article IS the operative expression of the decision-disposition duty of Article 10.3, the no-unresolved-decision rule of Article 10.5, and the recording obligation of Article 9.1. It creates no register, no store, no gate apparatus, and no authority.

### Ownership

28.1 **Constitutional Decision Assimilation** — the lifecycle every constitutionally agreed decision SHALL traverse, the disposition every such decision SHALL carry, and the evidence each disposition SHALL rest upon — IS a governance concern within the decision domain of Article 5.1, and IS owned by this instrument, which already owns decision authority (Article 10), the decision hierarchy (Article 11), and the duty that every decision resolve to exactly one recorded outcome (10.3). This Article IS its single canonical definition; no other instrument SHALL define it (CEP-001 LAW-4, INV-2).

28.2 The **canonical owner** of the concern IS Governance Authority (Article 1.2), which assigns and registers owners by governed determination (Articles 7.2, 14.2). Day-to-day custody SHALL be held by a **Governance Steward** under Article 15, assignable and revocable only by Governance Authority (15.3). Loss or ambiguity of stewardship SHALL place the concern under Governance Authority until stewardship is re-established (15.5).

28.3 This Article creates **no new register and no new storage**. The decision registers of the Program ARE the located registers already established and referenced by the corpus — the Constitutional Decision Register (`02-MASTER/UCOS-Ω∞-CONSTITUTIONAL-DECISION-REGISTER.md`), the architectural decision record set (`adr/`), the canonical decision store of the knowledge authority (`knowledge/decisions.json`), the Architectural Decision Assimilation Matrix (`03-ARCHITECTURAL-DECISION-ASSIMILATION-MATRIX.md`), and the append-only decision index of operational memory (`00-MASTER/MCP-004-MASTER-DECISIONS.md`, governed by `MCS-000`). A second decision register, decision store, decision pipeline, decision gate, or decision authority IS PROHIBITED (8.3, CEP-001 VII.2). This Article adds a **disposition and evidence obligation over the located registers**; it does not replace them.

### The assimilation obligation

28.4 **No constitutionally agreed decision SHALL remain only in conversation history.** An agreement that subsists only in the record of a discussion IS not a decision of the Program: it IS undispositioned matter, and SHALL be treated as an unresolved decision under 10.5 until it IS registered under this Article.

28.5 Repository Truth IS the sole admissible evidence that a decision exists and that it carries the disposition claimed for it. Conversational assertion, recollection, restatement, and summary are **NOT evidence** (9.1, and by the standard of 25.4 that every declaration be provable by evidence and traceability). A decision whose only trace is conversational IS, for the purposes of this instrument, **not recorded**.

28.6 Registration SHALL be made in the located register competent for the decision's class under 11.1, and SHALL thereafter be indexed in operational memory. Registration SHALL precede indexing; indexing SHALL NOT substitute for registration.

28.7 The obligation of 28.4 IS **total**. It binds every constitutionally agreed decision without regard to origin, size, apparent obviousness, or the seniority of the party that agreed it, and no decision IS exempt for being considered settled.

### The mandatory decision lifecycle

28.8 Every constitutionally agreed decision SHALL traverse the following stages, and SHALL occupy exactly one of them at any time. The stages SHALL be exactly:

- **DISCUSSION** — the matter is raised and examined; nothing is yet decided.
- **CONSTITUTIONAL AGREEMENT** — the competent authority of 11.2 agrees the decision.
- **DECISION REGISTRATION** — the decision is entered in its located register (28.3, 28.6).
- **REPOSITORY MAPPING** — the decision is mapped to the canonical owner, artefact, and location that will carry it, and its disposition IS determined (28.13).
- **IMPLEMENTATION** — the decision is realized in the repository.
- **VALIDATION** — the realization is validated by its located validation owner (CEP-004).
- **CERTIFICATION** — the validated realization is certified by its located certification owner (CEP-005).
- **REPOSITORY TRUTH UPDATE** — the register, the index, and the derived projections are reconciled to the realized state.
- **CLOSURE** — the decision is discharged and the record IS closed.

28.9 The initial stage SHALL be DISCUSSION. The terminal stage SHALL be CLOSURE. Every stage SHALL be reachable from DISCUSSION, and every non-terminal stage SHALL have at least one outgoing transition.

28.10 The lifecycle SHALL be traversed **in order**. No stage SHALL be skipped, and no stage SHALL be reached by descent, save that a decision dispositioned under 28.13(d) or 28.13(e) — whose disposition IS determined at REPOSITORY MAPPING and requires no realization — SHALL pass from REPOSITORY MAPPING directly to REPOSITORY TRUTH UPDATE and thence to CLOSURE.

28.11 The only backward transition SHALL be a **defect-driven reopening**: where a defect in a closed decision IS proven by evidence, the decision SHALL return to DISCUSSION, the prior record SHALL be retained, and the reopening SHALL be recorded with the proven defect as its trigger. A closed decision SHALL NOT be revisited absent such proof.

28.12 A decision SHALL NOT advance by silence, by lapse of time, by unilateral action of the deciding party, or by absence of objection (22.4, 24.4). Each advance SHALL be a governed determination resolving to exactly one recorded outcome (9.1, 10.3).

### The constitutional disposition model

28.13 Every constitutionally agreed decision SHALL carry **exactly one** disposition, drawn from the following closed set. The set IS closed; a disposition outside it IS void, and the set SHALL be extended only by amendment under Article 21.

- **(a) IMPLEMENTED** — the decision IS realized in the repository. Evidence SHALL be the located artefacts that carry the realization, and the decision SHALL have reached at least REPOSITORY TRUTH UPDATE.
- **(b) REPRESENTED BY AN EXISTING CANONICAL CAPABILITY** — the substance of the decision IS already discharged by a located canonical owner, and no further realization IS required. Evidence SHALL be the single canonical owner and its located artefact. Two canonical owners for one decision IS a duplication finding under 14.3.
- **(c) REGISTERED AS AN IMPLEMENTATION WORK PACKAGE** — the decision IS agreed but not yet realized. Evidence SHALL be a located work package carrying an owner, a constitutional route, and an acceptance condition. A work package IS a registration of work, not a deferral of decision, and its absence makes the disposition void.
- **(d) REJECTED WITH CONSTITUTIONAL JUSTIFICATION** — the decision IS declined. Evidence SHALL be the constitutional basis of the rejection, cited to the governing clause. A rejection without a cited basis IS not a disposition.
- **(e) SUPERSEDED** — the decision IS displaced by a later decision. Evidence SHALL be the identity of the superseding decision, which SHALL itself be registered and dispositioned. Supersession SHALL NOT be recorded against an unregistered successor, and the superseded record SHALL be retained (18.1).

28.14 A decision that carries **no** disposition, **more than one** disposition, or a disposition whose required evidence does not resolve in the repository IS **undispositioned**, and IS an unresolved decision under 10.5 for every purpose of this instrument.

28.15 A disposition SHALL NOT be assigned by silence or inference. It SHALL be recorded, append-only; a disposition IS never deleted or overwritten, and a changed disposition IS recorded as a new determination retaining the prior (8.6, 18.1).

28.16 Recording a matter in the Deferral Register under Article 27 IS **not** a disposition under 28.13, and SHALL NOT satisfy any obligation of this Article. Deferral suspends a matter; disposition resolves a decision. The two SHALL NOT be conflated (by the standard of 27.8).

### The Implementation Evidence Gate

28.17 The **Implementation Evidence Gate** IS a governance gate within Article 24.1. Its subject IS the completeness of decision disposition; its outcome IS OPEN or CLOSED; and its outcome SHALL be computed from Repository Truth, never asserted.

28.18 **No subsequent implementation programme, architectural work, or successor stage SHALL be authorized while any previously ratified or recorded decision IS undispositioned within the meaning of 28.14.** The Gate IS CLOSED while any such decision stands, and OPEN only when every previously ratified or recorded decision carries exactly one disposition under 28.13 supported by evidence that resolves in the repository.

28.19 The Gate SHALL be discharged **mechanically**, by the enforcement apparatus already established at Article 24.1 and CEP-001 XXIII. No new gate, pipeline, scheduler, or daemon IS created for this purpose: the Gate's executable expression IS a check bound in the located aggregate gate declaration, whose verdict IS the Gate's outcome.

28.20 A CLOSED Gate SHALL emit a finding and SHALL place the affected programme in HALTED until every undispositioned decision IS dispositioned (17.3, 24.2). The Gate SHALL NOT be waived, deferred, bypassed, or overridden, and no governance act SHALL reduce its scope (8.5, 24.4).

28.21 Governance completion SHALL NOT be declared, and no successor stage SHALL be authorized, while the Gate IS CLOSED (25.1, 25.3). This Article makes that obligation **decidable**: the decision population IS enumerable from the located registers, every member has exactly one disposition from a closed set, and every disposition has a mechanically resolvable evidence obligation.

28.22 This Article legislates governance operation only. It SHALL NOT legislate the artifact state model (reserved to CEP-001 Article VIII), execution, validation method, certification method, ratification, implementation, discovery, or engineering method (8.1, 8.2, P.2). Where this Article conflicts with CEP-001 or CEP-000, the higher instrument governs (23.2, 26.2) and the conflicting clause IS void to the extent of the conflict; severability IS structural.
