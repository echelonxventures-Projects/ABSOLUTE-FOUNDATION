> This document is normative constitutional law legislating governance only. It inherits and references CEP-000 and CEP-001 and SHALL NOT rewrite, duplicate, or restate them. It SHALL NOT legislate execution, validation, certification method, implementation, repository discovery, or engineering methods; those are reserved to CEP-001 and to later constitutions.

---

## PREAMBLE

P.1 This instrument IS the permanent constitutional governance instrument of the UCOS Ω∞ Constitutional Engineering Program.

P.2 This instrument SHALL legislate how governance operates, and SHALL NOT legislate why the Program exists (reserved to CEP-000) or how the Program executes (reserved to CEP-001 and later constitutions).

P.3 This instrument SHALL bind every governance act of the Program until amended or frozen under its own rules.

---

## ARTICLE 1 — GOVERNANCE AUTHORITY

1.1 Governance Authority SHALL vest in Program Authority as defined by CEP-000 §5 (Tier 2).

1.2 Governance Authority SHALL be the sole authority empowered to assign jurisdictions, register owners, issue governed determinations, and resolve governance disputes and conflicts.

1.3 Governance Authority SHALL NOT extend to constitutional content, to execution, or to any matter reserved to another tier or a later constitution.

1.4 Governance Authority SHALL NEVER self-elevate, and SHALL NEVER create a governance power not established by this instrument.

---

## ARTICLE 2 — GOVERNANCE HIERARCHY

2.1 The governance hierarchy SHALL be strictly ordered and acyclic.

2.2 The governance hierarchy SHALL be, in descending precedence: Constitutional Authority; Governance Authority; delegated Governance Stewards; Execution Authority acting within a governed determination.

2.3 A lower level of the hierarchy SHALL NOT govern, overrule, or bind a higher level.

2.4 No cycle SHALL exist in the governance hierarchy; a governance relationship that would form a cycle IS PROHIBITED.

2.5 Every governance role SHALL occupy exactly one position in the hierarchy.

---

## ARTICLE 3 — CONSTITUTIONAL AUTHORITY MODEL

3.1 Constitutional Authority, as defined by CEP-000 §5.5 Tier 1, SHALL be recognized within governance as supreme on matters of constitutional content and finality.

3.2 Governance SHALL exercise no power over constitutional content and SHALL treat Constitutional Authority as superior on all such matters.

3.3 Where final constitutional finality requires an authority residing outside the corpus, governance SHALL recognize that authority as superior for finality and SHALL treat governance rulings dependent on it as PROVISIONAL per CEP-001 Article XIII.

3.4 Governance SHALL NOT assert, simulate, or substitute for Constitutional Authority.

---

## ARTICLE 4 — GOVERNANCE JURISDICTIONS

4.1 A jurisdiction SHALL be a bounded area of governance responsibility assigned to exactly one authority or steward.

4.2 Every jurisdiction SHALL be registered in the Governance Registry (Article 19) with its boundary, its owner, and its authorizing determination.

4.3 Jurisdictions SHALL NOT overlap. Two jurisdictions asserting the same responsibility SHALL be resolved under Article 23.

4.4 An act performed outside the actor's jurisdiction IS PROHIBITED and SHALL be void.

4.5 A jurisdiction without a registered owner SHALL be inactive, and no act SHALL be performed under it until an owner is registered.

---

## ARTICLE 5 — GOVERNANCE DOMAINS

5.1 The domains subject to governance SHALL be: canonical ownership; jurisdiction assignment; scope determination; finding disposition; deferral disposition; the Governance Registry; stewardship; dispute and conflict resolution; and governance change control.

5.2 Each domain SHALL fall within exactly one jurisdiction.

5.3 Governance SHALL NOT extend a domain to encompass execution, validation, certification method, implementation, discovery, or engineering method.

5.4 A matter that does not fall within a registered domain SHALL be recorded as a deferral and SHALL NOT be governed until a domain and jurisdiction are established.

---

## ARTICLE 6 — GOVERNANCE RESPONSIBILITIES

6.1 Governance SHALL be responsible for maintaining exactly one canonical owner and one canonical definition per concern, per CEP-000 §21 and CEP-001 LAW-4.

6.2 Governance SHALL be responsible for maintaining the Governance Registry as accurate, complete, and reconciled against repository truth.

6.3 Governance SHALL be responsible for disposing of every finding and every deferral routed to it.

6.4 Governance SHALL be responsible for resolving every dispute and conflict deterministically and for recording each resolution.

6.5 Governance SHALL be responsible for ensuring no governance control exists that closes no named risk.

---

## ARTICLE 7 — GOVERNANCE POWERS

7.1 Governance MAY assign, re-assign, activate, and deactivate jurisdictions.

7.2 Governance MAY register, transfer, and retire canonical owners and definitions.

7.3 Governance MAY issue governed determinations, and MAY halt the Program upon a governance violation.

7.4 Governance MAY delegate a power under Article 13, and MAY revoke any delegation.

7.5 A power not granted by this Article DOES NOT exist for governance.

---

## ARTICLE 8 — GOVERNANCE RESTRICTIONS

8.1 Governance SHALL NOT author, alter, or decide constitutional content.

8.2 Governance SHALL NOT legislate or perform execution, validation, certification method, implementation, discovery, or engineering method.

8.3 Governance SHALL NOT create overlapping jurisdictions, duplicate ownership, or conflicting terminology.

8.4 Governance SHALL NOT act outside a registered jurisdiction and domain.

8.5 Governance SHALL NOT waive, defer, or override enforcement.

8.6 Governance SHALL NOT delete registry, determination, dispute, or audit lineage.

---

## ARTICLE 9 — GOVERNANCE DUTIES

9.1 Governance MUST record every governance act as a governed determination that is addressable, reproducible, and auditable.

9.2 Governance MUST reconcile the Governance Registry against repository truth at every boot and MUST correct divergence before any governance act.

9.3 Governance MUST resolve ownership contests by the deterministic rule of Article 14 and MUST record the outcome.

9.4 Governance MUST route matters beyond its scope to the Deferral Register and MUST NOT act upon them.

9.5 Governance MUST prove, not assert, that no jurisdiction overlaps and no ownership is duplicated at every governance gate.

---

## ARTICLE 10 — DECISION AUTHORITY MODEL

10.1 Decision authority SHALL be partitioned per CEP-000 §20 without overlap.

10.2 A decision SHALL be made at the lowest hierarchy level competent for its domain, and SHALL rise only by escalation (Article 12).

10.3 Every decision SHALL resolve to exactly one recorded outcome.

10.4 A decision made outside the decider's authority SHALL be void and SHALL be re-decided by the competent authority.

10.5 No decision SHALL be left unresolved once raised; an unresolved decision SHALL place the Program in HALTED until decided or deferred.

---

## ARTICLE 11 — DECISION HIERARCHY

11.1 Decisions SHALL be classified as routine, governance, constitutional, or finality.

11.2 Routine decisions SHALL be decided by Execution Authority within a governed determination; governance decisions by Governance Authority; constitutional decisions by Constitutional Authority; finality decisions by Ratification Authority per CEP-001 Article XIII.

11.3 A decision of a higher class SHALL NOT be decided by a lower authority.

11.4 A decision spanning multiple classes SHALL be decided at the highest class it touches.

---

## ARTICLE 12 — ESCALATION MODEL

12.1 An unresolved or contested decision SHALL escalate to the next higher competent authority in the governance hierarchy.

12.2 Escalation SHALL be recorded with its trigger, its origin level, and its destination level.

12.3 Escalation SHALL terminate at the highest competent authority; where that authority is out-of-corpus, the matter SHALL be recorded PROVISIONAL and SHALL be non-blocking to engineering per CEP-001 Article XIII.

12.4 Escalation SHALL NOT skip a level, and SHALL NOT descend.

12.5 An escalation that cannot terminate within the corpus SHALL be recorded as an open matter and SHALL be surfaced at every readiness assessment.

---

## ARTICLE 13 — DELEGATION MODEL

13.1 Delegation SHALL flow only downward in the governance hierarchy.

13.2 A delegated power SHALL be bounded, recorded, and revocable, and SHALL NOT exceed the delegator's own power.

13.3 Ultimate accountability SHALL NOT be delegated; a delegator SHALL remain accountable for delegated acts.

13.4 A delegate SHALL NOT sub-delegate beyond the bounds recorded by the delegator.

13.5 Revocation of a delegation SHALL be recorded and SHALL take effect upon record.

---

## ARTICLE 14 — CANONICAL OWNERSHIP GOVERNANCE

14.1 Every concern SHALL have exactly one canonical owner registered in the Governance Registry.

14.2 Ownership SHALL be assigned by governed determination and SHALL NEVER be implied.

14.3 An ownership contest SHALL be resolved by the earliest ratified definition prevailing; the contest SHALL be logged and the superseded claim recorded, never silently discarded (per CEP-000 §21, CEP-001 Article X).

14.4 Ownership transfer SHALL occur only by governed determination, SHALL be recorded, and SHALL preserve the prior owner in lineage.

14.5 A concern without a registered owner SHALL NOT progress.

---

## ARTICLE 15 — STEWARDSHIP MODEL

15.1 Stewardship SHALL be the delegated custody of a jurisdiction, register, ledger, or determination record.

15.2 A steward SHALL preserve the integrity, completeness, and reconciliation of the entrusted record and SHALL NOT alter it beyond the steward's recorded jurisdiction.

15.3 Stewardship SHALL be assignable and revocable only by Governance Authority.

15.4 A steward SHALL NOT confer authority, decide constitutional content, or exceed delegated bounds.

15.5 Loss or ambiguity of stewardship SHALL place the affected record under Governance Authority until stewardship is re-established.

---

## ARTICLE 16 — CONSTITUTIONAL REVIEW MODEL

16.1 Governance instruments SHALL be subject to review for internal consistency, authority consistency, cross-reference consistency, and terminology consistency.

16.2 Review SHALL be triggered at every governance gate and upon any amendment of a governance instrument.

16.3 Review SHALL be evidentiary; a review conclusion SHALL be provable by reference and SHALL NOT be asserted.

16.4 A review that discovers an inconsistency SHALL emit a finding and SHALL place the affected matter in HALTED until resolved or superseded.

16.5 Review SHALL introduce no new governance content; it SHALL only verify.

---

## ARTICLE 17 — GOVERNANCE COMPLIANCE MODEL

17.1 Every governance act IS REQUIRED to comply with this instrument, CEP-001, and CEP-000.

17.2 Governance compliance SHALL be verified at every governance gate and proven by evidence and traceability, never asserted.

17.3 A governance non-compliance SHALL place the Program in HALTED until remediated or superseded.

17.4 No governance matter SHALL be declared complete while an unresolved governance compliance failure exists against it.

---

## ARTICLE 18 — GOVERNANCE AUDIT MODEL

18.1 Every governed determination, jurisdiction change, ownership change, delegation, escalation, dispute, conflict resolution, and review SHALL be recorded in an append-only, content-addressed record, per CEP-001 Article XVII.

18.2 Governance audit records SHALL be sufficient to reconstruct the governance state and history deterministically.

18.3 Governance audit records SHALL be operational memory and SHALL NEVER enter the constitutional corpus.

18.4 A governance act that cannot be audited SHALL be treated as if it did not lawfully occur.

---

## ARTICLE 19 — GOVERNANCE REGISTRY MODEL

19.1 The Governance Registry SHALL be the single canonical record of jurisdictions, canonical owners, canonical definitions, delegations, and stewardships.

19.2 The Governance Registry SHALL enforce uniqueness: no duplicate owner, no overlapping jurisdiction, and no conflicting terminology SHALL be admitted.

19.3 The Governance Registry SHALL be append-only in lineage, content-addressed, and reconciled against repository truth at every boot.

19.4 An entry absent from the Registry SHALL be deemed ungoverned; an act under an ungoverned entry IS PROHIBITED.

19.5 The Governance Registry SHALL be operational memory and SHALL hold no authority over constitutional content.

---

## ARTICLE 20 — GOVERNANCE CHANGE CONTROL

20.1 Every change to a governance instrument, jurisdiction, ownership, delegation, or stewardship SHALL be classified as additive, corrective, or superseding, per CEP-000 §24.

20.2 Additive governance changes SHALL NOT alter existing frozen governance content.

20.3 Corrective governance changes to non-frozen governance content SHALL be re-reviewed before progression.

20.4 Superseding governance changes to frozen governance content SHALL occur only through the Governance Amendment Process (Article 21) and SHALL be append-only.

20.5 Every governance change SHALL be recorded with its classification, justification, and evidence.

---

## ARTICLE 21 — GOVERNANCE AMENDMENT PROCESS

21.1 Amendment of a governance instrument SHALL follow the general Amendment Model of CEP-001 Article XV, specialized for governance content.

21.2 Governance amendment SHALL proceed as proposal, evidence, impact analysis, governance review, re-ratification, and supersession, and SHALL be append-only.

21.3 Governance amendment SHALL be raised only upon a logged inconsistency finding, a dispute ruling, or a conflict resolution requiring change.

21.4 A superseded governance instrument SHALL be retained for lineage and marked superseded; prior ratification SHALL NOT carry over.

21.5 This instrument MAY be amended only under this Article and only while it is not frozen.

---

## ARTICLE 22 — GOVERNANCE DISPUTE RESOLUTION

22.1 A dispute SHALL be a contested matter between parties or claims within governance.

22.2 A dispute SHALL be raised as a recorded finding and SHALL be resolved by the lowest competent authority, escalating under Article 12 where unresolved.

22.3 Dispute resolution SHALL be deterministic and evidence-based and SHALL resolve to exactly one recorded outcome.

22.4 A dispute SHALL NOT be closed by silence, by lapse, or by unilateral action of a party to it.

22.5 An unresolved dispute SHALL place the affected matter in HALTED until resolved or deferred.

---

## ARTICLE 23 — GOVERNANCE CONFLICT RESOLUTION

23.1 A conflict SHALL be an inconsistency between governance instruments, jurisdictions, or rules.

23.2 Conflicts SHALL be resolved by precedence: CEP-000 over CEP-001 over this instrument over subordinate governance instruments; and, among instruments of equal rank, the earliest ratified definition prevailing.

23.3 Overlapping jurisdictions SHALL be resolved by re-assignment such that exactly one owner remains; the superseded assignment SHALL be recorded.

23.4 Conflict resolution SHALL be deterministic and SHALL resolve to exactly one recorded outcome.

23.5 A conflict that cannot be resolved by precedence SHALL escalate under Article 12.

---

## ARTICLE 24 — GOVERNANCE ENFORCEMENT

24.1 Enforcement of governance rulings SHALL occur at governance gates through the general Enforcement Model of CEP-001 Article XXIII.

24.2 A governance violation SHALL place the Program in HALTED, emit a finding, and require remediation or supersession before ADVANCING resumes.

24.3 A void act (an act outside jurisdiction, authority, or domain) SHALL have no effect and SHALL be recorded as void.

24.4 Governance enforcement SHALL NEVER be waived, deferred, or overridden by any authority tier.

24.5 Governance enforcement records SHALL be append-only, content-addressed, and auditable.

---

## ARTICLE 25 — GOVERNANCE COMPLETION RULES

25.1 Governance for a stage SHALL be complete when every jurisdiction, owner, and definition it requires is registered; every finding and deferral routed to governance is disposed; and every dispute and conflict against it is resolved or deferred.

25.2 Governance completion SHALL be distinct from stage completion and SHALL NOT authorize a successor stage.

25.3 No governance completion SHALL be declared while an overlapping jurisdiction, duplicated owner, unresolved dispute, unresolved conflict, or unrouted finding exists.

25.4 Every declaration of governance completion SHALL be provable by evidence and traceability.

---

## ARTICLE 26 — SUPREMACY, DERIVATION, AND VERSIONING

26.1 This instrument IS supreme over all governance operation and over every subordinate governance instrument.

26.2 This instrument derives from CEP-000 and CEP-001 and IS subordinate to both; conflicts resolve in favor of the higher instrument per Article 23.2.

26.3 This instrument carries an explicit version; the present version IS 1.0, and any change SHALL occur only through Article 21 and SHALL increment the version.

26.4 A superseded version SHALL be retained for lineage; a frozen version SHALL be immutable and content-addressed.

26.5 Every subordinate governance instrument SHALL cite the version of this instrument under which it was produced.
