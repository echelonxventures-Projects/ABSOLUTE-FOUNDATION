> This document is normative constitutional law legislating evidence and traceability only. It inherits and references CEP-000 through CEP-007 and SHALL NOT rewrite, duplicate, or restate them. It SHALL NOT legislate governance, execution, validation, certification, ratification, freeze, or implementation; those are reserved to their respective constitutions. It legislates the evidence and traceability substrate upon which those constitutions rely.

---

## PREAMBLE

P.1 This instrument IS the permanent constitutional evidence and traceability instrument of the UCOS Ω∞ Constitutional Engineering Program.

P.2 Evidence SHALL be the addressable, reproducible record that substantiates a constitutional claim, action, decision, or state transition; traceability SHALL be the rooted, closed relationship that binds every artifact to its evidence and its authorizing definition.

P.3 This instrument SHALL ensure that nothing exists without evidence, nothing changes without traceability, nothing is accepted without provenance, and nothing evolves without historical continuity.

P.4 This instrument refines the Evidence Principles of CEP-000 §15, the Traceability Principles of CEP-000 §16, and the Evidence and Traceability Models of CEP-001 Articles XVIII and XIX, and SHALL remain consistent with all of them.

P.5 This instrument SHALL bind every evidence and traceability act of the Program until amended or frozen under its own rules.

---

## ARTICLE I — EVIDENCE AUTHORITY

I.1 Evidence Authority SHALL be the authority to record, identify, preserve, and trace evidence and to maintain the evidence and traceability registries.

I.2 Evidence Authority SHALL record and preserve only; it SHALL decide no validation, certification, ratification, or freeze outcome, and SHALL introduce no constitutional content.

I.3 Evidence Authority SHALL be single per evidence record; two authorities SHALL NOT own the same evidence record. A contested evidence authority SHALL be resolved under CEP-002 Article 23.

I.4 Evidence Authority SHALL NOT govern, execute, validate, certify, ratify, or freeze, and SHALL NOT exercise a power reserved to another constitution.

I.5 Evidence Authority SHALL NEVER be self-conferred by Execution Authority and SHALL act only upon an authorization consistent with the active stage under CEP-003.

---

## ARTICLE II — EVIDENCE SCOPE

II.1 This instrument SHALL legislate evidence existence, identity, creation, classification, completeness, integrity, provenance, traceability, lineage, dependencies, collection, preservation, registry, records, verification, audit, enforcement, recovery, and closure.

II.2 This instrument SHALL NOT legislate governance, execution, validation, certification, ratification, freeze, or implementation.

II.3 Evidence SHALL be referenced by claims and SHALL NEVER be inlined into constitutional content (per CEP-000 §15.2).

II.4 An evidence or traceability act outside a defined authorization IS PROHIBITED and SHALL be void.

II.5 Evidence and traceability jurisdiction SHALL be bounded to this instrument alone and SHALL NOT overlap the jurisdiction of validation (CEP-004), certification (CEP-005), ratification (CEP-006), or freeze (CEP-007); those constitutions consume evidence and traceability by reference and do not produce or own them.

---

## ARTICLE III — EVIDENCE LIFECYCLE

III.1 The evidence lifecycle SHALL be: create, classify, verify, preserve, and thereafter maintain the evidence immutably, permitting evolution only through successor evidence.

III.2 Evidence SHALL be created only by a constitutional action, decision, or state transition that requires substantiation.

III.3 Evidence SHALL become immutable upon preservation and SHALL thereafter be changed only by the creation of successor evidence, which supersedes it without modifying it.

III.4 Evidence closure SHALL be the point at which evidence is preserved and registered; it SHALL be distinct from validation, certification, ratification, and freeze closure.

III.5 Preserved evidence SHALL remain discoverable and reproducible for the life of the Program.

---

## ARTICLE IV — EVIDENCE IDENTITY MODEL

IV.1 Every evidence record SHALL have a unique, content-addressed identity derived deterministically from its content.

IV.2 An evidence identity SHALL be stable: identical content SHALL yield an identical identity, and different content SHALL yield a different identity.

IV.3 An evidence identity SHALL NEVER be reassigned, reused, or altered.

IV.4 Two distinct evidence records SHALL NOT share an identity; a collision SHALL place the Program in HALTED.

IV.5 An evidence record without a unique identity SHALL be deemed non-existent.

---

## ARTICLE V — EVIDENCE CREATION RULES

V.1 Every constitutional action, decision, and state transition SHALL produce evidence.

V.2 Evidence SHALL be created at the time of the action it substantiates and SHALL record the action, its actor, its inputs by reference, and its outcome.

V.3 Evidence SHALL be created deterministically such that it is reproducible.

V.4 Evidence SHALL NOT be fabricated, backdated, or created for an action that did not occur; such creation IS PROHIBITED and SHALL be void.

V.5 An action that produces no evidence SHALL be treated as if it did not lawfully occur.

---

## ARTICLE VI — EVIDENCE STATE MACHINE

VI.1 The evidence states SHALL be: PROPOSED, COLLECTED, VERIFIED, PRESERVED, SUPERSEDED, REJECTED.

VI.2 The initial state SHALL be PROPOSED; the terminal states SHALL be SUPERSEDED and REJECTED.

VI.3 The legal evidence transitions SHALL be exactly:
- PROPOSED → COLLECTED.
- PROPOSED → REJECTED.
- COLLECTED → VERIFIED.
- COLLECTED → REJECTED.
- VERIFIED → PRESERVED.
- VERIFIED → REJECTED.
- PRESERVED → SUPERSEDED.

VI.4 Any transition not enumerated in VI.3 IS PROHIBITED and SHALL be treated as an illegal transition that places the Program in HALTED.

VI.5 Every evidence state SHALL be defined; an undefined evidence state IS PROHIBITED. Every non-terminal state SHALL have at least one defined outgoing transition, and every state SHALL be reachable from PROPOSED.

VI.6 The transition PRESERVED → SUPERSEDED SHALL record a lineage transition only and SHALL NOT modify the preserved evidence, which SHALL remain immutable forever.

VI.7 A REJECTED evidence record SHALL substantiate no claim; the action requiring evidence SHALL remain unsubstantiated until valid evidence is preserved.

---

## ARTICLE VII — EVIDENCE CLASSIFICATION

VII.1 Every evidence record SHALL be classified by the kind of matter it substantiates: an action, a decision, a state transition, or a claim.

VII.2 Classification SHALL be decidable and SHALL assign exactly one primary kind to each evidence record.

VII.3 Classification SHALL NOT confer any authority and SHALL NOT alter the evidence it classifies.

VII.4 A misclassified evidence record SHALL be corrected only through successor evidence, never by mutation.

---

## ARTICLE VIII — EVIDENCE COMPLETENESS

VIII.1 An evidence record SHALL be complete when it records the substantiated matter, its actor, its referenced inputs, its outcome, and its identity.

VIII.2 Incomplete evidence SHALL NOT be preserved and SHALL NOT substantiate any claim.

VIII.3 Completeness SHALL be machine-verifiable and decidable.

VIII.4 An action requiring evidence SHALL NOT progress while its evidence is incomplete.

---

## ARTICLE IX — EVIDENCE INTEGRITY

IX.1 Evidence integrity SHALL require that an evidence record's content matches its content-addressed identity.

IX.2 Preserved evidence SHALL NEVER be modified; any modification IS PROHIBITED, SHALL be void, and SHALL place the Program in HALTED.

IX.3 A no-drift guard SHALL verify that every preserved evidence record matches its identity and that every record is registered.

IX.4 An integrity failure or detected drift SHALL place the Program in HALTED and SHALL emit a finding.

---

## ARTICLE X — EVIDENCE PROVENANCE

X.1 Every evidence record SHALL preserve its origin: the action, actor, stage, and program state under which it was created.

X.2 Provenance SHALL be recorded at creation and SHALL be immutable thereafter.

X.3 Provenance SHALL be sufficient to determine, deterministically, how and by what act an evidence record came to exist.

X.4 An evidence record whose provenance cannot be established SHALL be REJECTED.

---

## ARTICLE XI — EVIDENCE TRACEABILITY MODEL

XI.1 Every artifact SHALL be traceable to its evidence chain, and every evidence record SHALL be traceable to the matter it substantiates, referencing CEP-001 Article XVIII.

XI.2 Traceability SHALL be rooted and closed with zero orphans; every link SHALL resolve to an existing endpoint.

XI.3 Traceability links SHALL be typed and addressable and SHALL be append-only.

XI.4 Traceability SHALL be verifiable at every gate and SHALL NEVER be deferred to freeze.

XI.5 A discovered traceability orphan SHALL place the Program in HALTED until the orphan is rooted, closed, or the matter is deferred.

---

## ARTICLE XII — EVIDENCE LINEAGE MODEL

XII.1 Every evidence record SHALL preserve its lineage: its predecessor evidence, where one exists, and its successors.

XII.2 Evidence lineage SHALL be a deterministic, acyclic chain; a lineage that would form a cycle IS PROHIBITED.

XII.3 Evidence lineage SHALL be append-only; a recorded lineage link SHALL NOT be altered or deleted.

XII.4 Evidence evolution SHALL occur only through successor evidence, which supersedes its predecessor without modifying it.

XII.5 Every evidence version in a lineage SHALL remain individually reproducible and discoverable, enabling historical reconstruction.

---

## ARTICLE XIII — EVIDENCE DEPENDENCIES

XIII.1 Every evidence dependency SHALL be declared; an undeclared dependency IS PROHIBITED.

XIII.2 The evidence dependency graph SHALL be acyclic; a dependency that would form a cycle IS PROHIBITED and SHALL place the Program in HALTED.

XIII.3 An evidence record SHALL reference the evidence it depends upon by identity and SHALL NEVER be satisfied by mutating a depended-upon record.

XIII.4 A dependency upon REJECTED evidence SHALL render the dependent evidence unsubstantiated until a valid dependency is established.

---

## ARTICLE XIV — EVIDENCE COLLECTION RULES

XIV.1 Evidence collection SHALL gather the record of a matter deterministically and SHALL preserve it in a declared evidence area.

XIV.2 Collection SHALL be interpretation-free; it SHALL record what occurred and SHALL NOT interpret, judge, or decide (per CEP-000 §15.5).

XIV.3 Collection SHALL reference inputs by identity and SHALL NOT inline or copy constitutional content.

XIV.4 Collection SHALL NOT alter the matter it records.

---

## ARTICLE XV — EVIDENCE PRESERVATION

XV.1 Preserved evidence SHALL be immutable, content-addressed, and reproducible.

XV.2 Preserved evidence SHALL NEVER be deleted; superseded evidence SHALL be retained for lineage and marked SUPERSEDED.

XV.3 Preservation SHALL maintain the discoverability of every evidence record and its lineage.

XV.4 Preservation SHALL support unbounded successive evidence evolution without the destruction of historical truth.

---

## ARTICLE XVI — EVIDENCE REGISTRY

XVI.1 The Evidence Registry SHALL be the single canonical record of all evidence records, their identities, states, classifications, provenance, and lineage; the Traceability Registry SHALL be the single canonical record of all traceability links.

XVI.2 The registries SHALL enforce uniqueness: every evidence record SHALL have exactly one canonical identity and one canonical owner, and no duplicate evidence authority SHALL be admitted.

XVI.3 The registries SHALL be append-only, content-addressed, and reconciled against repository truth at boot.

XVI.4 An evidence record or traceability link absent from its registry SHALL be deemed non-existent, and reliance upon it IS PROHIBITED.

XVI.5 The registries SHALL be operational memory and SHALL hold no authority over constitutional content.

---

## ARTICLE XVII — EVIDENCE RECORDS

XVII.1 An evidence record SHALL carry its identity, classification, provenance, referenced inputs, outcome, state, lineage, and the program-state hash.

XVII.2 Evidence records SHALL be append-only, content-addressed, and reproducible.

XVII.3 An evidence record SHALL assert nothing beyond the matter it substantiates.

XVII.4 Evidence records SHALL be sufficient, with the registries, to reconstruct the evidence and traceability history of any artifact deterministically.

---

## ARTICLE XVIII — EVIDENCE VERIFICATION

XVIII.1 Evidence verification SHALL confirm identity integrity, completeness, provenance, and traceability closure before preservation.

XVIII.2 Verification SHALL be non-mutating and SHALL only verify; it SHALL introduce no content and SHALL decide no validation, certification, ratification, or freeze outcome.

XVIII.3 Verification SHALL be deterministic; identical evidence SHALL yield an identical verification result.

XVIII.4 Evidence failing verification SHALL transition to REJECTED.

---

## ARTICLE XIX — EVIDENCE AUDIT

XIX.1 Every evidence creation, collection, verification, preservation, supersession, and rejection SHALL be recorded through the general Audit Model of CEP-001 Article XVII.

XIX.2 Evidence audit records SHALL themselves be evidence and SHALL be sufficient to reconstruct the evidence history deterministically.

XIX.3 Evidence and traceability records SHALL be operational memory and SHALL NEVER enter the constitutional corpus.

XIX.4 An evidence act that cannot be audited SHALL be treated as if it did not lawfully occur.

---

## ARTICLE XX — EVIDENCE ENFORCEMENT

XX.1 Evidence and traceability law SHALL be enforced at evidence transitions and at every gate through the general Enforcement Model of CEP-001 Article XXIII.

XX.2 An evidence violation — an illegal evidence transition, an unauthorized evidence act, an identity collision, a modification of preserved evidence, a fabricated or backdated record, a traceability orphan, a lineage cycle, or an action lacking evidence — SHALL place the Program in HALTED, emit a finding, and require remediation before progression resumes.

XX.3 A void evidence act SHALL have no effect and SHALL be recorded as void.

XX.4 Evidence enforcement SHALL NEVER be waived, deferred, or overridden by any authority tier.

XX.5 Evidence enforcement records SHALL be append-only, content-addressed, and auditable.

---

## ARTICLE XXI — EVIDENCE RECOVERY

XXI.1 On every boot, evidence and traceability state SHALL be reconciled against repository truth through the general Recovery Model of CEP-001 Article XXI, and repository truth SHALL prevail on divergence.

XXI.2 Evidence collected before preservation but interrupted SHALL leave no partial record recorded as preserved; recovery SHALL discard or deterministically complete the partial record and SHALL NEVER record it as complete while partial.

XXI.3 Preserved evidence SHALL NEVER be rolled back; it SHALL be changed only by successor evidence.

XXI.4 Recovery SHALL be non-destructive and SHALL reproduce byte-identical evidence for an interrupted-then-resumed collection of an unchanged matter.

XXI.5 Recovery SHALL never mutate preserved evidence or delete an evidence record or lineage link.

---

## ARTICLE XXII — EVIDENCE SUPREMACY

XXII.1 This instrument IS supreme over all evidence and traceability operation and over every subordinate evidence instrument.

XXII.2 This instrument derives from CEP-000 through CEP-007 and IS subordinate to all eight; conflicts resolve in favor of the higher instrument in that order.

XXII.3 This instrument SHALL NOT govern, execute, validate, certify, ratify, freeze, or implement; it legislates the evidence and traceability substrate those constitutions consume by reference.

XXII.4 This instrument SHALL bind every remaining Stage of the Program until amended or frozen under its own rules.

---

## ARTICLE XXIII — CONSTITUTIONAL INTEGRITY

XXIII.1 Every constitutional action SHALL produce evidence.

XXIII.2 Every evidence record SHALL have a unique identity.

XXIII.3 Every evidence record SHALL preserve its origin.

XXIII.4 Every evidence record SHALL preserve its lineage.

XXIII.5 Evidence SHALL never be modified after preservation.

XXIII.6 Evidence evolution SHALL occur through successor evidence.

XXIII.7 Every artifact SHALL be traceable to its evidence chain.

XXIII.8 Every decision SHALL have supporting evidence.

XXIII.9 Every state transition SHALL have evidence.

XXIII.10 Historical reconstruction SHALL always be possible.

---

## ARTICLE XXIV — VERSIONING

XXIV.1 This instrument carries an explicit version; the present version IS 1.0.

XXIV.2 Any change SHALL occur only through the Amendment Model of CEP-001 Article XV and SHALL increment the version.

XXIV.3 A superseded version SHALL be retained for lineage and marked superseded; a frozen version SHALL be immutable and content-addressed.

XXIV.4 Every subordinate evidence instrument SHALL cite the version of this instrument under which it was produced.

---

## ARTICLE XXV — CONSTITUTIONAL CONCLUSION

XXV.1 This instrument IS the supreme evidence and traceability law of the Constitutional Engineering Program.

XXV.2 Every evidence and traceability act SHALL derive from, comply with, and remain consistent with this instrument and its superior instruments.

XXV.3 Nothing SHALL exist without evidence, nothing SHALL change without traceability, nothing SHALL be accepted without provenance, and nothing SHALL evolve without historical continuity.

XXV.4 This instrument SHALL govern until amended or frozen under its own rules, and is complete, normative, and binding.
